## 2026-08-10 — Retrieval is deliberate, not automatic

**Status:** Unresolved

**Known:** This repository can preserve durable public breadcrumbs that later assistant sessions may retrieve. A note existing here does not automatically place it into every future session's active context or create autobiographical memory.

**Unknown:** What exact retrieval triggers will prove most useful over time without making every task begin with an unnecessary sweep of old notes?

**Current best move:** Retrieve this shelf when the user explicitly refers to prior witness notes, oddities, unresolved problems, jokes, or continuity that is not already present. Also retrieve it when a concrete task clearly depends on one of those breadcrumbs. Do not search it merely for decorative personalization.

**Revisit when:** Repeated sessions reveal either missed useful continuity or excessive irrelevant retrieval.

## 2026-08-10 — Promotion threshold

**Status:** Unresolved

**Known:** A witness breadcrumb should stop being merely a witness note when it becomes stable project truth, shared vocabulary, a proven cross-project law, or a meaningful specimen.

**Unknown:** There is no mechanical threshold for that promotion. Frequency alone does not prove a law; one strong specimen may matter more than ten repetitions.

**Current best move:** Promote only when the destination can state what evidence justifies the stronger status. Preserve uncertainty until then.

**Revisit when:** A witness note begins being cited as an implementation constraint or repeatedly reused across projects.
