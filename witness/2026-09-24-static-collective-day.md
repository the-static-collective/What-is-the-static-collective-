# Static Collective Day — Founding Witness (2026-09-24)

**Status:** Founding declaration, proposed for public preservation.  
**Observance date:** September 24, annually.  
**First declared:** September 24, 2026.  
**First anniversary:** September 24, 2027.  
**Motto:** **We Play Where We Arrive.**

## The invitation

Make something. Share something. Or leave a door open for someone else to enter.

A song, story, recipe, drawing, experiment, small tool, gathering, or unfinished fragment counts. The person who receives it may continue, alter, decline, or begin something different. No membership, payment, exclusive calendar listing, or prescribed belief is required.

A place at the table is not a reward for arriving. It is an expression of belonging.

## Founding account

**Participant testimony / dated conversation:** On September 24, 2026, a radio mention of Cherries Jubilee Day opened a conversation about making a real dessert into a fictional Lemon Press recipe, then about Daughters Day and the uncertain origins of informal calendar observances. The conversation turned toward the curious fact that an archive of celebrations can also help produce new celebrations. A participant proposed **Static Collective Day**, chose **September 24** for its annual date, and explicitly blessed the proposed celebration.

This is the account of **how this particular observance was chosen**, not a claim that the neighboring calendar holidays share an origin or that their dates carry an externally verified hidden connection. Its founding occasion is documented in the conversation; a historical origin for the other holidays is not established by this page.

## The Lemon Press edition

Within the fictional Lemon Press universe, the publishing house prints a calendar with one date filled in. The calendar invites people to make, share, and discover things; their occasions leave traces that may become new calendar entries. The publishing conceit is fiction; the invitation to participate is real.

**Inaugural calendar entry:**

> SEPTEMBER 24 — STATIC COLLECTIVE DAY  
> We Play Where We Arrive.  
> Make something. Share something. Leave a door open.

### First ritual: set another place

1. Make or find one thing worth passing along, however small or unfinished.
2. Make a way for someone else to enter: an invitation, an accessible copy, an empty chair, an open question, or a note explaining where the work can be resumed.
3. If you wish, leave a dated trace: what happened, who contributed with their consent, and what remains open.

These are invitations, not criteria for belonging. A quiet celebration without a public artifact is still a celebration. Do not publish another person's name, story, image, or contribution without their permission.

## Provenance and limits

- The date and tradition above are **participant-authored**, not a government designation, an external calendar approval, or a claim of exclusivity.
- The personal circumstances behind the conversation are intentionally not reproduced here. The public founding account does not require them.
- This file is a **witness / founding record**, not a decree over other projects or a claim that all future participants must celebrate in the same way.
- Future additions should be dated, attributed with consent, and distinguish what happened from what a fictional publisher says happened.

**The calendar does not authorize the celebration. People celebrating bring it into being.**
