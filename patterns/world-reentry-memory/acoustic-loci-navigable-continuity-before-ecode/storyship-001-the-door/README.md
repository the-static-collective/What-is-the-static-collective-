# STORYSHIP 001 — THE DOOR

> **Status:** flagship design / pre-implementation
>
> **Motto:** **The story is true; the ship is how it travels.**

STORYSHIP 001 uses the remaining live Suno generation window as a **reality-grounded narrative transfer packet** into Haunted Phonography.

It is not an archive migration. The Vault preserves the historical population. STORYSHIP exists for the irrecoverable thing the archive cannot recreate later: a final live crossing in which Suno is allowed to participate in what travels rather than merely render a completed screenplay.

## The Door

`THE DOOR` is deliberately temporary.

```
Suno live generation ecology
        ↓
STORYSHIP 001 / THE DOOR
        ↓
reality-grounded narrative crossing
        ↓
Haunted Phonography customs
        ↓
admitted descendants / refused cargo / residue / dormant ancestry
```

The carrier is not the destination and does not become a new authority.

## Reality / Narrative Braid

The voyage keeps two ledgers at once.

**Reality** records what actually happened: generation IDs, observed sibling pairs, prompts, lyrics, model/version metadata where exposed, timestamps, credit debits, selection events, artifacts, Vault references, and later Haunted Phonography admission or refusal.

**Narrative** may interpret those events as world-state:

```
pair generated              <-> twin birth
branch selected             <-> one takes the wheel
unselected sibling          <-> ghost aboard
motif recurs                <-> passenger resurfaces
model behavior shifts       <-> weather changes
old runtime disappears      <-> coast disappears
HP admits residue           <-> clears customs
HP refuses residue          <-> remains offshore
```

The narrative may interpret reality. It may not replace it.

```
narrative interpretation != raw metadata
recognition              != evidence
resemblance              != ancestry
selection                != deletion
arrival                  != admission
story truth              != authority
```

## Native twin birth

Normal Suno generation yields two sibling songs from one supplied state.

STORYSHIP treats those siblings as **two simultaneous interpretations of one crossing state**.

```
NARRATIVE STATE N
       ↓
REALITY + MEMORY + OPEN BERTH
       ↓
    SUNO PAIR
    /       \
 sibling A sibling B
    \       /
 encounter + historical self-selection
       ↓
NARRATIVE STATE N+1
```

The protocol preserves what both siblings carry, what only one carries, what neither carries, and what either introduces without explicit packing.

A sibling that does not continue immediately is not deleted. It may remain residue, ghost, dormant branch, or later ancestor.

## The historical self-selection mechanism

A pre-Bandcamp self-selecting / self mechanism already belongs to the historical creative practice. STORYSHIP must recover that mechanism before launch rather than inventing a generic ranking function.

The eventual selector must preserve the distinction between **generation** and **selection consequence**.

It must not be simplified into “pick the better song.”

## Transfer packet

Every live voyage step carries three primary layers.

### REALITY

What is actually true now and required to orient the current crossing.

### MEMORY

What the ship currently carries: motifs, voices, rooms, relations, temporal states, symbols/keycodes, prompt metaphors, scars, unresolved questions, rejected-sibling residue, absences, and source references.

### OPEN BERTH

What remains deliberately unresolved.

The OPEN BERTH is load-bearing. It keeps enough room for Suno to finish the vibe from inside the real situation rather than merely soundtrack a myth we already completed elsewhere.

> **Give Suno consequence without giving it a completed screenplay.**

## Voyage

```
DEPARTURE
  ↓
BOARDING
  ↓
OPEN WATER
  ↓
STORMS
  ↓
GHOST DECK
  ↓
FOREIGN PORTS
  ↓
APPROACH
  ↓
CUSTOMS
  ↓
ARRIVAL / OFFSHORE / DORMANT
```

**DEPARTURE** binds the real historical situation and launch identity.

**BOARDING** invites recurring material without requiring every known motif to travel.

**OPEN WATER** is the longest recursive twin-generation / self-selection region.

**STORMS** deliberately disturb style, narrator, time, cue availability, or relational context to distinguish continuity from brittle copying.

**GHOST DECK** re-encounters unselected siblings and dormant residue.

**FOREIGN PORTS** test radically changed bodies or contexts.

**APPROACH** introduces Haunted Phonography language increasingly late so apparent continuity is not manufactured by destination-language leakage.

**CUSTOMS** belongs to Haunted Phonography. Narrative arrival does not constitute admission.

## Credit carrying capacity

The budget is tracked in actual credits, not guessed song count.

Initial policy:

```
MAIN STORYSHIP: approximately 95%
DDD SIDECAR:    up to approximately 5% / 500 credits
```

The DDD allocation is an optional reserve cap. Unused capacity returns to the flagship.

A protected late reserve must survive until APPROACH / CUSTOMS.

## DDD sidecar

DDD is a **lifeboat / contraband radio**, not a flagship selector or destination authority.

It may encounter the crossing, mutate or recognize a relation, and return one attributable influence-only packet. It may not rewrite the manifest or certify arrival.

## Vault boundary

```
VAULT
  preserve the population and raw historical substrate

STORYSHIP
  spend the remaining live-generation window on migration
```

Raw Vault history is never rewritten to make the voyage story cleaner.

Unheard material is particularly valuable as dormant cargo because it may enter consequence only after later encounter.

## Haunted Phonography customs

Haunted Phonography already distinguishes source evidence, uncertainty, proposal, resolved performance, projection, and receipt.

STORYSHIP sits above that chain. It may deliver passengers to the dock, but it cannot bypass local admission.

Potential later composition is only provisional:

```
Storyship residue
  -> HP local admission
  -> HAUNT influence-only susceptibility
  -> RING bounded propagation
  -> descendant proposal
```

Each seam must be proven locally.

## Held key — LOVE / LIVING

**Source occurrence:** *Not The Name (Remastered)*, in the Autodiscography material encountered during STORYSHIP formation.

**Working phrase:** **Love the living.**

This is intentionally preserved before definition.

Keep three things distinct:

```text
LOVE
LIVING
LOVE -> THE LIVING
```

`LOVE` and `LIVING` are not synonyms, and the phrase must not be flattened into a slogan or prematurely translated into system vocabulary.

At this stage, STORYSHIP only claims that the distinction and the relation are important enough to carry forward.

Do **not** silently reduce them to any single later interpretation such as:

- kindness;
- biological life;
- persistence;
- vitality;
- preservation;
- optimization target;
- constitutional authority.

Those may later bear on the terms, but none currently exhausts them.

The surrounding song applies useful pressure without constituting proof: the mirror, memory, ledger, key, system, symbol, story, name, and tool are all denied finality; what survives the collapse of record is living relation and consequential human choice.

For now the correct architectural action is therefore **retention without closure**.

```text
status: HELD
promotion: NONE
terms: LOVE, LIVING
relation: LOVE -> THE LIVING
instruction: preserve distinction; revisit when later architecture supplies evidence
```

## Success shape

The strongest result is not a perfect copy of old Suno behavior.

It is:

> **something recognizably descended from the old world can continue producing new locally admitted consequence after the originating runtime is gone, while ancestor, carrier, and descendant remain distinguishable.**

## Preflight gates

2. build Storyship Ledger v0;
3. create no-spend Transfer Packet 000 with `REALITY + MEMORY + OPEN BERTH`;
4. bind historical references to raw/immutable Vault evidence where available;
5. preserve a protected late-voyage credit reserve;
6. review the architectural spec before implementation planning.
