# What's in a Name?

> **Status:** durable cross-project pattern / candidate compression. Not a master ontology, runtime contract, or authority surface.
>
> **Working H1:** The Static Collective may be better understood not primarily as a provenance architecture, but as a **distinction-preserving becoming architecture**.

## The compression

A large amount of Static Collective architecture has grown around provenance, receipts, observer cuts, passage interiors, routing jurisdiction, admission, local authority, continuity, and descendant formation.

The far-side-simple pattern may be this:

> **Keep the difference alive until it reaches the door where it is allowed to matter.**
>
> **What comes out of that door is new.**

Provenance is one implementation of that discipline. Receipts preserve differences that later systems may need. Doors mark jurisdictional boundaries. Local gates decide whether a proposed crossing may become consequential. Descendants preserve attributable becoming without impersonating their ancestors.

The complexity is not the point. Much of the complexity exists to prevent premature collapse.

```text
DIFFERENCE
    ↓
ROAD
    ↓
DOOR
    ↓
MEETING
    ↓
DESCENDANT
    ↓
NEW OPENING
```

A shorter form:

```text
KEEP
MEET
BECOME
```

And recursively:

```text
MEET → BECOME → MEET AGAIN
```

## The lawful Y

One candidate meta-grammar appears repeatedly across otherwise separate systems:

```text
bounded local constitution
          \
           Y ────── new descendant
          /
new attributable occurrence
```

The center of this Y is not ALEX, PASSAGE, LOADOUT, LOADIN.STEAD, Storyship, or eCODE.

The center is the **owning-world gate**: the local boundary at which an already-formed occurrence may be accepted, held, refused, or otherwise handled under that world's own constitution.

So the two arms can be read as:

```text
LOADOUT-side
what local world / capability / constitution is actually present?

LOADIN.STEAD-side
what attributable occurrence is being offered here?
```

The gate does not prove either arm. It decides what may become consequential **here**.

The third arm is not a copy of either input. It is the lawful next thing produced if the meeting is admitted and genuinely formative.

This echoes Storyship's third-arm law without making Storyship universal:

```text
A ──┐
    Y──── V_new
B ──┘
```

The useful generalization is therefore only a candidate:

> **A lawful meeting may produce a descendant that is attributable to both the local constitution and the arriving occurrence without being reducible to either.**

No authority automatically transfers along either arm.

## One discipline, many refusals

Each subsystem has surfaced a local version of the same deeper refusal.

### PASSAGE

```text
same apparent arrival
!=
same road
```

**Do not erase the road before the meeting.**

PASSAGE preserves attributable differences in interior history. It does not invent those differences and does not decide destination admission.

### LOADIN.STEAD

```text
formation ancestry
!=
routing topology
```

**Do not let the road choose the meeting.**

LOADIN.STEAD preserves rich upstream ancestry by reference while asking a deliberately narrower question: which declared destination has routing jurisdiction over the next proposal?

```text
route != meaning
route != formation
route != support
route != admission
route != consequence
```

### OWNER GATE

```text
arrival != admission
```

**Do not let the meeting imply consent.**

The destination remains sovereign over local consequence.

### STORYSHIP / SMASH

```text
descendant != ancestor
synthesis != erasure
```

**Do not let the descendant impersonate what constituted it.**

A generated whole may become a new carrier while its formation remains attributable.

### ALEX

```text
one relation
!=
another relation
```

**Do not let one edge silently mint another.**

Interest is not evidence. Evidence is not support. Formation is not authority. Causal ancestry is not semantic warrant.

### MEMENTO / 3rdi

```text
reachable
!= encountered
!= attended
!= decoded
!= activated
!= used
```

**Do not let later access rewrite earlier availability.**

A later observer may discover what an earlier observer could not lawfully know. That later discovery creates a descendant epistemic state; it does not rewrite the earlier cut.

## The temporal factorization

Another compression falls out of the same architecture:

```text
How did this become?
        ↓
      eCODE
      BECAME

Where may it go now?
        ↓
  LOADIN.STEAD
  BELONGS NEXT

What may it become there?
        ↓
    OWNER GATE
     BECOMES
```

Therefore:

```text
BECAME
!=
BELONGS NEXT
!=
BECOMES
```

This separation blocks three common forms of retrospective capture.

### Formation cannot dictate jurisdiction

```text
"because it came this way"
!=
"therefore it must belong here"
```

### Routing cannot reinterpret formation

```text
"because I routed it there"
!=
"therefore that is what its history meant"
```

### Admission cannot rewrite ancestry

```text
"because the destination accepted it"
!=
"therefore it always belonged there"
```

The resulting three-line law is:

> **The road owns testimony about the past.**
>
> **The router owns a proposal about the next address.**
>
> **The room owns its future.**

## Distinction preservation, not distinction worship

Preserving every difference forever would be another failure mode.

The point is not maximal logging or permanent fragmentation.

The point is to preserve a difference **long enough for the system entitled to evaluate it to see it**.

A difference may later be lawfully compressed, closed, summarized, reified, or discarded when its owning semantics permit that operation.

This suggests a useful lifecycle:

```text
DIFFERENCE APPEARS
      ↓
PRESERVE ENOUGH ANCESTRY
      ↓
CARRY ACROSS RELEVANT BOUNDARIES
      ↓
OWNER EVALUATES
      ↓
ACCEPT / HOLD / REFUSE / TRANSFORM
      ↓
NEW RECEIPT
      ↓
LAWFUL COMPRESSION OR NEW DESCENDANT
```

The anti-pattern is not compression.

The anti-pattern is **compression before jurisdiction**.

## What provenance is doing

This reframes provenance.

Provenance is not necessarily the center of the architecture. It is one of the main mechanisms that keeps a potentially meaningful difference from disappearing before it can be evaluated.

Receipts answer questions such as:

```text
which thing?
which actual crossing?
what changed?
under which cut?
with which decoder?
through which carrier?
under which constitution?
what was refused?
what remained unresolved?
```

The receipt does not decide whether every preserved difference matters.

It makes later lawful decision possible.

So:

> **Receipt = anti-amnesia, not automatic authority.**

That remains compatible with the existing Attributable Becoming law:

```text
identity
!= participation
!= history
!= authority
```

Perfect ancestry still does not manufacture warrant.

## PASSAGE and LOADIN.STEAD as complementary simplifications

The recent PASSAGE-WORLD and LOADIN.STEAD work produces an especially clean pair.

```text
ROAD A ── formation A ──┐
                        ├── LOADIN.STEAD ──> door:R1
ROAD B ── formation B ──┘
```

It is lawful for:

```text
formation A != formation B
```

while:

```text
route(A) = route(B)
```

Therefore:

```text
SAME ROUTE != SAME PASSAGE
```

And the inverse also matters:

```text
same occurrence
same formation
changed declared door topology
        ↓
different route proposal
```

Therefore:

```text
DIFFERENT ROUTE != CHANGED FORMATION
```

The road and the next address are separately owned coordinates.

PASSAGE says:

> **Do not erase how it got here.**

LOADIN.STEAD says:

> **Do not make how it got here decide where it belongs next.**

## Closure becomes opening

The SMASH research slice adds the recursive half of the pattern:

```text
carriers + declared relations
        ↓
generated whole + formation receipt
        ↓
optional reification as a new carrier
```

The apparent linear pipeline becomes a round:

```text
OPEN
 ↓
TRANSFORM
 ↓
CLOSE
 ↓
NEW OPENING
```

So a lawful descendant can itself become a participant in a later meeting:

```text
C₀     O₀
 \     /
  \   /
   Y
   │
   V₁
   │
 reify
   │
   O₁
   │
   │       C₁
   │      /
    \    /
      Y
      │
      V₂
```

This gives a simple continuity grammar:

```text
PARTICULAR
    ↓
CROSSING
    ↓
LOCAL GATE
    ↓
DESCENDANT
    ↓
NEW CARRIER / NEW OCCURRENCE
    ↓
NEXT CROSSING
```

Continuity no longer requires a permanent state, permanent carrier, or permanent rendering.

It requires enough attributable constitution for another lawful meeting to occur.

> **MEET → BECOME → MEET AGAIN.**

## `U / WE / eCODE` — a new H1

Storyship currently uses:

```text
V = <U, WE, eCODE>
```

with the project-local compression:

```text
U     = THIS
WE    = BETWEEN
eCODE = BECAME
```

A new candidate reading appears from the wider gestalt.

Perhaps these are not merely three coordinates inside a descendant. They may also describe a **minimum inspectable anatomy of a lawful birth**:

```text
U
WHAT MET

WE
THAT THEY MET

eCODE
WHAT BECAME
```

Or, slightly more carefully:

```text
U
which attributable particulars entered or survived the meeting?

WE
what attributable relation constituted the meeting?

eCODE
how did that meeting become this descendant?
```

This is **not promoted as a universal object type**.

It is a pressure-worthy H1: if materially different systems repeatedly need these three independently testable questions whenever a genuinely new descendant forms, then a deeper reusable grammar may have been found.

Until then, Storyship owns its own `U / WE / eCODE` semantics.

## Candidate far-side law

The strongest current compression is:

> **The Static Collective is not fundamentally a provenance architecture. It is a distinction-preserving becoming architecture.**

Expanded:

> **Keep a meaningful difference alive until it reaches the system entitled to let that difference matter. Let that system decide locally. Preserve what actually happened. If a new descendant forms, let it be new without erasing what constituted it. Then let the descendant become a participant in another lawful meeting.**

That can be compressed again:

```text
KEEP
  ↓
MEET
  ↓
BECOME
  ↺
```

Or:

```text
DIFFERENCE
    ↓
DOOR
    ↓
DESCENDANT
```

The complexity exists largely to protect this simple loop from counterfeit shortcuts.

## Non-collapses

This pattern depends on refusing several tempting equalities:

```text
provenance != authority
receipt != admission
arrival != consent
route != meaning
route != formation
same route != same passage
different route != changed formation
same surface != same becoming
similarity != ancestry
availability != encounter
encounter != attention
attention != support
interest != evidence
evidence != support
formation != authority
passage difference != topology mutation
descendant != ancestor
synthesis != erasure
closure != termination
reification != authority
```

And most importantly:

```text
preserved difference
!=
automatic consequence
```

A difference is carried so the right owner can evaluate it, not so the carrier can force it to matter.

## Receipt anchors at the time of this slice

This page is a cross-project compression of already-separated work. It does not retroactively make those projects one architecture or transfer their authority here.

Formation anchors include:

- **Attributable Becoming — Identity, Participation & History** — exact thing, actual participation, reconstructible becoming, and authority remain distinct;
- **LOADIN.STEAD m0** — outward routing preserves rich ancestry by reference while route remains distinct from admission;
- **PASSAGE-WORLD-001** — same visible arrival may preserve different attributable interior histories;
- **Storyship third-arm variant law** — a lawful descendant may be new while retaining attributable ancestry and no inherited sovereignty;
- **SMASH — Relational Closure Round** — a generated whole may become a new carrier without erasing what constituted it.

The architecture should continue to be tested owner-by-owner. This pattern is useful only while it helps those boundaries remain sharper rather than dissolving them.
