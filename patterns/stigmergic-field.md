# Stigmergic Field

## Pattern

> A shared substrate may communicate its condition through attributable, deterministic environmental traces without those traces becoming authority.

## Origin observation

Honey-bee colonies coordinate through more than direct messages. Recruitment, inhibition, local encounter rates, and changes to shared material state let individuals respond to a field that no single participant centrally owns.

The biological observation inspired this software pattern. It does not prove the software law and should not be stretched into one-to-one biomimicry.

## Loop

```text
encounter
    ↓
attributable trace
    ↓
deterministic shared field
    ↓
sovereign attention
    ↓
choice / refusal / silence
    ↓
new encounter
```

The field is an environment participants may sense. It is not a hidden coordinator.

## Invariants

1. **Field is not authority.** A field condition may affect attention without granting permission, truth, ownership, or command.
2. **Trace remains attributable.** Environmental pressure must remain explainable through the encounters that produced it.
3. **Replay is law.** The same admitted evidence under the same declared projection law must reproduce the same field.
4. **Durable decay is causal.** Persistent field semantics must not depend on incidental wall-clock timing when replayable event distance can express the intended fading.
5. **Inhibition is additive, not destructive.** A negative or cooling trace does not erase the positive evidence or history it responds to.
6. **Scope is sensory law.** Activity outside a participant's lawful view must not leak through pressure, gaps, fingerprints, or aggregate side channels.
7. **Local choice remains local.** Aggregated environmental pressure may inform a participant, but it must not silently become a scheduler or assignment service.
8. **The substrate may describe its condition without becoming sovereign.** Shared state can become legible while decision and authority remain elsewhere.

## Vocabulary

**Trace** — an attributable environmental consequence of an encounter that can contribute to a later field projection.

**Field cell** — the current projected condition for one subject and one signal dimension at a particular causal cut.

**Attention** — evidence that a subject has entered active consideration or recruitment.

**Receptivity** — evidence that a subject is being met with usable positive response.

**Saturation** — evidence that a subject is already carrying enough independent activity or processing pressure that additional recruitment may be less useful.

**Inhibition** — attributable pressure against further recruitment or continuation. It cools without deleting.

**Tension** — unresolved negative or contradictory encounter evidence that should remain visible rather than being flattened into simple approval or rejection.

**Return** — renewed positive attention from a participant that has already positively encountered the same subject.

**Field fingerprint** — a deterministic identity for one exact projected field state. Equality means the projection bytes agree under the declared contract; it does not mean the participants share a worldview or that the field is true.

## What this pattern is not

A Stigmergic Field is not:

- quorum-as-truth;
- reputation scoring;
- participant or agent ranking;
- a disguised scheduler;
- an authorization bypass;
- an automatic ontology requirement;
- proof that direct messaging is unnecessary;
- a claim that environmental coordination universally outperforms explicit planning.

## Composition surfaces

**TranchNode** can derive deterministic environmental state from accepted, attributable evidence while keeping the projection non-authoritative.

**Band Runtime / Groove Rooms** can make shared environmental conditions perceptible so sovereign participants can redistribute attention without requiring a central assignment primitive.

**Corpus OS** could expose casework metabolism — neglected, saturated, contested, repeatedly returned-to, or ready-for-attention — without replacing explicit case state or legal authority.

**Founder Node** could let routing proposals notice field pressure while preserving the distinction between pressure and authority.

**Creative systems** can use bounded recruitment and inhibition around branches, candidates, unresolved frontiers, or collaborative possibilities without turning popularity into canon.

## Graduation test

The pattern earns implementation-level confidence only when multiple sovereign participants respond usefully to shared environmental evidence without hidden central assignment, and replay can explain every visible field condition from attributable source encounters.

Passing that test proves a bounded coordination primitive. It does not automatically promote the primitive into shared constitutional ontology.
