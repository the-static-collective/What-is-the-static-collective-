## What this proves

### 1. The packaged bridge repair survived a real performance

Source tests already established that the sandboxed preload could expose the required bridge. This specimen adds the missing appliance-level witness: song selection, Listener preparation, candidate/timeline execution, and full render all survived the packaged runtime.

### 2. The visual composer retained its voice

The repair did not flatten or sterilize the known-good alpha.7 visual behavior. The result remained specific, coherent, surprising, and musically appropriate.

### 3. Zero entropy is a successful regime, not a quality rule

This is another excellent zero-patch / zero-entropy specimen, but prior field evidence includes excellent higher-entropy runs and a boring zero-entropy linear run.

The durable law remains:

> **Low meddling is excellent when the base grammar is expressive. Restraint cannot rescue an under-articulated topology.**

### 4. Appropriate style can emerge without being named

“Anime wind” should not become `final form → anime wind` or a new named preset. Its value is that a human recognized a highly specific appropriate gesture that emerged from lawful interaction among song structure, topology, motion, camera, field dynamics, atmosphere, and timing.

That is evidence for compositional emergence.

> **Do not turn a witnessed surprise into a compulsory recipe.**

## Relationship to v-next

This run restores confidence that the current visual foundation is still a valid base for the next version. The next work should build forward from the repaired appliance rather than reopening the renderer merely because the runtime broke around it.

Keep separate:

* packaged/runtime integrity;
* Listener timing trust and correction UX;
* renderer evolution;
* deeper memory/witness experiments.

A failure in one layer is not evidence that the others should be rewritten.
