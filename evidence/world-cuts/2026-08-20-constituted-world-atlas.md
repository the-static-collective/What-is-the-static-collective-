> **Constitutional finding:** the eCODEsystem is not becoming one platform. It is becoming a federation of local constitutions joined by non-authoritative witness membranes.

This atlas distinguishes five evidence classes:

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **LANDED** | Present on the repository’s default branch at the evidence cut. |
| **CANDIDATE** | Present only in an open PR or non-default branch. Mergeability does not promote it. |
| **PROJECTED** | Described in GitBook, a design, plan, issue, or research note without corresponding landed execution. |
| **WITNESSED** | Bound to a real human or field encounter. This is orthogonal to LANDED: code may be landed but not field-witnessed. |
| **FOG** | Evidence is private, conflicting, incomplete, stale, or insufficient to make the stronger claim. |

### Higher-order architecture: the Constitutional Encounter Loop

The recurring structure is now larger than the “Continuity Triangle”:

~~~mermaid
flowchart TD
    D["Possibility / nearby door"] -->|"boundary metadata + relevance"| S["Threshold scan<br/>Front Room + Founder Node"]
    S -->|"human selection"| I["Intent testimony<br/>TranchNode"]

    E -->|"attributable witness"| R["Continuity + re-entry<br/>TranchNode · Upper Room · Toaster"]
    R -. "new relevance, never automatic" .-> S
~~~

The loop has five planes:

1. **Meaning / conformance:** Project0 asks what a claim means and whether mappings preserve distinctions.
2. **Lineage / transformation:** TranchNode records what crossed, changed, failed to cross, or remained unresolved.
3. **Constitution / local authority:** Corpus OS and Authority Kit keep admission and execution local.
4. **Encounter / consequence:** Full Measure and the appliances make an admitted or refused attempt perceptible and consequential.
5. **Witness / re-entry:** Upper Room, Toaster, Phonograph, jublEchat, National Treasure, and the Human Witness Relay preserve return paths without impersonating original presence or authority.

The architectural invariant is:

> **Possibility may be visible. Testimony may travel. Authority stays local. Consequence is recorded. Re-entry remains attributable.**

- state: active, incubating, historical, superseded, empty, or private-sidecar;

- authority owner;
- last witnessed cut;
- recovery rule.

This should be metadata, not a central command registry.
