# Linear v2 — Elastic Spine

## Field trigger

The **Thirteenth Circle** alpha.7 specimen was visually boring despite having potentially lively ingredients: orbit/pulse motion, swarm dynamics, voxels, drift/orbit camera behavior, and palette change. Its topology was `linear`, compiled as `linear-v1`, with no field-envelope policy. It also used **zero ordinary timeline patches and zero entropy spent**.

That makes it an important negative specimen. Several favorite alpha.7 renders also used zero ordinary patches, so the lesson is not “zero patches equals good.”

> **Low meddling is excellent when the base grammar is expressive. Restraint cannot rescue an under-articulated topology.**

## Core law

> **Linear should be simple in topology, not simple in behavior.**

And:

> **Do not merely draw the waveform on the line. Let the waveform exert force on the line.**

The goal is not a conventional oscilloscope. The goal is an **elastic visual spine** whose identity remains linear while measured musical pressure changes its geometry.

## Elastic Spine

Linear v2 should remain one continuous unbroken spine in its first version. Splitting or fraying can be explored later; keeping one object preserves a strong identity distinct from ring, mirror, fracture, and field families.

The line can deform through a bounded vocabulary:

* **Broad bow** — low-frequency or sustained pressure bends a long region slowly.
* **Ripple** — local waveform energy travels along the spine.
* **Kink / snap** — transient evidence produces a brief angular displacement.
* **Breathing** — quiet passages retain tiny bounded micro-curvature instead of dead stillness.
* **Strain** — rising pressure increases tension/curvature without requiring camera escalation.
* **Spring-back** — release produces damped recovery rather than an instant reset.
* **Residual curvature** — strong events can leave a temporary scar in the line.

Possible control witnesses should remain analysis evidence rather than claims of recovered stems: low-end pressure, transient salience, sustained harmonic/body evidence, and later compression-pressure lanes.

## Why straightness is useful

A straight line is not aesthetically empty if straightness is treated as a **reference state**.

Because the viewer knows what straight is, bending becomes legible tension. A linear topology can therefore carry a phrase such as:

`straight → bend → strain → kink → recoil → imperfect return`

This makes Linear particularly well suited to tension/release. Its expressive power can come from how far the object departs from its remembered resting form and whether it fully returns.

> **What happened to the thing should remain visible in what the thing becomes.**

Residual curvature also creates a direct bridge to Haunted Memory: the spine can remember pressure without needing a separate explanatory effect.

## Relationship to low-entropy composition

The strongest current Toaster specimens often seem to benefit from large-scale compositional law carrying the piece while ordinary patch machinery stays quiet. The Thirteenth Circle counterexample clarifies the condition:

> **Low entropy works when the base grammar itself has enough expressive degrees of freedom.**

So Linear v2 should not solve boredom by forcing more patches. It should make the topology capable of phrasing on its own.

## Guardrails

* Do not turn Linear into a generic waveform visualizer.
* Do not map loudness directly to displacement everywhere.
* Preserve smoothing, bounded geometry, lyric readability, and Witness Window safety.
* Keep camera secondary; the spine should move first.
* Preserve silence and restraint as legitimate states.
* Keep the first version single and unbroken.
* Do not alter the preserved Gold Star renderer as part of this design slice.
