# CANON 001 — The Collection Event

**Status:** Author-approved story canon · 2026-09-23\
**Scope:** Fictional crossover architecture for _The Static Collective Movie_ × _The Pet Sitter_ × _The Room Inside the Room_. This is **not** a historical claim about the real-world Static Collective's founding.\
**Parent seed:** [The Room Inside the Room](./).

> **The Collective collected here.** They were not secretly members of a preassembled organization, recruited by the government, or chosen by the room. They became a collective by helping one another do something consequential.

## Canonical event

Deuce Burbank is streaming from the abandoned animal-research facility to almost nobody. Renji, the architect of the roomspace engine, is the original anonymous viewer. Two or three additional viewers arrive through an ordinary chain of interest and shared links. They carry different encounters with Renji's book: photographed pages, highlighted text, cropped AI conversations, diagrams, mistakes, and provisional interpretations. Their exact identities and existing connections to the real-world Static Collective are **not yet settled as character canon**; the authorized fictional fact is that this encounter is where the collective **collects**.

The stream stalls on an immediate, small, particular problem: a passage keeps collapsing before one rat can reach its companion. Each participant contributes only what they can see or do. One recognizes a geometric relationship; another brings a differently annotated diagram; someone tests a possible connection; Renji holds a provisional passage; Deuce attends to the animals. The rat crosses and waits. Its companion follows. No participant authored every step or compelled either animal.

The chat does not announce a grand institution. Someone asks whether it worked. Someone says they ought to save this. The recording, diagram, chat, and later a song made from the stream's audio become the first shared, attributable residue of their cooperation. What they produced gives them a reason and a place to return to. **The collection event is a first shared act with consequences, not a retrospective declaration that they were always a collective.**

## Three independent accounts of the same event

* _The Static Collective Movie_ can witness the strange humans, their creative activity, their link-sharing, and the act of saving and transforming the result.
* _The Pet Sitter_ retains the substitute's independent comic and emotional story: he is helping animals without knowing who else is trying to make a route available.
* _The Room Inside the Room_ gives access to Renji's side, Deuce's livestream, animal decisions, the engine's changing geography, and the marginal readers' partial screenshots.

No medium owns the whole event; each must be comprehensible without requiring the others. A song, screenshot, or saved stream may travel between them as an artifact with its own provenance rather than making one reality secretly the origin of all the others.

## What is fixed; what remains open

**Fixed:** The Collective forms _through_ the shared rescue/door event; the initiating encounter is an obscure stream with few participants; cooperation is localized, selective, and consequential; the rats act for their own reasons; preserving a witness of the event matters; the formation is discovered rather than predeclared.

**Open:** Participant names and handles; who knows whom beforehand; whether or how real collaborators appear as fictional counterparts; the exact visual grammar of the first passage; the first song's title; the order in which audiences encounter the three accounts; whether a later scene explicitly names the collection event; why the animals need this particular route.

**Non-claims:** This does not imply the real Static Collective literally originated on Deuce's stream, that Renji or H.A.B.I.T.A.T. exist outside the fiction, or that the substitute is obligated to accept unseen intervention. Do not convert the animals' independent actions into puppet behavior merely to make the origin beat neat.

## Scene kernel worth keeping

Deuce: “Okay. Who the hell are you people?”

A viewer: “idk man.”

Another: “we should probably save this.”

They do. Someone makes a song from the recording. Another person hears it, follows the sound, and finds the room.

**The room did not create the Collective. The people who entered made room for one another.**
