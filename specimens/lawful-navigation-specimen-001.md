Record the first executable cross-repository NAV crossing as a human-readable Front Room evidence specimen:

```text
Project0 → Corpus OS
```

> **Evidenced nearby door → deliberate crossing → bounded changed-field witness.**

## Why Corpus OS was a nearby door

The crossing did not begin from semantic similarity, model suggestion, or an automatically expanded graph.

The pinned Authority Kit registry records:

```text
corpus-os CONFORMS_TO project0
```

That typed relation explains why Corpus OS was an evidenced neighboring destination from the Project0 frame.

It does **not** grant permission to cross.

The Front Room threshold law remains in force:

> **Scan the doors freely. Cross them deliberately.**

## What became visible

After entering the Corpus OS frame, one new particularity becomes inspectable: **Latent Reachability**.

Corpus OS can prospectively inspect whether a genuine unspent Action Warrant remains eligible to cross its Session attempt boundary without consuming that warrant, invoking the host, predicting output, or constituting future state.

That capability was not present in the bounded Project0 frame used by this specimen. Its appearance therefore changes the evidence field and produces `particularity:prospective-reachability = new_after`.

This is illumination, not authority transfer.

Latent Reachability does not authorize NAV, execution, or future consequence. It is destination evidence that became visible after the deliberate crossing.

```text

```

NAV therefore reports constitution as `indeterminate`.

The repository commits identify the bounded Project0 and Corpus OS frames used by the specimen. They are not relabeled as constitutions merely because they are precise commits.

### The observer and decoder remained stable

The participant and read-only decoder remain preserved across the crossing. The changed field is therefore not explained by swapping observers or silently changing the comparison mechanism.

## Receipt boundary

```text

```

This specimen does not promote NAV into the canonical receipt vocabulary.

## Residual fog

This one crossing does not establish:

- a universal route graph;
- automatic traversal;
- route ranking or optimization;
- a `TraversalWarrant`;
- a general rule that `CONFORMS_TO` is always the right reason to traverse;
- a governing constitution for either bounded frame;
- authority to execute inside Corpus OS;
- a promise that Latent Reachability will produce any future consequence.

It also does not prove that every useful cross-repository traversal will be materially changed. NAV can lawfully report a bounded crossing with no material difference observed when that is what the declared frames show.

## Verdict

The narrow claim survives contact with executable evidence:

> An evidenced neighboring door can be crossed deliberately, and the crossing can reveal a materially changed field without manufacturing authority or pretending an unresolved constitution is known.

The useful new thing is not that Project0 and Corpus OS are secretly one world.

It is that **the path from relevance to crossing to newly visible particularity can now be witnessed without collapsing relevance, authority, constitution, and evidence into one relation.**
