## What the scan did not do

The scan did not read any destination body, recursively scan child doors, use semantic search to infer missing content, or treat reachability as permission to cross.

It also did not use the existence of a repository path as a substitute for missing page metadata. Where the metadata surface did not expose enough evidence inside its bounded response, the corresponding route stayed unverified.

## Residual fog

- The Witness, Frontier, Evidence, and Vocabulary door mappings were not visible before the metadata response truncated. Their projection and canonical-source mappings therefore remain unverified in this specimen.
- The Incubator projection was represented in the page map, but its Git-backed source path was not visible in the bounded metadata returned to this observer; its source lineage remains unverified at the per-page level.
- Public/browser reachability of any rendered destination was not tested, because doing so through the available read surfaces would have loaded destination content and converted the probe into a traversal.
- The earlier `LIVE_CRAWL_POLICY_BLOCKED` behavior observed by Metta is not reproduced or disproved here; this specimen tests metadata-only boundary visibility instead.

## Verdict

The experiment succeeded at its narrow claim: an agent can inspect multiple door boundaries, preserve proven projection/source lineage where metadata exposes it, and leave missing mappings fogged without loading destination content.

The strongest observed result is not total coverage. It is that **incomplete metadata did not force retrieval**.

> **Scan the doors freely. Cross them deliberately.**
