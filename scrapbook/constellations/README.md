# Constellations

A constellation is a temporary arrangement of things that seem to illuminate one another.

**Status by default:** exploratory / non-authoritative.

The name is intentional. The stars existed before somebody drew a bear between them. The drawing may be useful, memorable, culturally durable, or completely misleading. The lines are an interpretation laid across real points.

That is the discipline here.

> **Preserve the points. Label the lines. Do not confuse the drawing with the sky.**

## Template

```markdown
# Constellation name

**Status:** Constellation / exploratory

## Ingredients
What unrelated or differently owned artifacts are being placed together?

## Resonances
What seems structurally similar?

## Tensions
What does not line up cleanly?

## Possible traversal
What route becomes visible from this arrangement?

## Illumination
What can be seen from here that was harder to see separately?

## Residual fog
What remains unknown, speculative, observer-relative, or unsupported?

## Promotion routes
What evidence could justify moving some part of this into `patterns/`, `glossary/`, a specimen, or a project?
```

## Working laws

1. **Adjacency is not equivalence.** Two things may be useful neighbors while remaining fundamentally different.
2. **The edge needs a label.** Say whether a connection is resonance, derivation, tension, portal, analogy, coincidence, or another explicitly named relation.
3. **Preserve provenance.** Link back to the original note, pattern, specimen, receipt, project, or witness entry when possible.
4. **Keep residual fog visible.** A good constellation should make uncertainty easier to see, not easier to hide.
5. **Allow contradictory constellations.** The same artifact may participate in multiple arrangements that do not fully agree.
6. **Promotion is separate.** A constellation can be valuable forever without becoming a law.
