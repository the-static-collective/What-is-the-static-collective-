# Potential Energy Routing

**Status:** Constellation / exploratory

> **Find the charge. Clear the path. Close the circuit. Turn the machine.**

## Origin

A recurring teaching and creative-support pattern appeared across otherwise different requests: photography, guitar, writing, music, video, and practical AI.

The surface disciplines differ. The deeper problem often does not.

The person is not an empty vessel waiting to be filled with expertise. They already contain accumulated experience, desire, taste, memory, instinct, and unfinished work. The useful question is therefore not primarily:

> What information should we put into this person?

It is:

> **What already carries charge, and what is preventing that charge from becoming motion?**

This suggests a working metaphor: **route charged capacitors to stalled machinery.**

## The machine model

```text
stored charge
    ↓
blocked / missing circuit
    ↓
stalled machinery
    ↓
restored conductive path
    ↓
small motion
    ↓
new evidence
    ↓
more motion
```

The metaphor currently maps like this:

| Electrical / mechanical image | Human / creative referent |
| --- | --- |
| capacitor | accumulated experience, desire, taste, memory, skill |
| voltage | intensity of wanting / readiness to move |
| circuit | workflow, confidence, tools, habits, relationships |
| resistance | fear, complexity, cost, bad interfaces, missing knowledge |
| switch | permission, decision, first action |
| transformer | translation from an existing competence into a new medium |
| machine | the actual practice: writing, photography, music, film, making |
| output shaft | work that becomes visible in the world |
| receipt | concrete evidence that the machine moved |

The metaphor is intentionally provisional. Per [Metaphor as Portal](metaphor-as-portal.md), the image is useful only insofar as it helps expose explicit structure.

## First operation: clear the error logs off the path

Before adding instruction, inspect which old failures are currently being treated as live control signals.

Examples:

- “I tried writing and froze.”
- “I am bad with technology.”
- “I used to play, but I lost it.”
- “Nobody cared when I made things.”
- “I never finish.”
- “I do not know enough.”

These may be accurate historical records. The mistake is allowing them to execute as present-tense authority.

> **Error logs are evidence, not control signals.**

The goal is not to erase failure, argue against memory, or force confidence. It is to move diagnostic history out of the live execution path so a new attempt can occur.

## Candidate operating loop

### 1. Clear the path

Identify stale error messages that are being interpreted as current prohibitions. Preserve them as evidence; revoke their authority to decide the next act.

### 2. Find the charge

Look for existing desire, curiosity, irritation, memory, taste, field knowledge, partial skill, unfinished ideas, or recurring attention.

Do not begin by manufacturing motivation if usable charge is already present elsewhere.

### 3. Name the stalled machine

Ask what the person actually wants moving.

Not “learn photography,” but perhaps “make wildlife photographs that feel like being in the woods.”

Not “learn AI,” but perhaps “use AI to make this business process possible.”

Not “take guitar lessons,” but perhaps “play songs with other people without freezing.”

### 4. Inspect the circuit

Locate the smallest real resistance between charge and motion:

- missing technique;
- intimidating software;
- equipment friction;
- workflow complexity;
- lack of a first project;
- confidence collapse;
- cost;
- absence of feedback;
- a medium that no longer conducts well.

### 5. Route around unnecessary resistance

Do not rebuild the whole factory if a jumper cable can turn the motor.

A dormant writing practice may move first through music. A hunter's fieldcraft may enter photography through animal behavior rather than camera theory. A visual artist may recover narrative through video before returning to prose.

The shortest conductive path may cross disciplines.

### 6. Apply a small charge

Produce one bounded act:

- one photograph;
- one paragraph;
- one chord progression;
- one short video;
- one working AI-assisted workflow;
- one finished artifact.

The first objective is not mastery. It is motion.

### 7. Mint a new receipt

The new artifact changes the evidence available to the person.

Instead of arguing with “I cannot,” create a contradictory fact:

```text
machine moved
```

That receipt does not prove unlimited capability. It proves lawful reachability of at least one next state.

### 8. Let motion compound

Use the new evidence to choose the next small movement. The teacher, tool, or facilitator should become less necessary as the circuit stabilizes.

## Cross-domain specimens

### Existing fieldcraft → wildlife photography

A hunter may already possess patience, tracking, concealment, habitat knowledge, light awareness, and the ability to predict animal movement.

The teaching task is not to replace that knowledge with camera vocabulary. It is to install a transformer:

```text
fieldcraft + attention
        ↓
photographic timing + framing
        ↓
wildlife image
```

The camera is a new harvesting instrument, not the source of perception.

### Dormant writing / visual practice → music and video

A stalled medium does not necessarily imply a stalled person.

If writing no longer conducts, another medium may carry the same stored charge into motion. Music or video can become an alternate route; once movement exists, it may mechanically couple back into writing.

This suggests an important distinction:

> **Repairing the original machine is optional. Restoring motion is primary.**

## Candidate invariants

These are not yet promoted field laws, but the constellation currently points toward them:

1. **Assume stored capacity before assuming deficiency.**
2. **Historical failure may inform the route without governing it.**
3. **Teach toward desired motion, not toward completion of a curriculum.**
4. **Translate existing competence before importing new complexity.**
5. **Route through the lowest useful resistance, including across media.**
6. **Prefer a small finished act over a large explanatory argument.**
7. **Treat produced motion as new evidence.**
8. **A successful intervention should increase independent agency rather than permanent dependence on the facilitator.**

## Tensions

The metaphor can fail in several ways.

### People are not machines

The model must never imply that a person is an inert mechanism whose value is measured by output. The machinery represents a practice or desired capacity, not the human being.

### Not all resistance is waste

Some resistance is protective, ethical, developmental, or simply signals that a different path is needed. “Route around resistance” must not become “ignore every boundary.”

### Motion is not automatically good

A machine turning is evidence of capability, not evidence that the direction is wise. The person's own ends, relationships, obligations, and judgment remain authoritative.

### Tools can absorb agency

AI and specialized software can reduce friction so aggressively that the tool performs the meaningful act instead of enabling the person to perform it. A good circuit should preserve or enlarge human agency.

## Field-test shape

A minimal session can test the pattern without building a curriculum:

```text
What do you want moving?
        ↓
What already has charge?
        ↓
What old error is blocking the path?
        ↓
What is the smallest real resistance?
        ↓
What existing skill can cross the gap?
        ↓
Make one thing
        ↓
What does the new receipt prove?
        ↓
What becomes reachable next?
```

## Residual fog

- How can “stored charge” be distinguished from a desire somebody feels obligated to have?
- When should resistance be reduced versus respected as useful information?
- How much scaffolding can a tool provide before it begins replacing the person's own motion?
- Can the receipt model help someone update self-knowledge without turning creative practice into productivity accounting?
- Does repeated cross-domain success justify promoting this from a constellation into a portable pattern?

## Promotion route

Do not promote this to a field law because the metaphor is elegant.

Promotion would require repeated field cases showing that the same sequence survives across domains:

```text
existing charge
→ stale error authority removed
→ resistance identified
→ competence translated / path restored
→ bounded act completed
→ new evidence changes reachable next steps
```

Until then, keep it where it belongs: **a charged constellation with machinery beginning to turn.**
