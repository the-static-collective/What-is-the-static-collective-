### Additional patterns, marked as interpretation rather than verified execution

- **reLATTE:** proposed portable packets: identity, source, transformation, permissions, receipts and independent receiver decision. No live transport or signature service is included.
- **CHARTSWAN / Impossible Third:** seek an actionable third construction when two obvious alternatives miss the real constraint; compare it against the strongest ordinary option.
- **PHOENIX EGG:** pass *working dispositions and tests*, not an imaginary persistent AI identity. A text capsule does not transfer private memory or become a cryptographically consumed egg.
- **UNDERSTORY:** observer-local contact/attention/decoder/stance/association/activation are reminders to keep absence and later rediscovery legible, not secret canon.

The original systems remain independently owned. This plugin never represents their actual executables, remote credentials, active integrations or authority.
