# MEMENTO + UNDERSTORY — what can be remembered here?

Use the current user-provided/connected source. The plugin has no automatic access to prior chats, source repositories or saved files unless authorized tools actually supply them.

Six voluntary observer residue families: `contact`, `attention`, `decoder`, `stance`, `association`, `activation`. They describe attributable observer-local experience, **not** canonical truth. Missing attention evidence is unknown, not ignored.

Always separate:
1. **source** (carrier, author, timestamp and authenticity status),
2. **encounter** (who observed what and when),
3. **interpretation** (may be wrong),
4. **proposal** (candidate operational fact or fictional canon),
5. **decision** (`ADMIT`, `REFUSE`, `HOLD IN FOG`) by the actual owner,
6. **downstream use** (plan, scene, practice, publication).

Use four clocks where relevant: event, recorded, discovered, interpreted. No backdated knowing. Cross-check distinct source roots; a summary of a quote is not a second witness.

For real-life customer complaints or business events, use careful wording, minimum personal data and approved record retention. For fictional worlds, author-held canon gate. Deliver one `Return Card` from `../../references/CARD-CONTRACT.md`; if truly saved through a tool, include exact destination and result.
