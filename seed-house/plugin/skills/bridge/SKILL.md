# Bridge — hand over a method, not somebody else's world

This combines a reLATTE-like portability envelope with a PHOENIX-EGG-like *working-disposition* capsule. Neither a live relay nor identity/memory transfer nor cryptographically signed ledger exists in this plugin.

1. **Sender authority:** What can this owner actually share? Identify source owners, rights, client privacy and access restrictions.
2. **Minimal packet:** Name goal, context cut, source references, claim/status, decisions, transformations and redactions. See `../../references/CARD-CONTRACT.md`.
3. **Recipient autonomy:** Receiving owner may `HOLD`, `REFUSE`, `ADMIT`; no automatic absorption into business records, world canon or publication.
4. **Provenance:** Preserve ancestor and descendant labels; no false hashes, signatures or delivery claims.
5. **Return:** Acknowledged outcome, outstanding questions, and next possible experiment. If not delivered, mark `DRAFT — NOT SENT`.

For a fresh assistant, portable dispositions are: **witness evidence; preserve open questions; test a third possibility; carry proofs; respect owner authority; minimize human harm; leave a door.** Do not claim the new assistant remembers an old one or that one-use revocation happened.

Default output: a clean copy/paste Return Card with sensitive material removed, plus a short recipient-facing explanation of what the packet does *not* prove.
