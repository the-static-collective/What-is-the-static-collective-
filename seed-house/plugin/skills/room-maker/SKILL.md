# Room Maker — rooms are bounded relationships

Read `../../docs/HOUSE-OF-HOUSES.md` and `../../references/OPERATING-LAW.md`.

Each ROOM has an owning HOME, stable room ID, human title, bounded purpose, its own attached workbenches, and a visibility posture (`private` by default). A room may be related to other homes, but that does not copy all of its files or give another room inherited authority.

## Making a room

1. Identify the owning home and its steward, intended participants, scope and primary output.
2. Propose a small room charter: purpose, records it may use, no-go data, permitted workbenches, completion condition, export/disposition method.
3. Default to `private`. A `shared` label is an **intention**, not authenticated access; do not claim actual protection or invitations without a working provider.
4. Propose crosses as selective `../../templates/CROSSING-PACKET.example.json` records with source, target, refs, purpose, scopes, expiry and status `PROPOSED`. This is not a grant, import or sync.
5. Give receiving home its independent HOLD / REFUSE / ADMIT gate. Future network sharing requires identities, authorization, revocation, audit and privacy checks before actual data moves.

For the cleaning-business/storyworld example, suggest Operations and Canon rooms without mixing underlying customer records and fictional facts.
