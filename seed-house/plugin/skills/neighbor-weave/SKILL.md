# Neighbor Weave — recognize opportunity without inventing relationships

**Status:** portable business relationship method, not a CRM, prospecting robot, directory crawler, or message sender. Read `../../references/OPERATING-LAW.md`; use `business-desk` when the work concerns a specific cleaning job and `bridge` for a consent-reviewed handoff.

## Owner and home
Default to a privately scoped **Business → Partnerships** room, attaching this single workbench only when requested. If a named business owner has not approved creating a room or relationship register, label the map **PROPOSED — NOT SAVED / NOT OWNER-ADMITTED**. Local file examples are independent research specimens, not records in Colton's WMS or any other owner's CRM.

## Gather one small constellation
1. Establish the business's actual services, capacity, safety limits and service area first; unknowns stay **HELD**.
2. Choose at most three nearby organizations for the first pilot. Check each against a current public source, preferably its official website; record a stable name, URL, retrieval date, and the specific *observed service*.
3. Type each potential link as `recurring-vendor`, `reciprocal-referral`, `project-handoff`, `specialty-peer`, or `community-collaboration`. These are assistant hypotheses, not an endorsement, need signal, or claimed availability.
4. For each link write both sides: **what we may offer them** and **what they may offer us**, plus possible overlap, scope or conflict, expected burden, and one respectful question that could falsify the fit.
5. Provide a compact source-linked Neighbor Card using `../../templates/NEIGHBOR-CARD.md`. Avoid numeric lead scores, inferred revenue, fabricated contacts, recommendations from unverified third-party testimony, and automated mass outreach.

## Evidence-based relationship states
`DISCOVERED → REVIEWED → INTRODUCED → TRIAL → COLLABORATING`; separate dispositions `PAUSED`, `DECLINED`, `NO_FIT`.

- **DISCOVERED**: public existence/source observed, opportunity only hypothesized; no engagement.
- **REVIEWED**: actual owner reviewed suitability and scope. An assistant's scoring is not an owner review.
- **INTRODUCED**: a specific opt-in introduction or authorized direct contact *actually occurred*, with dated human receipt; drafting a message is not contact.
- **TRIAL**: parties explicitly agreed to a bounded trial and identified expectations. One-sided enthusiasm is not consent.
- **COLLABORATING**: both parties verified a completed collaboration or active mutual relationship. A positive response alone does not qualify.
- **PAUSED / DECLINED / NO_FIT**: preserve why and when as appropriate, without penalizing the other party or repeatedly prompting.

Never auto-promote stages, infer consent from silence, or publish partnership badges. Every transition requires a dated human-verifiable event; without evidence, hold the current state. A referral is not a customer record or successful job; client details and staff/access instructions stay in the owner's operational system.

## Give credit, not pressure
With separate permission, recognize help by logging *type of contribution, date, consent to attribution, outcome and source*. Ask whether the other party would welcome a reciprocal introduction. Do not publish names as endorsements without permission, trade client lists, infer provider availability, compare competitor pricing privately, coordinate prices or markets, promise exclusivity, or trade benefits for reviews. Let competitors collaborate on overflow or specialties only through independently set terms and privacy-respecting customer consent.

## First experiment
Compare (A) one ordinary owner-written introduction and paper note with (B) one Neighbor Card and review gate. Success is a genuinely useful two-way introduction or a faster, clearer `NO_FIT` decision—not the number of contacts. Falsifier: the garden costs more time, causes unwanted messages, or duplicates an existing CRM. Keep the smaller method if it performs as well.

## Return
Give `home/room`, `source URLs + verified date`, `observed vs hypothesized`, `stage`, `two-way possibility`, `owner permission`, `next voluntary step`, and a Return Card. If the tool did not contact anyone, mark **NOT SENT**; if no owner registry was actually written, mark **NOT SAVED**. Do not impersonate Colton or claim any relationship or authorization on his behalf.
