# STATIC SEED v2 — a house that grows with its owner

This is a collection of methods for building your own independent work ecosystem. Start with two or three **homes**—for example, Cleaning Business, Story World, and Publishing. Within each home, create as many **rooms** as you need. Attach a relevant **workbench** (planning, evidence, bookmaking, business operations, or research) only when it helps.

It does not connect to or read your existing accounts. Your customer records and manuscripts remain yours. The plugin is instructions, not a live service. A local registry script and sample files are provided for people who want durable portable project structure; otherwise you can copy the no-install starter prompt into ChatGPT and save your own approved results.

**First experiment:** "Help me build a House with a Cleaning Business home and a Story World home. Make an Operations room and a Canon room; tell me which workbench belongs in each and leave a reusable Return Card."

**Second experiment:** "Give me a safe crossing proposal for turning an original fictional mascot into a possible business promotion, without assuming I have approved the artwork, client data sharing, or publication." 

The creator controls what gets written, published, invoiced, shared, or treated as canon. The receiver controls whether to admit a handoff. An invitation does not convey rights, access, or history.
