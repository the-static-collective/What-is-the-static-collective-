# Start with one room

You do not need an account, a server, or knowledge of the Static Collective's terminology to start.

Ask your assistant: **"Show me my house and help me make a home for my current project."**

Choose any number of homes that are *actually useful*. A cleaning business may have an Operations home; a novel series may have a Story World home; published books may be under a Publishing home. You can create another home later without recreating the House.

Every home has rooms. Each room chooses only the workbenches it needs. One project can cross another through a *proposal packet* without silently exposing the private contents of either home.

For persistent operation, save the created House Registry to a file. The skills-only ChatGPT plugin does not save a personal registry automatically. The optional command-line registry tool provides local file-based persistence; it does not provide a shared website.

Start with one actual task and leave a Return Card. Do not invent a customer's data or declare fictional canon on the author's behalf.
