The point is not to call a machine autonomous. It is to distinguish a readable proposal from a power to act.

## A permission ladder

**Observe → prepare → verify exact source → propose → human/receiving owner admits or refuses → independently execute if authorized → inspect the physical/remote consequence → receipt.**

At every rung, stop cleanly if permissions, source, availability, energy, rights or safety cannot be established. The original state is not silently overwritten merely because an artifact was generated.

## Open engineering distinction

Many repos explore **portable, source-addressed descriptions**. This is not evidence of a universally implemented physical network. The next proof must leave the simulated garden: same consent, same offline requirement, two truly separate machines, two independent witnesses, and a readable failure.
