# Frontier · Music, image, page, and the right to return

Different engines transform different source particulars. Their common demand is lineage, not a shared renderer.

## Audible garden

- [Static Live #38](https://github.com/the-static-collective/static-live/pull/38) proposes a portable Living Hymnal adapter.
- [reLATTE #117](https://github.com/the-static-collective/reLATTE/pull/117) documents a synthetic text-only, SHA-pinned, P-256 signed crossing with two local recipient fixtures: one ADMIT, one REFUSE. Rights and church permission are **not externally proven**.
- [GHoT #131](https://github.com/the-static-collective/GHoT/pull/131) proposes a local read-only WAV-header stage-preflight capability. Its PR explicitly refuses claiming a live console/mixer/stream integration.
- [Static Live #41–43](https://github.com/the-static-collective/static-live/pull/43) stacks withdrawal leases, GHoT work and a two-laptop kit. Stacked ancestry must be respected.
- [Haunted Phonography #27](https://github.com/the-static-collective/the-haunted-phonography/pull/27) stages an exact Suno WAV → evidenced musical mutation → Static Live object experiment. The source artist/owner and rights remain local.

## Picture and page

- [Haunted Blender MANGALIZE #64–68](https://github.com/the-static-collective/the-haunted-blender/pull/68): separate harvest, reuse, performed use, pixel execution and presentation stages. A descendant must not retroactively change the parent.
- [LemonPRESS #35](https://github.com/the-static-collective/lemonPRESS/pull/35) treats an English→Japanese→returned-English synthetic relay and source-pixel rectangles as distinct particulars. It refuses text overflow/unsupported glyph substitution and leaves actual real-page intake as a separate gate.
- [Story DNA #1–5](https://github.com/the-static-collective/story-dna/pull/5) keeps relationship/character continuity and Grok Build rescue separate from an approved edition.
- [VidMage #21](https://github.com/the-static-collective/vidmage/pull/21) describes an iPhone-facing creative portal and separate Home/AWS machines, with DNS and paid infrastructure deliberately **not authorized** by that PR.
- [Haunted Toaster #360](https://github.com/the-static-collective/the-haunted-toaster/pull/360) exposes a source-pinned six-up experiment **without KEEP or render**; proposal selection is not production commitment.

## Editorial synthesis

**Song → documented source particular → derived arrangement / image / page / film candidate → human-selected use → separate rendering / release receipt.**

Never collapse 'proposed image' into 'owned source,' 'synthetic proof' into 'a finished media release,' or 'draft hymn' into 'congregational authorization.' The music can furnish a recognizable cultural entry point without making churches or neighbors participants by implication.
