**Question:** can the software help a real person make a specific, reversible offer or ask a bounded question without requiring a platform commitment?

## One smallest honest field loop

**A person notices something → privately records it → deliberately formulates a question → prints one locally readable card → presents it voluntarily → recipient can ignore, refuse or respond → optional receipt returns to the originating person.**

The response must not silently create a household case file, pretend a need is fulfilled, claim a church/co-op affiliation, or issue payment or points. The Coalition of Care event and the Waseca community-grocery hypothesis are **possible human conversations**, not an approved system integration.

## First real test proposal

Take **one** volunteered, non-sensitive public question and **one** paper card. Have a second human independently read the text/QR, say what it permits, and refuse it without penalty. Record legibility, source/QR mismatch, phone-offline behavior, misunderstanding and any data leakage. Obtain explicit permission before posting anyone's response.

**The human relationship is the system of record for consent. The wiki can only document the witnessed trace.**
