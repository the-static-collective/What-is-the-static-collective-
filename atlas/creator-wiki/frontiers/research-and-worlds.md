## Candidate reusable question

**When something changes representation, what survives, what is lost, who can recognize the result, and who can refuse admission?**

It turns up across mathematics, recorded Scripture, playable rooms, creative derivatives and physical quests. This is an **interpretive relationship**; no source inspection here establishes one common executable schema across them.

## Unresolved fog to keep

- How would a skeptical, independent recipient reconstruct one claimed relation without trusting the wiki?
- What is a counterexample to the claimed portability of a given pattern?
- Can a refusal alter the *visible future possibilities* while leaving constituted history and authority untouched?
- Are apparently identical return rooms materially distinct because of their different histories?
- Which specimens actually ran on independent hardware, and which are synthetic fixtures?
- What exact source/witness/owner action, if any, would graduate a proposal?

Inquiry remains useful without promotion.
