## Review gates

**Before the first coalition-facing demo:** pick only public-safe fake or explicitly volunteered input; test print legibility; create a nontracking, non-coercive exit; get source-owner review. **Do not imply endorsement** from a meeting organizer, community business, church or participant.

**Before deployment:** identify which exact branch was tested, current CI, license/right to reuse, domain/DNS authority, data handling, audit logs, offline refusal and accountable service operator.

**Before calling anything canon:** owning human or project must select the exact scope and exact evidence. A creator wiki's vote is always **none**.
