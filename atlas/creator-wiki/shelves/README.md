# Eight public terrain shelves

Shelves are navigational addresses, not categories of being, status, correctness or maturity. Each project owns its identity. The complete machine inventory is [here](../inventory.md).

- [Origins and witnesses](origins-and-witnesses.md) — Origin stories, preserved history and continuity witnesses. A historical repo is not obsolete merely because it is quiet.
- [Contracts and memory](contracts-and-memory.md) — Boundaries between evidence, authority, calculation, continuity and action.
- [Participation and care](participation-and-care.md) — Voluntary physical participation, needs, food, garden and local return.
- [Songs and stages](songs-and-stages.md) — Musical encounter, song ancestry, stage projections and performance.
- [Images and appliances](images-and-appliances.md) — Compositional machines for image, audio, music, video and materialization.
- [Worlds and inquiry](worlds-and-inquiry.md) — Playable spaces, historical research, interpretation and Scripture encounters.
- [Operating surfaces](operating-surfaces.md) — Hosts, desks, physical transports, local operating experiments and status envelopes.
- [Unplaced public repositories](unplaced.md) — New or not-yet-editorially-placed repos, intentionally not interpreted by naming alone.
