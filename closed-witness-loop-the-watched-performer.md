# Closed Witness Loop — The Watched Performer

The internal Witness Window should not only receive the performance. The Haunted Toaster should be able to sense the state of its own witness and let that sensed attention alter the next compositional choice.

## Core law

> **The Witness does not control the performance. The performance becomes aware of being witnessed.**

Closed-loop shape:

> **song / image / current composition → performer → Witness Window → witness state → bounded performer response → next compositional choice**

This begins as an entirely internal compositional loop. No real-user telemetry, engagement metrics, or external audience analytics are required.

## Why closed-loop first

An internal witness lets the system discover the grammar of witnessed performance without confusing that grammar with popularity or audience measurement.

The useful questions are compositional:

* What does a performer do when it feels intensely watched?
* What changes while the witness blinks?
* What happens when the witness is overwhelmed?
* When does scrutiny provoke showing off, withdrawal, intimacy, or restraint?
* Can a moment become stronger because the performer senses that the witness is fully present and therefore refuses to break it?

The target is not engagement optimization. The target is **stage awareness**.

## Witness states

Keep the first vocabulary small and behaviorally distinct.

### Focused

Attention is locked onto the performance. Focus may encourage holding, revealing, precision, or restraint rather than simple escalation.

### Wandering

Attention is drifting. The performer may simplify, misdirect, introduce a small surprise, or allow the field to breathe.

### Blinking

Perception briefly disappears. A blink can become a lawful opportunity for a hidden mutation, jump, substitution, or state change that the witness does not directly see happen.

### Pressured

The witness is under perceptual strain. Pressure may provoke either greater display or retreat; it must not map mechanically to more intensity.

### Clear

Calm, stable seeing. Clear witness may invite chromatic decompression, native-color testimony, stable framing, or other moments where the source is allowed to re-emerge.

### Haunted

The witness carries residue from what it just saw. A haunted witness may bias attention toward scars, echoes, returning colors, prior geometry, or unresolved visual consequences.

### Breathless

Attention is so complete or suspended that the witness effectively **stops breathing for a moment**.

Breathless is not merely a higher pressure value. It is a distinct **held-attention state**.

The performer may respond by:

* sustaining a fragile state longer than expected;
* delaying an obvious release;
* becoming quieter rather than louder;
* exposing a detail instead of escalating the whole frame;
* suspending motion while maintaining tension;
* refusing to break a moment because it senses that the witness is fully there.

Breathless therefore gives the composition a lawful reason to **hold the room**.

## Performer responses

Witness state should alter **choice**, not just effect amplitude.

Possible responses:

* **hold** — sustain because the moment is being truly seen;
* **slip** — change while the witness is absent or blinking;
* **show** — reveal more under sustained attention;
* **hide** — withdraw under scrutiny;
* **swell** — take a bounded risk under pressure;
* **soften** — become intimate instead of louder;
* **misdirect** — move around the witness instead of directly toward it;
* **recover** — simplify or clarify after overload;
* **suspend** — under breathless attention, preserve a charged state and delay release.

## Anti-law: attention is not an energy knob

Do not reduce the loop to rules such as `focused = +20% effects` or `pressure = +30% shake`.

More interesting mappings are categorical and contextual:

* focus may extend a phrase;
* blink may permit an unseen mutation;
* pressure may provoke display **or** retreat;
* clarity may invite Native Color Witness;
* haunted attention may pull residue forward instead of introducing novelty;
* breathless attention may cause the performer to suspend an expected transition.

> **Being witnessed changes the available choices, not merely their intensity.**

## Memory

Closed Witness Loop should leave compositional memory inside the song:

* this phrase was strongly witnessed;
* this transition happened unseen;
* this event overwhelmed the witness;
* this section returned under a changed witness condition;
* this moment produced breathless attention and was deliberately held;
* the witness recovered clarity after distortion;
* an earlier perceptual wound is still influencing what gets noticed.

This lets recurrence become relational:

> **The same phrase performed to a changed witness is not the same performance.**

Haunted Memory may eventually harvest recurring cross-toast patterns, but current-song evidence remains primary and long-term memory remains non-sovereign.

## Relationship to the Fallible Witness

Fallible Witness and Closed Witness Loop are reciprocal but distinct.

**Fallible Witness:** the performance changes what the witness can see.

**Closed Witness Loop:** the witness's resulting state changes what the performer chooses next.

Together they form a causal circle:

> **performance affects perception → perception affects performance**

The loop should remain bounded so it does not self-excite into meaningless instability.

## Authority and provenance

The Witness Window can be fallible as perception. The performer can react to that fallibility. The receipt must still truthfully record the causal sequence.

> **The Witness may influence composition. It may not manufacture truth.**

The Witness remains part of the composition, not a hidden authority plane.

## First proof boundary

If implementation is later approved, begin with only a small state set:

`focus · blink · pressure · clarity · residue · breathless`

Allow those signals to influence only a few decisions:

* phrase hold duration;
* permission for hidden mutation;
* reveal versus withhold;
* recovery after large events;
* residue carry-forward;
* suspension / delayed release under breathless attention.

This is enough to prove the loop without making the renderer mushy or self-referential for its own sake.

## Working laws

> **A watched performance is a different performance.**

> **The Witness Window is not only how the piece is seen. It is part of what the piece is performing to.**

> **Breathless attention is not a request for more. Sometimes it is a reason not to move.**
