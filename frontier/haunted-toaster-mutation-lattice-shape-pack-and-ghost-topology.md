# Haunted Toaster — Mutation Lattice, Shape Pack & Ghost Topology

**Status:** implementation candidate on PR #131; exact-head package gate running\
**Target:** `0.5.0-alpha.9`\
**Authority:** one accepted score → timeline → preview/render → sidecars → receipt chain

## Intent

The next Toaster update expands the visible outcome space from a handful of broad families into a compositional field. Toast Feel remains a pressure system, not a preset selector; all choices remain deterministic, replayable, lock-respecting, and receipt-bound.

> Toast Feel biases the creature. It does not dictate the creature.

## Mutation Lattice v1

| Layer    | Responsibility                                   |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Skeleton | authoritative topology and optional topology arc |
| Body     | primitive structure and primitive dynamics       |
| Frame    | field envelope and camera grammar                |
| Skin     | material, palette, and native-color relationship |
| Weather  | atmosphere                                       |

Each ordinary Toast Feel gains sparse categorical affinity across all six layers in addition to its alpha.8 numeric pressure. MADD CLOWN continues to use the seeded maximum-distance STOMP rail.

A normal unlocked six-up targets at least four authoritative topologies, at least four cross-layer signatures, no duplicate skeleton/body/frame signature, one Shape Pack topology, and one apparition when eligible. Impossible targets become explicit shortfall evidence; locks are never weakened.

## Shape Pack v1

The topology vocabulary grows from five to nine:

* **Elastic Spine** — a vertical living waveform with bounded flex and recoil.
* **Split Horizon** — two phase-related bodies whose negative space is structural.
* **Cathedral Fan** — an open upward field of arches, ribs, and radial wedges.
* **Echo Tunnel** — a bounded stack of phase-separated descendants creating depth and recurrence.

Every shape has a stable identifier, v3-only compiler identity, field-envelope policy, finite parameters, production FFmpeg smoke, capability identity, and receipt evidence. Historical v1/v2 compilers keep their meaning.

## Ghost Topology / Resonant Succession

A topology arc follows:

`hold → apparition → resonant overlap → dissolve | scar | succession`

* **Apparition:** the ghost appears and dissolves; the source remains authoritative.
* **Scar:** the ghost dissolves and leaves one admitted change in primitive field, field envelope, material, or motion.
* **Succession:** the ghost crosses the accepted threshold and becomes authoritative after release.

The canonical schedule contains zero to two ordered, non-overlapping windows and at most one succession. Windows bind source/ghost compiler identities, entrance/peak/release ticks, boundary evidence, overlap policy, outcome, optional scar, and SHA-256 evidence.

Topology locks prohibit every arc. A scar-axis lock prohibits that scar. Ordinary moods bias events, Risky Hybrid widens lawful pairings, and MADD CLOWN seeks one lawful event without bypassing constraints.
