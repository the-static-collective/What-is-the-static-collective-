# Physics of Meeting — Constitutional Worlds Without Capture

**Status:** project-backed primitive with one bounded executable seed — larger horizon remains incubating; not constitutional law, not universal protocol authority

## The claim

The long trajectory is not one giant application, one omniscient agent, or one canonical graph.

It is a substrate in which **many bounded worlds can remain themselves, meet, exchange testimony, and change through accountable encounter without being collapsed into one authority plane.**

The useful compression is:

```
plurality
+
attribution
+
bounded authority
+
lawful crossing
+
durable consequence
=
coherence without flattening
```

This is no longer only a north-star metaphor. Project0 now contains one executable, deliberately narrow specimen of the underlying meeting law. The larger horizon has not thereby been proved.

## Candidate world shape

A future constituted world may be less like a database and more like a bounded frame carrying recoverable relations such as:

```
World / Frame
├── identity / roots
├── accountable history
├── inhabitants / participants
├── local authorities
├── disclosure boundaries
├── artifacts and witnesses
├── unresolved plurality
├── reachable neighboring doors
├── refusal / scar topology
└── lawful ways of becoming something else
```

This is deliberately **not** proposed as a new universal Project0 node kind.

Enough bounded structure may exist for a world to be re-entered, compared, encountered, and changed without requiring every world to share one internal ontology.

## The physics of meeting

The first executable law is:

> **A source frame may send bounded testimony across a declared boundary; the destination must decide locally what that testimony is allowed to become. Crossing never carries sovereignty.**

A healthy encounter looks like:

```
WORLD A
  local meaning
  local history
  local authority
      |
      | bounded testimony + provenance
      v
[ encounter envelope ]
      |
      | destination-local evaluation
      v
WORLD B
  different meaning may survive
  different authority remains sovereign
  received testimony may be admitted,
  refused, or left indeterminate
```

The envelope is not a treaty granting control. It is not a universal translator proving equivalence. It is a bounded object that can preserve what is offered, where it came from, source provenance and limitations, disclosure requirements, destination-local disposition, and evidence of what changed.

## Translation without ontology capture

The far horizon becomes interesting when agents can act as bounded translators rather than global narrators.

A translator may eventually say:

```
In frame A, this object occupies relation X.
In frame B, the nearest useful interpretation is Y.
These properties survived translation.
These did not.
These remain unresolved.
No authority crossed with the interpretation.
```

That would permit semantic interoperability without requiring schema identity.

The translation itself would remain attributable proposal / interpretation until the destination grants it whatever local standing is appropriate.

**No implementation has earned this translation claim yet.**

## Three geometries, perhaps four

The recent Corpus / NAV / Refusal work suggests that a mature field could hold several different geometries simultaneously:

### Constituted

What actually became reachable through accountable history.

### Latent

What appears reachable from the current frame without pretending it has happened.

### Refused

What was attempted, encountered a boundary, and did not become constituted state.

### Unknown

What has not been traversed or honestly characterized.

The system should resist converting these into one scalar score or one master optimization surface. Their value is precisely that they remain different.

## Human plurality is the point

One person's observation can remain that person's observation.

One person's interpretation can remain that person's interpretation.

A disagreement can remain attributable without being averaged away.

A machine contribution can remain a proposal without impersonating human witness.

A failed encounter can become useful evidence without becoming everybody's rule.

A shared world can therefore accumulate memory without requiring a single unquestionable narrator.

## Internet of sovereign contexts

The extreme extrapolation is not one Static Collective world.

It is many independently governed contexts — families, bands, churches, labs, schools, archives, games, collectives, trusts, studios, agents — each preserving its own history and authority while exchanging bounded testimony with others.

Ordinary interoperability often means:

> Convert your thing into my schema.

The stronger possibility is:

> Keep your ontology. Keep your authority. Keep your history. Here is a lawful crossing between us.

This remains a horizon. One Project0 fixture does not establish a universal protocol.

## What must stay invariant

1. **Plurality is not failure.** Independent frames may remain unreconciled.
2. **Crossing is not authority transfer.** Provenance may travel; sovereignty does not silently travel with it.
3. **Receiving is not canonizing.** Visibility, retrieval, verification, usefulness, or repetition cannot upgrade standing automatically.
4. **Destination authority is local.** A destination decides under its own declared authority boundary.
5. **Source history is not rewritten by reception.** Encounter creates new attributable consequence rather than editing the past.
6. **Disclosure precedes inspection.** A cross-world envelope is not a loophole around privacy or bounded revelation.
7. **Translation is attributable.** Interpretation across vocabularies does not manufacture equivalence.
8. **NAV is an instrument, not the master map.** It may witness differences and crossings but may not become global constitutional authority.
9. **Refusal and indeterminacy remain visible.** Failed or unresolved encounters are topology, not garbage.
10. **Creative particularity survives abstraction.** The protocol exists to let strange, local worlds meet — not to sand them into interchangeable nodes.

## Failure conditions

Reject or redesign this horizon if it requires:

* one global ontology before independent worlds can meet;
* one global identity provider or central authority;
* one omniscient agent context;
* source authority becoming destination authority through transport;
* private material being inspected before permission is established;
* histories being rewritten for narrative neatness;
* disagreement being averaged into synthetic consensus;
* NAV ranking itself into truth;
* refusal topology becoming automated prohibition outside the frame that produced it;
* a master world graph claiming authority over the worlds it describes.

## Design sentence

> **The Static Collective may be growing the physics by which many worlds can remain sovereign, become mutually intelligible, and change through encounter without requiring any one of them to become the world for everyone else.**

The word **may** remains important. Project0 has now proved one seed. The rest must continue earning itself through encounters.
