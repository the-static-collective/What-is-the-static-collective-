## The opening

A growing creative system eventually needs more than new effects. It needs ways to **sense its own field** and regulate increasingly complex interactions without appointing an opaque governor.

Open-source DSP, computer vision, embedding models, state-machine frameworks, and research-grade music-analysis systems contain useful organs for this purpose.

The proposed law is:

> **A learned or foreign component may produce testimony. It may never produce authority.**

The system may borrow eyes, ears, similarity geometry, or reflex machinery. Those components do not get to decide what becomes canonical, what renders, what is true, or what wins.

## Perceptual Reflex Layer

The first primitive is a portable sensing-and-reflex boundary:

```
source audio / video / lyrics / runtime state
                ↓
        PERCEPTION ORGANS
  DSP / CV / frozen embeddings / probes
                ↓
         typed observations
        authority: testimony only
                ↓
        REFLEX / REGULATOR
 deterministic native rules / statecharts
                ↓
       bounded influence / proposals
                ↓
      receiving project's own authority
```

A useful sensor witness carries enough evidence to answer:

```
what sensor?
which version?
what exact source bytes / interval?
what parameters?
what observation?
what confidence?
what model/weight hash, if any?
what license witness?
what authority? → testimony only
```

Measurement and policy remain separate.

A frozen visual model may report that candidate-family distance has collapsed. It does not say **EXPLORE**. A native deterministic policy may decide that the measured collapse permits increased mutation pressure.

That boundary keeps learned perception from silently becoming governorship.

## Candidate organs

The Haunted Toaster design checkpoint records a first research catalog. These are fits, not mandatory dependencies.

### librosa — audio / MIR bench

Useful for onset/transient evidence, energy envelopes, spectral measures, pulse hypotheses, harmonic/percussive evidence, and recurrence/section features.

### OpenCV — visual geometry

Useful for frame difference, optical flow, foreground/background change, edge/contour/occupancy measures, quiet-region sensing, and deterministic image transforms.

### DINOv2 — visual feature geometry

Useful for candidate-family distance, near-duplicate collapse detection, novelty/coverage measurement, and visual-neighborhood relationships.

Its clean role is microscope, not taste judge.

### MediaPipe — anatomy / topology witness

Useful for body/hand/face landmark geometry, foreground masks, pose axes, typography avoidance, and body-attached visual behavior without requiring semantic or emotional claims.

### Sentence Transformers / small text embeddings — semantic coordinates

Useful for lyric similarity, motif recurrence, specimen/tag proximity, bounded retrieval, and semantic distance without asking a model to write.

### XState — reflex topology

Useful for explicit deterministic state machines, thresholds, refusal/degraded states, and inspectable transition history.

### ONNX Runtime — model-cartridge socket

Potentially useful as a boring local inference boundary: exact model in, tensor result out, typed witness emitted. Model identity and license remain separate from runtime identity.

### BehaviorTree.CPP — robotics reflex reference

Potentially useful as a source of reactive-tree and transition-logging ideas. A JS-native re-expression may be more appropriate than importing the C++ dependency.

### Essentia — First Dropout

Essentia is unusually broad: classical DSP, spectral/temporal/tonal descriptors, beat/rhythm analysis, and optional learned-model machinery.

That makes it valuable for asking:

> **What senses should this organism possess?**

Its licensing and dependency surface also make it an excellent founding specimen for a second primitive: **Compost Translation**.

## Compost Translation

Compost Translation does not mean making an Essentia-compatible clone.

It means allowing foreign machinery to enter a bounded research stage, reveal useful laws, and then be regrown in the native grammar of the receiving ecosystem.

> **Knowledge may cross; dependency stays behind.**

```
foreign research organism
        ↓
      INGEST
        ↓
controlled specimens + receipts
        ↓
      DIGEST
        ↓
behavioral hypotheses / public math / spec
        ↓
       GROW
        ↓
native eCODEsystem primitive
        ↓
     COMPOST
        ↓
foreign runtime dependency drops out
```

### Ingest

Reference runs are bound to exact tool/model version, parameters, specimen bytes, outputs, and observed license provenance.

### Digest

Controlled specimens make behavior legible:

```
silence
single impulses
periodic impulses
sine tones
frequency sweeps
noise
amplitude ramps
multi-tone mixtures
sample-rate variants
frame/window-size variants
fixed real specimens
```

Hypotheses are versioned and allowed to fail rather than being silently rewritten.

```
H17
  explains R001-R438
  fails R439
  supersedes H11
```

### Grow

The graduating primitive is shaped around eCODEsystem semantics instead of preserving a foreign API.

```
foreign concept:
  detectOnsets(audio)

native concept:
  witnessTransientPressure(window)
    → interval
    → pressure
    → confidence
    → contributing bands
    → lineage
```

Now the result can compose with Toaster, Phonograph, Band Runtime, Camera, TranchNode, or another project without carrying an adapter-shaped ontology.

### Compost

The foreign dependency can disappear from production while its historical contribution remains attributable through research receipts, public literature, failed hypotheses, specimen corpora, and native proof.

The dependency dies. Its contribution does not.

## First Dropout — Essentia

Essentia should begin as a quarantined research organism rather than a Toaster runtime dependency.

The research harness may compare an Essentia reference result against native/basic DSP on fixed specimens and gradually characterize one bounded behavior.

A clean stage-separation shape is:

```
REFERENCE LAB
  executes foreign tool
  reads documented API / public papers
  emits behavior spec + receipts

          ║ ONLY SPEC CROSSES ║

NATIVE IMPLEMENTATION
  consumes behavior spec + public math
  does not depend on foreign runtime/source

          ║ DIFFERENTIAL PROOF ║

REFERENCE OUTPUT ↔ NATIVE OUTPUT
```

Receipts strengthen provenance and separation. They are not a magical license eraser; exact copyright, patent, contract, model-license, dependency, and anticircumvention questions remain scoped to the actual tool/use.

## The first grounded Toaster experiment

Haunted Toaster issue #183 already provides the right seam.

Some VSPantry clips carry attached audio. The Toaster may hear that audio as a weak deterministic **Specimen Pulse** without placing it in the final soundtrack or granting it a second song clock.

The smallest research descendant is:

```
VSPantry attached audio
        ↓
Specimen Pulse research bench
        ├── native/basic DSP baseline
        └── optional Essentia reference
        ↓
receipted comparison corpus
        ↓
one native graduated witness
```

The first graduation target should remain low-level and inspectable: transient pressure, spectral flux, or bounded energy dynamics are stronger starting candidates than beat semantics, source separation, classifier stacks, or embeddings.

The proof is not that Essentia can run.

The proof is that one useful law can be learned, expressed in native witness grammar, reimplemented, tested against fixed specimens, and then used without Essentia in the production path.

## Three adoption postures

### Green — ordinary / permissive candidate

May be considered for production after runtime, packaging, privacy, determinism, transitive dependency, and exact-license inspection.

### Yellow — learned microscope

Frozen models may provide testimony when exact weights, version, hash, and license are known and outputs remain non-authoritative.

### Red — research-only until separately cleared

Useful in quarantine when licensing, runtime size, opacity, model terms, or dependency chains make direct production adoption undesirable.

Essentia is the founding specimen here.

These are local adoption postures, not permanent judgments about upstream projects.

## Cross-ecosystem fit

The value of native witness grammar is that the same sense can migrate without shared execution authority.

* **Haunted Toaster** — candidate diversity, Specimen Pulse, motion/topology response, typography placement.
* **Haunted Phonograph** — rhythmic/structural seeds without semantic authority.
* **Band Runtime** — density, silence, recurrence, collision, shared field pressure without central assignment.
* **Founder Node** — similarity/neighborhood geometry without proximity becoming routing authority.
* **Corpus OS** — anomaly/pressure testimony without statistics constituting legal state.
* **TranchNode / Project0** — deterministic projections while projection remains witness.
* **Haunted Camera** — pose, motion, foreground, and reflex sensing without an AI governor.

This composes the BEE direction already present elsewhere in the Collective:

> **Provenance may travel; authority stays local.**

Here that law gains a technical sibling:

> **Knowledge may cross; dependency stays behind.**

## What remains unresolved

The current frontier does not yet decide:

* the final generic `SensorWitness` schema;
* whether XState earns a production dependency or merely informs a native statechart shape;
* which learned microscopes are small/stable enough to package locally;
* the exact first Essentia algorithm chosen for differential characterization;
* whether the perceptual layer eventually deserves a standalone shared package or remains a repeated BEE-transplanted pattern.

Those are intentionally left open until the first #183-derived specimen produces evidence.
