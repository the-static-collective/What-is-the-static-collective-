# Collective Dreamstate — Shared Dream, Separate Bodies

**Status:** Frontier / Primitive Incubator. This page preserves a design hypothesis, not a shared runtime, ontology, or authority surface.

## Compression

> **Shared dream. Separate bodies. Local consequence. Returned residue.**

The **Collective Dreamstate** is a proposed shared possibility field in which unfinished ideas, images, sounds, memories, relations, event phrases, contradictions, and residue may coexist without first becoming one constituted world.

Its likely engineering substrate is a future **Free Event Graph** or **Open Phrase Graph**. The graph is the machinery. **Collective Dreamstate** is the human-facing name for what that machinery makes inhabitable.

The central distinction is:

```text
DREAMSTATE != AUTHORITY
DREAMSTATE != CONSTITUTED WORLD
DREAMSTATE != CONSENSUS
DREAMSTATE != SHARED EXECUTION STATE
```

The Collective may dream freely. Each participating body still decides locally what it may admit, execute, refuse, transform, or carry forward.

## Felt possibility

The eCODEsystem repeatedly produces useful material before that material has one settled owner or final meaning:

* a visual mutation that might become a Haunted Toaster behavior;
* a phrase that later becomes Tranchish vocabulary;
* an encounter residue that illuminates continuity somewhere else;
* a sound, image, research clue, topology, relationship, or unresolved contradiction whose future use is not yet known;
* multiple incompatible possibilities that are each worth preserving until a local context makes one relevant.

Today those possibilities live in many local forms: issues, branches, receipts, GitBook frontier notes, visual candidates, witness fields, memories, scraps, and conversations.

The Dreamstate asks whether there is eventually a useful shared **composition field** above those local embodiments without creating a shared sovereign world.

## Dreaming is not doing

A dream may propose relations that no local body ever adopts.

A useful flow is:

```text
memory / observation / desire / residue / idea
                  ↓
         COLLECTIVE DREAMSTATE
                  ↓
       possibilities relate / fork
                  ↓
          local encounter
                  ↓
 recognize | ignore | transform | refuse
                  ↓
       project-local admission
                  ↓
       constituted consequence
                  ↓
        witness / residue
                  ↓
      dream changed by reality
                  ↺
```

The return arrow is load-bearing. The Dreamstate is not only a pre-execution idea board. **Actual consequence may return attributable residue to the possibility field**, changing what can be imagined next without rewriting what happened.

## No consensus requirement

The Dreamstate should be allowed to contain incompatible or unresolved possibilities simultaneously.

```text
possibility A ─┐
possibility B ─┼─ coexist without forced merge
possibility C ─┘
```

A Haunted Toaster interpretation, a Corpus consequence reading, a research analogy, and a lyric fragment may all touch the same neighborhood without becoming synonyms.

This is closer to **forkable shared imagination** than to a canonical global state.

## Candidate engineering substrate — Free Event Graph

The first technical sketch is intentionally loose.

A future graph may need some combination of:

```text
nodes       things / regions / motifs / participants / phrases / residues
relations   influence / ancestry / adjacency / tension / handoff / attraction
events      bounded actions with preparation, strike, consequence, and residue
channels    visual / sonic / textual / temporal / spatial / evidentiary
constraints locks / protections / exclusions / local compatibility
annotations mood / intensity / attention / uncertainty / provenance
```

This is **not yet a universal schema**. The names above are probes for what a shared composition field may need to express.

A graph fragment might conceptually say:

```text
lyric-fragment
    notices → face-region

face-region
    emits → ghost-frame

ghost-frame
    grabs → atmosphere-field

atmosphere-field
    grows → carried strands
```

That fragment is still a proposal-space object. It is not permission for any renderer, corpus, room, or runtime to act.

## The compiler boundary

The strongest current architectural guess is:

```text
COLLECTIVE DREAMSTATE
shared possibility / phrase graph
            ↓
      LOCAL COMPILER
            ↓
 project-owned accepted plan
            ↓
    project-local execution
            ↓
      terminal receipt
            ↓
 attributable residue may re-enter Dreamstate
```

A shared graph therefore does **not** require a shared execution semantics.

Different bodies may encounter the same graph fragment and compile it differently—or refuse it entirely.

Examples:

* Haunted Toaster may compile a fragment into candidate recipes, topology events, Resolution Field decisions, and finally an accepted `VisualScore → ResolvedTimeline` path.
* A future room or interaction surface may compile related phrases into presentation or navigation choreography.
* A continuity-oriented project may use a graph only as proposal/evidence input and refuse any direct executable interpretation.

The shared substrate carries possibility and attributable relation. **Authority stays local.**

## Founding executable descendant — Haunted Toaster render mutation grammar

Haunted Toaster is the first obvious pressure specimen because it already has deterministic candidate ecology, a second six-up move deck, Topology Events, Resolution Field, Atmosphere, materials, and accepted timeline authority.

The proposed first bounded grammar is:

```text
VERB × TARGET × ENVELOPE × CONSEQUENCE × MATERIAL × ATTENTION
```

For example:

```text
GRAB
× atmosphere
× snap → recoil
× persistent scar
× dust
× 1/4-resolution periphery
```

The same small set of orthogonal axes can produce many materially different recipes while remaining inspectable and replayable.

This grammar is intentionally **smaller than the desired future graph**.

It should be implemented first because it can prove three important things cheaply:

1. a few composable primitives can multiply creative range without multiplying permanent effect presets;
2. deterministic recipe proposals can remain distinct from accepted execution authority;
3. the resulting grammar can later be lifted into graph clauses instead of thrown away.

Working development law:

> **When one new primitive is added, make it pay rent by composing with several existing surfaces before adding another primitive.**

## Imminent frontier — from grammar to free graph

The Free Event Graph is not a distant decorative idea. The current desire is explicitly to grow toward it **soon after the bounded grammar proves itself**.

The likely progression is:

```text
small orthogonal grammar
        ↓
authored named recipes
        ↓
recipe sequences / event phrases
        ↓
small explicit event graph
        ↓
free graph authoring / composition field
        ↓
multiple local compilers
```

Do not skip the early stages merely because the graph is attractive. The small grammar gives us executable evidence about which distinctions are actually independent, which combinations collapse, and where legality/refusal belongs.

The graph should inherit those discoveries rather than guessing a universal ontology in advance.

## Smallest Dreamstate graph experiment

After the Toaster grammar exists, construct one deliberately tiny graph specimen:

* 3–5 nodes;
* 4–6 attributable relations/events;
* at least one fork or unresolved alternative;
* at least one constraint/lock;
* no ambient randomness;
* no graph-local execution authority.

Then ask two independent consumers to encounter it.

The first consumer should be Haunted Toaster. It compiles one lawful descendant into its own accepted render plan.

A second consumer should **not** be forced to produce the same interpretation. Success would be stronger if it can truthfully derive a different local projection—or explicitly refuse the fragment—while the shared graph remains unchanged.

## What must stay invariant

1. **Dreamstate is proposal-space, not authority-space.**
2. **Shared relation does not imply shared ontology.** Similar names or edges may compile differently in different bodies.
3. **Attribution survives.** Ideas, relations, transformations, and returned residue retain ancestry where available.
4. **Contradiction is legal.** The graph need not force consensus or convergence.
5. **Local refusal is first-class.** A body may be unable or unwilling to compile a dream fragment.
6. **Execution requires a local constituted plan.** No direct Dreamstate → side effect shortcut.
7. **Returned residue does not rewrite the dream's ancestry.** It adds later evidence and possibility.
8. **Unknown remains unknown.** The graph may preserve fog rather than inventing relations for neatness.
9. **No central interpreter becomes sovereign.** A shared compiler library, if one ever exists, cannot decide project-local authority.

## Metaphysical restraint

The name **Collective Dreamstate** is deliberately evocative. This page does **not** claim that collaborators share one consciousness, subconscious mind, hidden psychic channel, metaphysical field, or biological nervous system.

Those may be separate philosophical or spiritual questions. The engineering claim required here is much smaller:

> **A collective can maintain a shared, attributable, non-authoritative possibility field in which partial structures coexist and may later be encountered by locally sovereign participants.**

The architecture stands or falls on whether that field proves useful, not on a metaphysical explanation for why shared imagination is possible.

## Relationship to nearby work

This hypothesis composes with existing Static Collective work without absorbing it:

* **Stigmergic Field** proves environmental field state can become replayably legible without gaining authority.
* **Ghost / Residual Presence** supplies a discipline for history remaining influential without counterfeiting current presence.
* **Creative Handoff** and **Physics of Meeting** preserve encounter without automatic capture.
* **Continuity Witness** preserves plural descendants and attributable difference.
* **Mouth / Eye / Hand / Hair** distinguishes seeing, saying, doing, and carrying; Dreamstate primarily lives upstream of Hand.
* **Haunted Toaster Topology Events** supplies the concrete insight that a verb may act on a topology without becoming another permanent topology preset.
* **Resolution Field** supplies a composable attention dimension rather than another monolithic effect.

None of these sources independently proves a Free Event Graph. They provide pressure and reusable boundaries for the experiment.

## Graduation pressure

Do not promote Collective Dreamstate into a shared pattern merely because the metaphor fits.

Useful graduation evidence would require materially different local bodies showing that a shared possibility graph can:

* preserve attributable partial ideas and contradictions;
* support more than one local compilation;
* produce useful consequences without transferring authority;
* preserve refusal/unresolved states;
* accept returned residue without rewriting prior graph history;
* remain simpler and more useful than merely exchanging project-native artifacts directly.

If the only successful consumer is Haunted Toaster, keep the graph local to Haunted Toaster.

If a second domain requires the graph to lie about its own semantics in order to share it, **do not share the schema**.
