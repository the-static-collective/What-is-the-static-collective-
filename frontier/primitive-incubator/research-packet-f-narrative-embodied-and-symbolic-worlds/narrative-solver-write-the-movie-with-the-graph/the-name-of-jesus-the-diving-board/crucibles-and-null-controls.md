# Crucibles & Null Controls

These are the things most likely to prevent `The Name of JESUS` from becoming a resonance machine.

## COMMON-NAME-001

Many persons in the Second Temple / early Roman Jewish world bear forms represented in Greek as `Iēsous`.

Question:

> Can a theory explain why one referent becomes uniquely dense without pretending the lexical string was historically unique?

## JOSHUA-001

The Septuagint calls Joshua son of Nun `Iēsous`.

Question:

> Does the theory distinguish real linguistic continuity from later typological interpretation?

## JESUS-BARABBAS-001

Textual witnesses to Matthew 27 preserve `Jesus Barabbas`.

Question:

> Can the model resolve two Jesus-referents without smuggling later sacred uniqueness into the string itself?

## SCEVA-001

Acts 19 narrates copied invocation of “the Jesus whom Paul proclaims” without the expected authority.

Question:

> Does the model distinguish reference, invocation, relation, and authorization?

Strong candidate falsifier of `NAME = PASSWORD`.

## EXODUS23-NAME-001

A messenger bears the divine name.

Question:

> What did name-bearing mean in Jewish scriptural worlds, and what does it not settle automatically?

## PHIL2-NAME-001

Competing hypotheses for “the name above every name” must generate different predictions about syntax, Isaiah intertext, `kyrios`, exaltation, and worship.

Freeze predictions before adjudicating.

## QUMRAN-GRAPHIC-NAME-001

Compare square-script texts that switch to paleo-Hebrew for the Tetragrammaton with Christian nomina sacra.

Question:

> What structural similarity survives after direct genealogy is forbidden unless demonstrated?

## NOMINA-SACRA-001

Test whether graphic contraction predicts exact theological referent consistently within one manuscript.

P46’s irregular `pneuma` behavior is a good anti-overinterpretation control.

## MAGIC-CATEGORY-001

Compare Christian invocation, Jewish exorcistic/amuletic name use, Solomon traditions, and Greco-Egyptian ritual papyri.

Do not ask first:

> Which one is magic?

Ask:

```
what operation?
what authority claim?
what named power?
what narrative valuation?
what social boundary?
what material practice?
```

The category `magic` is itself historically and polemically unstable.

## PHONETIC-ESSENCE-001

Test modern claims that efficacy/identity depends on one reconstructed pronunciation.

Controls:

* multilingual ancient transmission;
* Greek New Testament `Iēsous`;
* LXX Joshua `Iēsous`;
* later Latin and vernacular continuity;
* lack of evidence for many modern reconstructed sacred-name forms.

Question:

> What exact mechanism is proposed, and is it attested before the claim itself?

## Final null

Randomize referents while preserving string frequency.

If a model still “discovers” the same sacred topology, it is reading the analyst rather than the corpus.
