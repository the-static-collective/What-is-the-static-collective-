# Lane 7 — Spirit Molecule: DMT, 5-MeO-DMT & Cross-Kingdom State Change

**Status:** Daughter research lane / Primitive Incubator seed, 2026-08-23.\
**Parent:** _From Perturbation to Constitution — Biological & Chemical Memory_.\
**Operating law:** **No wasted inquest. No silent metaphysics. No psychedelic exceptionalism.** Molecular occurrence, demonstrated biological function, human phenomenology, cultural interpretation, and metaphysical interpretation remain separately typed.

## Working proposition

> **The strange question is not merely what DMT does to a human. It is why living systems make this molecular family at all — and what changes when the same chemical primitive crosses into a nervous system capable of constructing a self and a world.**

This lane follows three strata:

`molecule in life → molecule in nervous system → molecule in experienced world`

No stratum silently constitutes another.

## Parent research question

> **Why do DMT, 5-MeO-DMT, and related indolealkylamines recur across living systems, what ordinary biological or ecological functions do they serve there, and what does their interaction with human nervous systems reveal about rapid reorganization of self-model, world-model, agency, salience, embodiment, time, and continuity?**

The lane treats **N,N-DMT and 5-MeO-DMT as related but non-interchangeable specimens**. Their receptor profiles, ecological contexts, phenomenology, and likely endogenous functions must be tracked separately before comparison.

***

## Evidence typing

Every claim should be marked as one of:

1. **MOLECULE\_DETECTED** — compound analytically detected in an organism, tissue, secretion, or fluid.
2. **BIOSYNTHESIS\_DEMONSTRATED** — endogenous pathway or enzyme activity directly demonstrated.
3. **FUNCTION\_DEMONSTRATED** — ecological or physiological consequence supported experimentally.
4. **FUNCTION\_PROPOSED** — plausible biological role with incomplete causal evidence.
5. **PHENOMENOLOGY\_REPORTED** — reproducible or documented human/animal subjective or behavioral effect.
6. **CULTURAL\_INTERPRETATION** — historically or ethnographically documented explanatory frame.
7. **METAPHYSICAL\_INTERPRETATION** — ontological claim extending beyond presently demonstrated mechanism.
8. **UNRESOLVED** — evidence insufficient to promote or dismiss.

Core rule:

`presence ≠ purpose ≠ phenomenology ≠ ontology`

## Stratum I — Molecule in Life: Cross-Kingdom Occurrence

### Research question

Why do structurally related psychoactive indolealkylamines recur in plants, fungi, and animals, often through distinct biosynthetic machinery?

Recent work makes the cross-kingdom fact unusually concrete. Psychedelic indolethylamines are naturally produced across **Plantae, Fungi, and Animalia**, and a 2026 _Science Advances_ study reconstructed complete pathways for DMT, psilocin, psilocybin, bufotenin, and 5-MeO-DMT in a single plant system by combining enzymes originating across kingdoms.

Useful anchors:

* P. Berman et al., **“Complete biosynthesis of psychedelic tryptamines from three kingdoms in plants”**, _Science Advances_ 12 (2026), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aeb3034
* Y. Wang et al., **“Chemical ecology and convergent evolution of natural hallucinogens: From ecological defense to conserved neural targets”**, _PNAS_ 123 (2026), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2535785123
* X. Chen et al., **“A cane toad (Rhinella marina) N-methyltransferase converts primary indolethylamines to tertiary psychedelic amines”**, _Journal of Biological Chemistry_ 299 (2023), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbc.2023.105231

### Chemical-ecology hypothesis

A strong modern hypothesis is that human psychoactivity is often **secondary to the molecule's ecological role**. Natural hallucinogens may function as chemical defenses, deterrents, symbiosis-associated signals, or manipulators of herbivore/pollinator behavior. Their effects on humans may arise because many animal nervous systems share deeply conserved serotonergic and other neuromodulatory targets.

Candidate translation:

`same molecular primitive + different receiving substrate → radically different consequence`

That is immediately eCODE-bearing: consequence is not a property of the message alone.

## Stratum II — 5-MeO-DMT as an Ecological Specimen

### Sonoran Desert toad

The Sonoran Desert toad (_Incilius alvarius_) provides one of the cleanest ecological specimens. Its glandular secretion contains high concentrations of 5-MeO-DMT and functions as a **chemical defense**.

A 2025 field study compared diet and gland secretions across wild _I. alvarius_ and sympatric anurans. The toads consistently secreted high 5-MeO-DMT concentrations, while their diet did not show the specialization expected if the compound were simply acquired from food. The immediate source remains unresolved: **endogenous synthesis and microbial symbiosis remain viable explanations**.

Useful anchor:

* M. D. Luccioni et al., **“Diet and chemical defenses of the Sonoran Desert toad”**, _PLOS ONE_ 20 (2025), e0335661, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0335661

Important distinction:

`demonstrated defensive secretion ≠ demonstrated biosynthetic origin`

### Comparative toad biosynthesis

A separate 2023 study identified an indolethylamine N-methyltransferase in cane toad (_Rhinella marina_) capable of converting primary indolethylamines into tertiary psychedelic amines. Close homologs in some frogs lacked the same activity, suggesting lineage-specific specialization rather than a generic amphibian by-product.

This creates a powerful evolutionary question:

> **When is a psychoactive molecule merely chemically possible, and when has evolution built dedicated machinery around it?**

## Stratum III — Endogenous Mammalian DMT: Presence Before Purpose

### What is comparatively firm

N,N-DMT has been detected in mammals, including humans, and mammalian tissues contain enzymatic machinery capable of producing methylated tryptamines. DMT has been detected in human blood, urine, and cerebrospinal fluid, and directly in living rat brain preparations.

Useful anchors:

* S. A. Barker, E. H. McIlhenny & R. Strassman, **“A critical review of reports of endogenous psychedelic N,N-dimethyltryptamines in humans: 1955–2010”**, _Drug Testing and Analysis_ 4 (2012), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/dta.422
* S. A. Barker et al., **“LC/MS/MS analysis of the endogenous dimethyltryptamine hallucinogens…”**, _Biomedical Chromatography_ 27 (2013), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/bmc.2981
* J. Hidalgo Jiménez & J. C. Bouso, **“Significance of mammalian N,N-dimethyltryptamine (DMT): A 60-year-old debate”**, _Journal of Psychopharmacology_ 36 (2022), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/02698811221104054
* J. Schimmelpfennig & K. Jankowiak-Siuda, **“Exploring DMT: Endogenous role and therapeutic potential”**, _Neuropharmacology_ 268 (2025), 110314, DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropharm.2025.110314

### What is not settled

The normal physiological role of endogenous mammalian DMT remains unresolved.

Candidate functions under active discussion include:

* neurotransmission / neuromodulation;
* sigma-1 receptor signaling;
* intracellular 5-HT2A signaling;
* mitochondrial or cellular stress regulation;
* cytoprotection under hypoxia / oxidative stress;
* immunomodulation;
* neuroplasticity / neurogenesis;
* developmental or perinatal roles.

These are **not equally established**. Detecting INMT or receptor binding is not proof that endogenous DMT performs a particular physiological function in vivo.

A useful current synthesis from chemical ecology is that **cytoprotection and stress-response signaling may be more biologically plausible starting points than an evolved hallucinogenic function**.

### High-value state-change question

Several lines of research ask whether endogenous DMT changes under severe physiological perturbation such as hypoxia or cardiac arrest. These results deserve investigation, but they must not be silently promoted into claims that DMT causes dreams, near-death experiences, mystical experiences, birth, or death transitions in humans.

Core receipt:

`measured endogenous change → physiological association`

is not automatically:

`measured endogenous change → explanation of extraordinary conscious experience`

## Stratum IV — DMT vs 5-MeO-DMT in Human Conscious State

### Shared family, different state transitions

Both compounds can produce extremely rapid and profound alterations in consciousness, but their phenomenological profiles should not be collapsed.

**N,N-DMT** is frequently associated with:

* intense visual and spatial restructuring;
* geometric or highly structured perceptual content;
* altered embodiment;
* changes in time and agency;
* seemingly autonomous presences or entities;
* abrupt entry into an experientially coherent alternative world-model.

**5-MeO-DMT** is more often characterized by:

* reduced ordinary self/world differentiation;
* strong ego dissolution;
* nondual or unitary experience;
* comparatively less elaborate visual content;
* content-light but extremely high-intensity state change.

The comparison creates two complementary specimens:

`DMT → world-model proliferation / radical alternative organization`

`5-MeO-DMT → self/world boundary collapse / radical simplification or unity`

The distinction must be empirically refined rather than treated as folklore.

## The Entity Problem

DMT-associated reports of apparently autonomous beings or presences are scientifically interesting without deciding their ontology in advance.

Possible explanatory layers include:

* perceptual generative modeling;
* predictive processing / prior-weighting changes;
* agency-detection mechanisms;
* dissociation or self-model partitioning;
* memory reconstruction;
* culturally shaped expectations;
* recurrent architecture in human cognition;
* unknown mechanism;
* metaphysical interpretation.

Research rule:

> **Study the structure of the encounter before deciding what kind of thing was encountered.**

Candidate measures:

`autonomy | agency attribution | linguistic behavior | novelty | recurrence | controllability | memory stability | cross-participant structural similarity | expectation dependence`

## Reconstitution: What Comes Back?

The most eCODE-relevant transition may be neither onset nor peak state, but **re-entry**.

Candidate state sequence:

`stable self/world model` `→ perturbation` `→ model destabilization or collapse` `→ radically altered state` `→ reconstruction` `→ remembered / narrated receipt`

Research questions:

* What variables remain continuous when autobiographical self-modeling is severely disrupted?
* Which aspects of the altered state survive into ordinary memory?
* Does narrative reconstruction create continuity that was not phenomenologically present during the peak?
* When a participant reports “I returned,” what exactly is the identity criterion connecting pre-state and post-state?
* Can a system undergo enormous transient change without constituting a durable new state?
* When does a transient state produce lasting behavioral, affective, or interpretive consequences?

This gives a human-side sibling to **Perturbation vs Constitution**.

## Candidate eCODE primitives

### 1. Consequence is receiver-relative

`operator + substrate + local state → consequence`

The molecule is not the experience. A molecular structure interacts with a receiving system whose receptors, topology, metabolism, prior state, and scale determine the effect.

### 2. Same primitive, different scale

A molecule can plausibly participate in:

`cell survival → tissue signaling → ecological defense → nervous-system modulation → conscious world reconstruction`

without those levels being equivalent.

### 3. Perturbation / constitution separation

A radical transient state does not automatically become a durable identity or behavioral change.

`extreme experience ≠ constituted transformation`

### 4. Re-entry as a primitive

A system can leave its usual attractor, traverse a radically different state space, and return with a partial receipt rather than an exact copy of the prior state.

### 5. Boundary collapse vs world proliferation

DMT and 5-MeO-DMT may provide experimentally separable forms of state transition:

* **world proliferation / alternate organization**;
* **boundary dissolution / self-world collapse**.

These may become useful abstract operators well beyond psychedelic research.

## Research hazards

This lane needs unusually strict epistemic hygiene.

Watch for:

* assuming endogenous presence implies psychedelic-level endogenous concentrations;
* treating INMT expression as proof of DMT synthesis in a specific tissue;
* treating DMT synthesis as proof of a specific physiological function;
* projecting human psychoactivity backward as the evolutionary purpose of a plant or animal metabolite;
* conflating DMT and 5-MeO-DMT;
* assuming pineal production explains dreams, birth, mystical states, or death;
* over-interpreting animal or cell-model findings as established human physiology;
* mistaking phenomenological similarity for shared mechanism;
* mistaking subjective certainty for ontological proof;
* dismissing metaphysical interpretations solely because they are not currently experimentally adjudicated;
* conservation harm or exploitative sourcing of wild animals or culturally significant plants.

## Flagship hypotheses to test

### H1 — Ecological-first hypothesis

Human psychedelic effects are substantially an evolutionary by-product of molecules selected for other ecological or physiological functions interacting with conserved animal receptor systems.

### H2 — Stress-regulation hypothesis

Endogenous mammalian DMT participates in stress-response, cytoprotective, or neuromodulatory functions at concentrations and contexts distinct from exogenous psychedelic exposure.

### H3 — Receiver-relative operator hypothesis

The same or closely related indolealkylamine primitive produces qualitatively different consequences because receiver architecture, local state, metabolism, and scale dominate the realized effect.

### H4 — Reconstitution hypothesis

The strongest persistent consequence of a radical psychedelic state is determined less by peak phenomenological intensity than by how the post-state system reconstructs memory, identity, meaning, and behavior during re-entry.

Each remains a hypothesis until independently supported.
