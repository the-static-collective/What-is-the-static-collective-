# From Perturbation to Constitution — Biological & Chemical Memory

**Status:** Primitive Incubator / research program seed, 2026-08-23.\
**Operating law:** **No wasted inquest.** Paid or prompted scientific reasoning should leave behind attributable, reusable structure without pretending that analogy is evidence.

## Why this appears now

eCODE repeatedly encounters the same structural questions in software, provenance, memory, and collaboration:

* What counts as memory when no single record is authoritative?
* What separates perturbation from actual state change?
* How can local nodes coordinate without a master observer?
* How can lineage persist without dictating present state?
* What does a boundary do besides block or allow?

Developmental biology, systems chemistry, microbial signaling, epigenetics, and reaction-diffusion materials are already asking physical versions of these questions. This slice preserves those scientific roads as research inputs. It does **not** claim that eCODE mechanisms are biological, that biological mechanisms prove eCODE law, or that vocabulary transfer is itself validation.

## Parent research question

> **From Perturbation to Constitution:** under what physical conditions does a distributed chemical or biological system preserve history, selectively admit change, coordinate across local interactions, and recover or reorganize without central control?

The parent decomposes into five daughter lanes.

***

## Lane 1 — Pattern Memory Without Central Control

### Scientific question

How can local membrane potentials, ion channels, gap-junction topology, and downstream transcription collectively encode a tissue-scale target morphology that survives disturbance — or is persistently rewritten?

### Why it matters scientifically

Developmental bioelectricity treats tissue electrical state as more than a passive readout. Spatial voltage patterns can coordinate proliferation, differentiation, migration, and organ-scale patterning. A major frontier is decoding how tissue-scale physiological states map to stable morphological outcomes.

Relevant anchors:

* Michael Levin, **“Bioelectric signaling: Reprogrammable circuits underlying embryogenesis, regeneration, and cancer”**, _Cell_ (2021).
* Michael Levin, **“The Biophysics of Regenerative Repair Suggests New Perspectives on Biological Causation”**, _BioEssays_ (2020), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/bies.201900146
* Michael Levin, **“The Multiscale Wisdom of the Body: Collective Intelligence as a Tractable Interface for Next-Generation Biomedicine”**, _BioEssays_ (2024), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/bies.202400196
* L. Pio-Lopez, B. Hartl & M. Levin, **“Aging as a Loss of Goal-Directedness…”**, _Advanced Science_ (2025), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202509872

### eCODE-bearing question

Can memory be modeled as a stable region or attractor in distributed state space rather than as a privileged stored record?

Potential bearing:

* Haunted Memory / Ghost / Residual Presence
* Free Graph state and recurrence
* world re-entry
* constituted-state vs transient observation
* target-state recovery after perturbation

### Smallest useful research product

A comparative map of three memory forms:

1. explicit stored representation;
2. local state plus coupling topology;
3. attractor-level global pattern.

Then ask which properties survive node replacement, partial damage, altered topology, or delayed intervention.

## Lane 2 — Admission as Chemistry

### Scientific question

In autocatalytic and inhibitory reaction networks, what determines whether an input merely perturbs a system versus pushing it across a threshold into a new persistent state? How do hysteresis, bistability, feedback, pulse duration, and prior history change that boundary?

### Why it matters scientifically

Nonequilibrium chemical reaction networks can show history dependence, hysteresis, resonance, adaptation, and multiple stable regimes. That makes them a concrete laboratory for distinguishing transient influence from persistent state change.

Relevant anchors:

* D. V. Kriukov, A. H. Koyuncu & A. S. Wong, **“History Dependence in a Chemical Reaction Network Enables Dynamic Switching”**, _Small_ (2022), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/smll.202107523
* Mathieu G. Baltussen et al., **“Chemical reservoir computation in a self-organizing reaction network”**, _Nature_ 631 (2024), 549–555.
* A. H. Koyuncu et al., **“Molecular Information Processing in a Chemical Reaction Network Using Surface-Mediated Polyelectrolyte Complexation”**, _ChemSystemsChem_ (2024), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/syst.202400050
* K. Kaygisiz & R. V. Ulijn, **“Can Molecular Systems Learn?”**, _ChemSystemsChem_ (2025), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/syst.202400075

### eCODE-bearing question

Can **No Silent Promotion** be given a quantitative sibling?

Candidate physical vocabulary:

* perturbation amplitude
* residence time
* threshold crossing
* basin of attraction
* hysteresis width
* metastability
* irreversible / slowly reversible transition
* energy cost of state maintenance

This does **not** make chemical thresholds equivalent to authority. It provides a disciplined model for asking when a system’s state has materially changed rather than merely been touched.

### Smallest useful research product

A toy dynamical system with:

`input → transient excursion → return`

versus

`input → barrier crossing → persistent new state`

and a receipt that records the difference.

## Lane 3 — Collective Sensing Without a Master Observer

### Scientific question

When does a population of individually noisy cells produce a better estimate of its environment through local signaling? When does heterogeneity increase robustness, and when does it fragment decision-making?

### Why it matters scientifically

Quorum sensing is increasingly modeled not only as population-density measurement but as collective environmental sensing. Cell populations can pool imperfect local information and coordinate group-level responses while retaining single-cell heterogeneity.

Relevant anchors:

* Stefany Moreno-Gámez, M. Hochberg & G. S. van Doorn, **“Quorum sensing as a mechanism to harness the wisdom of the crowds”**, _Nature Communications_ (2022/2023 publication record).
* Priyanikha Jayakumar et al., **“Collective decision-making in Pseudomonas aeruginosa involves transient segregation of quorum-sensing activities across cells”**, _Current Biology_ (2022).
* Ximing Li et al., **“Synthetic neural-like computing in microbial consortia for pattern recognition”**, _Nature Communications_ (2020).

### eCODE-bearing question

How can a whole improve its state estimate without installing a single privileged observer?

Potential bearing:

* receiver/transmitter as temporary node capabilities rather than fixed roles
* shared witness channels
* Free Graph aggregation without graph authority
* collective learning without a central learner
* disagreement and heterogeneity as possible signal rather than automatic error

### Smallest useful research product

Compare three aggregation regimes:

1. independent local estimates;
2. naive averaging;
3. local signaling with heterogeneous confidence / commitment.

Measure accuracy, robustness to noisy nodes, and failure modes under correlated error.

***

## Lane 4 — Lineage Memory Without Present Determinism

### Scientific question

Which molecular and epigenetic mechanisms preserve information about cellular history or ancestry while still permitting later plasticity, reprogramming, and fate change?

### Why it matters scientifically

Cell identity is not reducible to DNA sequence alone. Chromatin state, transcriptional feedback, RNA regulation, metabolic state, and trained or persistent cellular responses can retain history while remaining at least partly reversible.

Useful research neighborhoods:

* epigenetic memory and chromatin state inheritance
* transcriptional feedback and bistability
* trained immunity
* lineage tracing versus cell-state inference
* reprogramming and fate plasticity
* hysteresis in developmental gene-regulatory networks

### eCODE-bearing question

What information may legitimately travel from ancestry into present state without becoming present authority?

Potential bearing:

* Typed Continuity Braid
* descent / recurrence / answer / mutation edges in Free Graph
* Exact Return vs lawful re-entry
* artifact → ghost → later artifact
* source ancestry vs local adoption

### Smallest useful research product

Build a table separating:

`ancestry evidence | current molecular state | present functional competence | future reachable states`

Then explicitly test cases where ancestry predicts poorly but present state predicts well, and vice versa.

## Lane 5 — Executable Membranes in Matter

### Scientific question

Can semipermeable compartments coupled by reaction-diffusion fronts selectively transmit signals, filter noise, gate downstream reactions, and produce larger spatial patterns without centralized orchestration?

### Why it matters scientifically

Compartmentalized reaction networks and responsive materials already support chemical communication, threshold sensing, bistability, oscillations, gating, pattern formation, and fuel-driven fronts.

Relevant anchors:

* I. Maity et al., **“Feedback and Communication in Active Hydrogel Spheres with pH Fronts”**, _Angewandte Chemie International Edition_ (2021), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202109735
* P. J. de Jong, F. Trigka & M. M. Lerch, **“Towards Autonomous Materials—Challenges in Chemical Communication”**, _ChemSystemsChem_ (2024), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/syst.202400005
* V. A. Baulin et al., **“Material-Based Intelligence: Autonomous Adaptation and Embodied Computation in Physical Substrates”**, _Advanced Intelligent Systems_ (2026), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/aisy.202501450

### eCODE-bearing question

Can a boundary be understood as an active transform rather than a binary permission wall?

Candidate sequence:

`signal approaches → boundary conditions transform / filter → local state interprets → downstream consequence`

Potential bearing:

* Executable Membrane
* Declared Witness Channels
* Crossing Discipline
* Room / world boundaries
* foreign encounter vs local adoption

### Smallest useful research product

Model two communicating compartments where identical incoming signals produce different downstream effects because each compartment has different local state, permeability, or reaction history.

## The shared research packet

Any future chemistry / biology inquest that lands near one of these lanes should leave behind the smallest reusable packet possible:

1. **Scientific question** — stated without eCODE vocabulary.
2. **Mechanism / variables** — what is actually measured or modeled.
3. **Evidence map** — supporting, conflicting, and unresolved findings.
4. **Competing explanations** — not just the favored analogy.
5. **One falsifiable hypothesis or toy model.**
6. **eCODE bearing** — explicitly marked as inference / architectural translation.
7. **What does not transfer** — where the analogy breaks.
8. **Source receipt** — paper / DOI / dataset / date.

That separation is the protection against cargo-cult science.

## Candidate flagship synthesis

> **What remembers? → What admits change? → How does a collective know? → What does lineage carry? → What does a boundary do?**

A longer research program could eventually become:

1. **Pattern Memory Without Central Control** — developmental bioelectricity;
2. **Admission as Chemistry** — nonequilibrium reaction networks and hysteresis;
3. **Collective Sensing Without a Master Observer** — quorum sensing and microbial coordination;
4. **Lineage Memory Without Present Determinism** — epigenetic and cell-state inheritance;
5. **Executable Membranes in Matter** — reaction-diffusion compartments and autonomous materials.

The unifying question is not whether biology “proves eCODE.”

The useful question is whether natural and synthetic physical systems reveal **design constraints that eCODE would otherwise have to rediscover by accident**.

## Graduation criterion

This slice graduates only when at least one lane produces a model or experiment that changes an eCODE design decision in a specific, inspectable way.

Until then:

**research evidence stays research evidence; architectural inference stays architectural inference; no analogy silently promotes itself into law.**
