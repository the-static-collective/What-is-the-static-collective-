# Research Packet F — Narrative, Embodied & Symbolic Worlds

This packet preserves symbolic, theological, narrative, embodied, historical, and world-building research without silently collapsing their evidence classes.

Keep these labels explicit where relevant:

```
textual observation
historical evidence

creative hypothesis
```

Analogy may generate research. It does not manufacture empirical or runtime authority.
