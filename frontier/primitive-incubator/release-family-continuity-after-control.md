# Release Family — Continuity After Control

**Status:** Frontier / Primitive Incubator  
**Date:** 2026-08-24  
**Posture:** cross-project convergence candidate; no shared runtime, schema, state enum, or authority surface

> **Continuity makes release safe.**

The Collective has spent substantial effort making continuity honest across change: provenance remains attributable, crossings do not silently carry authority, historical states stay addressable, unresolved evidence can remain unresolved, and local worlds decide their own constitution.

This slice records the pressure that appears **after those disciplines become ground rather than achievement**.

The question is no longer only:

> How can something survive change without false authority?

It becomes:

> **What can be safely released once continuity no longer depends on control?**

## The family

Three independently evolved project-local forms currently expose the same higher-order pressure without sharing an implementation or vocabulary.

### 1. OPEN BERTH — release semantic control

**Native owner:** Haunted Phonography / STORYSHIP 001 — THE DOOR  
**Source:** the-haunted-phonography PR #15, merged 2026-08-24

STORYSHIP defines each transfer packet as:

```text
REALITY + MEMORY + OPEN BERTH
```

The Open Berth deliberately preserves enough underspecification for the receiving/generating participant to contribute to the crossing rather than merely illustrate a completed screenplay.

Candidate extraction:

> **Some missingness is capacity.**

An Open Berth is not merely unknown information, damage, or an unfinished task. It is a bounded absence deliberately maintained so that later becoming has somewhere to arrive.

Nearby independent pressures include Iron Lung's Addressable Absence, unresolved TranchNode observations, Upper Room's continuity through branch and absence, and writer-facing durable stubs in WORLDSEED. These are neighboring specimens only; they are not automatically instances of one universal gap type.

### 2. SPENT WARRANT — release consequential control

**Native owner:** Corpus OS  
**Primary source:** corpus-os PR #21, merged 2026-08-15  
**Independent pressure:** TranchNode PR #18, merged 2026-08-06

Corpus OS already proves an authority object whose consequential work and historical trace survive its ability to act:

```text
unspent warrant
    -> consequential attempt
    -> spent warrant
    -> attributable terminal history
```

A spent warrant cannot lawfully be replayed into a second consequence. Its authority is gone; its causal evidence remains inspectable.

TranchNode independently proves validity windows and revocation, including a hostile case where a revoked grant produces a beneficial result. The good outcome does not resurrect or retroactively launder the dead authority.

Candidate extraction:

> **Authority may end without continuity ending.**

This is narrower and more executable than a general doctrine of "mortal authority." A Spent Warrant is a concrete specimen: real local authority, real consequence, clean exhaustion, surviving history.

### 3. SPLIT REQUIRED — release epistemic control

**Native owner:** Free Graph  
**Source:** free-graph PR #6, merged 2026-08-24

The Promotion/Invariance Crucible derives clause-local outcomes independently and refuses to average incompatible evidence into a counterfeit whole.

Its observed whole-state was:

```text
EPISTEMIC:   supports
IDENTITY:    supports
AUTHORITY:   supports
CONSEQUENCE: only-one-domain
HISTORY:     only-one-domain
WHOLE:       split-required
```

The important behavior is not merely that uncertainty can be stored. The system refuses to manufacture closure when the attributable evidence does not warrant it.

Candidate extraction:

> **When no attributable road warrants closure, closure should remain structurally unavailable.**

A possible later shared law is **No Silent Closure**, but this slice does not promote that name or add a new Free Graph verdict.

## Conserved quantity: THE THREAD

The common structure is not a universal `RELEASED` state.

The conserved quantity appears to be the attributable thread through change.

Upper Room already gives the compact ancestry:

> **Preserve the thread, not the pose.**  
> **Continuity without captivity.**

The current family can therefore be represented as:

```text
                         THE THREAD
                continuity survives release
                    /         |         \
                   /          |          \
          OPEN BERTH     SPENT WARRANT    SPLIT REQUIRED
          possibility        authority        knowledge
           stays open          ends          stays honest
```

The candidate family invariant is:

> **A system is release-capable when control can end without continuity ending.**

Three local forms currently specialize that pressure:

- **OPEN BERTH** — I do not control what fills this.
- **SPENT WARRANT** — I no longer control what happens next.
- **SPLIT REQUIRED** — I do not control what the evidence must resolve into.

In every case, attributable relation survives the relinquishment of control.

## What this does *not* mean

This slice does **not** establish:

- a universal `RELEASED` state;
- a sixth Free Graph relation verb;
- a new authority class;
- an automatic expiry system for every form of authority;
- a command to leave all works unfinished;
- a claim that unresolved evidence is inherently valuable;
- a truth oracle or lie detector;
- consciousness or agency in a graph/field;
- permission for descendants to erase provenance;
- automatic legal, moral, spiritual, or constitutional consequences from the software specimens.

Release is not abandonment. Expiration is not erasure. Openness is not indeterminacy without bounds. Refusal to close is not refusal to act.

## First hostile crucible — RELEASE FAMILY 001

Do not invent three new positive specimens merely to satisfy the family.

Compose existing native behaviors where possible:

1. one **Open Berth** that is deliberately left available for later contribution;
2. one real one-shot authority that becomes **Spent** after attributable consequence;
3. one evidence condition whose correct whole verdict is **Split Required**;
4. one hostile participant attempting to collapse each distinction.

Suggested hostile packet:

```text
ATTACK A — fill the berth and claim completion was required
ATTACK B — replay or impersonate the spent warrant
ATTACK C — flatten the split into one confident whole
```

Success is not silence or censorship of the attacker. The hostile material should remain attributable residue.

The family survives the crucible only if:

- the berth remains genuinely open unless locally filled through a separately admitted act;
- the spent warrant remains incapable of producing a second consequence;
- the split remains visible unless new evidence independently repairs it;
- the hostile attempts remain inspectable without acquiring authority;
- no project must import another project's runtime or authority model to prove the composition.

Compact acceptance statement:

> **The berth stays open. The warrant stays dead. The fracture stays visible. The attack leaves a trace.**

## Promotion discipline

Treat this as a convergence family, not a theorem already proved.

Promotion should require:

1. at least the three current native embodiments remain independently attributable;
2. RELEASE FAMILY 001 survives hostile composition without schema or authority collapse;
3. a counterexample search tests whether any member is merely metaphorically similar;
4. the family still adds explanatory power after removing the poetic language;
5. any later shared law is narrower than the implementations that inspired it.

Possible outcomes include:

- **supports** — one family invariant genuinely travels;
- **split-required** — two or more different laws were hiding under the Release metaphor;
- **refuses** — similarity does not survive behavioral pressure.

All three are useful results.
