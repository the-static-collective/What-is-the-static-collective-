# Refusal Topology — Influence Without Authority / Multiverse NAV

**Status:** incubating hypothesis — not roadmap law, not implementation authority

## Phase-transition observation

> A constitution that can be cited is different from a constitution that can be failed.

Project0 Floor 1.1 creates the first cold, model-independent surface in the Collective that can refuse an attempted action with stable reason codes and attributable evidence. The interesting possibility is that repeated lawful refusals need not remain mere errors or logs. They may expose the boundary-shape of the constitution itself.

This page preserves that possibility without declaring the projection law solved.

## Candidate primitive: Refusal Topology

**Primitive:**

A durable, non-authoritative geometry derived from accountable attempted transitions that struck a constitutional boundary and did not become constituted state.

```
constituted world
= what became reachable through accountable history

constitutional negative space
= what was attempted,
  encountered the floor,
  and remained unreachable
  with attributable witness
```

A refusal boundary is not automatically a new canonical fact about the world. It is evidence about an encounter between a declared attempt and a declared constitutional cut.

Refused and indeterminate encounters may therefore become navigable boundary witnesses without being upgraded into successful state transitions.

## Complement to Lawful Reachability

**Lawful Reachability / Constituted Reality** asks:

> Given accountable history, what is allowed to count as the present world?

Refusal Topology asks the complementary question:

> Given accountable attempted history, which neighboring transitions failed to constitute a world, and why?

```
reachable state
     |
     +---- lawful path ------> new reachable state
     |
     +---- REFUSED ----------X
     |                        |
     |                        + durable boundary witness
     |
     +---- INDETERMINATE ----?
                              |
                              + unresolved frontier
```

The `X` and `?` are not automatically law. They are attributable traces of where a declared encounter met a boundary.

## Candidate law: Influence Without Authority

A residue may lawfully influence later attention, navigation, composition, search, or field construction without acquiring authority over the destination.

A useful target shape is:

```
admitted presence: yes
epistemic disposition: disputed / rejected / unresolved
authority: none
historical presence: durable
future influence: bounded and attributable
```

This is the mechanical version of several already-incubating Collective claims:

* a ghost may preserve prior presence but cannot counterfeit current authority;
* illumination may persist without silently transferring authority;
* rejected or unresolved proposals may remain queryable;
* recognition may affect later fields without rewriting earlier state.

**Invariant:** influence is not authority.

Visibility, recurrence, emotional salience, resonance, similarity, atmospheric pressure, and traversal frequency must never silently mint authority.

## Residual atmosphere

A residual atmosphere is a projection over admitted residues, refusals, tensions, unresolved edges, recognitions, and other non-sovereign traces.

It may change what becomes visible or inviting next. It must not:

* rewrite the constituted world;
* grant capabilities or leases;
* convert popularity into authority;
* erase contrary residues;
* smuggle authority from the source frame into a destination frame;
* claim truth merely because a trace is durable or frequently encountered.

The atmosphere may shape movement. It may not become a hidden ruler.

## Instrument hypothesis: Multiverse NAV

**Multiverse NAV is not yet a primitive or implementation plan.** It is an instrument hypothesis that becomes imaginable if Refusal Topology and Influence Without Authority prove mechanically tight.

A NAV layer would navigate among multiple coherent frames without requiring one frame to become the master map.

A frame might contain:

* one accountable constitutional cut;
* its currently reachable states;
* refusal boundaries visible from that cut;
* indeterminate frontiers;
* a bounded residual atmosphere;
* explicit lineage sufficient for lawful re-entry.

A lawful NAV instrument would need to preserve frame boundaries during traversal. Crossing into another frame cannot silently carry authority with it.

Potential traversal grammar:

```
frame A
  -> declared crossing
  -> preserve source-frame lineage
  -> carry bounded witness / influence packet
  -> re-evaluate under destination authority
  -> frame B projection
```

The goal would not be to discover the one best world. The goal would be to keep plural lawful and residual frames traversable without capture.

### Project-backed specimen — Project0 NAV v0.1 Crossing Witness

**Status:** experimental implementation / project evidence — not universal roadmap law and not authority over other frames.

Project0 PR #34 provides the first executable NAV specimen under review.

**What it proves:** bounded, explicitly declared `before` / `crossing` / `after` frames can be compared deterministically across frame, constitution, authority, decoder, evidence, participant, and particularity dimensions without manufacturing a `same_world` verdict. Exact input declarations and the resulting crossing witness are content-addressed under an experimental NAV domain while reusing Project0's canonicalization floor.

**What it does not prove:** minimum necessary world-change, global route search, alternate-authority/evidence recommendations, universal impossibility from a local refusal, or a master multiverse graph.

**Authority boundary:** NAV v0.1 compares declared authority references but cannot grant, synthesize, or transfer authority. Refusal evidence remains frame-relative. Visibility and residue remain influence, not authority.

**Executable evidence:** [Project0 PR #34](https://github.com/the-static-collective/project0/pull/34), head `2167aab95bb5b5e0714560d75555dd17165fe000` at the time this witness was recorded. `npm run verify:all` passes with 119/119 Node tests plus canonical fixture and conformance gates.

This page remains an incubating hypothesis. The executable specimen is project-backed evidence, not automatic promotion of Multiverse NAV into constitutional law.

## Re-entry hypothesis

This connects to the existing continuity principle that the original room may vanish while enough relational topology survives for lawful reconstitution.

Re-entry must remain distinguishable from occurrence or exact historical return.

A prior frame may be reconstituted only from attributable surviving topology, declared decoder/environment context, and the authority appropriate to the re-entry act. Residual atmosphere may inform the projection but cannot impersonate missing authoritative state.

## Promotion as accountable act

This hypothesis also sharpens the unresolved promotion question in the Front Room.

Promotion should not reduce to frequency or familiarity.

A candidate graduation may eventually need attributable answers to:

* What encounter occurred?
* What evidence survived?
* What lineage supports the candidate?
* What was refused or left unresolved?
* Did materially different specimens reproduce the law?
* What explicit receipt or authority admits promotion?

One strong, well-lineaged specimen may outweigh repeated soft recurrence. Repetition alone must not manufacture canon.

## What must stay invariant

1. **Refusal does not mutate constituted state.**
2. **A refusal witness is not automatically authoritative.**
3. **Indeterminate is not silently coerced into refused or admitted.**
4. **Influence never implies authority.**
5. **Cross-frame traversal re-evaluates authority at the destination.**
6. **Residual atmosphere cannot rewrite source history.**
7. **Re-entry is attributable reconstruction, not impersonation of occurrence.**
8. **Popularity, recurrence, similarity, and navigation frequency cannot promote themselves.**
9. **Plural frames may remain unreconciled.**
10. **A NAV layer must remain a projection/instrument, never the master constitutional graph.**

## Smallest experiment

Do **not** build Multiverse NAV.

First prove one much smaller thing:

> A refused attempted transition can leave a deterministic, attributable, queryable witness while changing neither constituted state nor authority.

Minimum fixture shape:

```
known state S
  + declared attempt A
  + evaluated constitutional cut C
      -> REFUSED(reason, evidence)
      -> durable refusal witness R

assert:
  S is unchanged
  authority is unchanged
  R is queryable
  R preserves A + C + reason lineage
  replay is deterministic / idempotent
  R cannot itself authorize A
```

A corresponding indeterminate fixture should remain distinct.

This experiment belongs near Project0's addressed-receipt / append-only graph conformance work unless implementation reveals that a versioned extension profile is necessary.

## Failure conditions

The hypothesis fails or must be redesigned if proving it requires any of the following:

* refused attempts mutating the canonical world merely so they can be remembered;
* a refusal receipt acquiring the authority of the constitution that produced it;
* one global NAV graph becoming authoritative over all frames;
* residual pressure silently ranking itself into truth or canon;
* cross-frame navigation bypassing destination disclosure or authority checks;
* reconstruction being presented as original occurrence;
* inventing a second canonicalizer or parallel identity floor.

## What would make this graduate

Refusal Topology should remain incubating until at least:

1. one executable Project0 fixture proves durable refusal witness without state mutation or authority transfer;
2. one materially different downstream organ uses that witness constructively rather than merely logging an error;
3. an indeterminate frontier remains visible without forced resolution;
4. influence from a residue can demonstrably affect a projection while remaining incapable of authorizing it;
5. cross-frame or re-entry behavior is tested without creating a master narrator or master map.

Multiverse NAV should remain an instrument hypothesis even longer. It graduates only if multiple real frames become meaningfully easier to traverse while their sovereignty, refusal boundaries, and unresolved remainder remain intact.

## Design sentence

> Refusal Topology is the accountable shape of worlds that did not become reachable; Influence Without Authority lets their residues matter without becoming kings; Multiverse NAV is the possible instrument for traversing that plurality without capture.
