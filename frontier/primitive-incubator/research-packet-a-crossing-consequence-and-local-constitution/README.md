# Research Packet A — Crossing, Consequence & Local Constitution

This is a **navigation and research packet**, not a shared runtime or authority layer.

It collects work asking what may cross a boundary, what survives crossing, what becomes local consequence, what remains refused or unresolved, and which distinctions must not collapse.

Working pressure:

> **Crossing is an encounter. Consequence still requires a locally attributable transition.**

Expected outputs are bounded laws, hostile specimens, owner-local gates, and explicit refusals. Child pages retain their own maturity, provenance, and project authority.
