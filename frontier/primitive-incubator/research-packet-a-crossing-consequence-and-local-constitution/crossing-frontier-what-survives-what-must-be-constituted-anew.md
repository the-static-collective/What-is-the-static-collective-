# Crossing Frontier — What Survives, What Must Be Constituted Anew

> **What exactly survives a crossing, and what must be constituted anew?**

This is a durable frontier question, not a promoted universal law.

Several project-owned specimens now touch the same seam from different directions. The purpose of this page is to keep those doors near one another without collapsing their native semantics.

## The distinctions to preserve

### Horizon

A horizon records the boundary of a witness:

```
what this traversal exhausted
≠
what reality contains
```

A candidate Horizon Receipt carries only enough to say where observation stopped:

```
surface identity
+ traversal policy/version
+ last stable witness
+ stop reason
+ completeness_claim:false
```

The Autodiscography Vault's `ui_exhausted` state is the strongest current executable specimen: the witnessed UI may stop yielding population evidence without proving provider completeness.

### Open relation

An open crossing is not missing evidence about whether anything happened. It is positive evidence that something began and remains unresolved:

```
begun
+ attributable
+ unresolved
+ no inherited destination consequence
```

Keep this separate from horizon:

```
HORIZON
= what this witness can no longer establish

OPEN CROSSING
= what this witness establishes as begun but not lawfully closed
```

### Constitution / birth

Haunted Toaster's topology-event authority carrier supplies a concrete local law:

```
constitution mints warrant
identity-preserving transport carries warrant
derivation cannot counterfeit ancestry
new constitution requires new warrant
```

Human shorthand:

> **Authority wants a birth certificate, not a memory.**

The transferable question is not whether every project has “birth.” It is where a new attributable identity or authority is actually constituted.

### Carry

Some facts may survive movement without being recreated:

* attributable lineage;
* exact carrier identity where identity is preserved;
* receipts and bounded historical residue;
* structural pressure where the owner proves it survives translation.

Other facts must not silently cross:

* destination consequence;
* local admission;
* execution authority;
* newly constituted identity;
* global completeness claims.

### Translation / native embodiment

Native Anatomy asks whether one structure can remain recognizable without forcing identical form:

```
same structure
≠ same geometry

same structure
≈ recognizable structural pressure
```

The next strong control is a nonvisual embodiment of one pressure such as `branches`. If the shared law reduces only to vocabulary, refuse promotion.

### Opportunity × sensitivity

WALK E suggests a creature model where the world exposes opportunities and a local body has a deterministic susceptibility profile:

```
WORLD declares opportunity
CREATURE declares susceptibility
ENCOUNTER constitutes event
```

The opportunity must not be invented by the creature merely because the creature responds to it.

### Local consequence

Crossing does not make consequence universal. Destination admission, execution, refusal, loss, timeout, or other effects remain owner-local until witnessed.

## First crucibles

### HORIZON-RECEIPT-001

Use two materially different owners:

1. Vault Census `ui_exhausted`;
2. one National Treasure source hunt ending at an explicit acquisition horizon such as `UNAVAILABLE` or `NOT YET DIGITIZED`.

Promote only if the shared receipt remains useful without importing owner-specific semantics.

### BIRTH-CARRIER-001

Pass one lawful object through:

```
identity-preserving wrapper
→ identity-preserving wrapper
→ genuine derivation
```

Carrier identity must remain exact through the first two and change at the derivation boundary. Equivalent payload without ancestry must fail.

### OPEN-CROSSING foreign specimen

Feed a Vault observation/apply round into the existing Open Crossing harness without teaching the harness Vault vocabulary.

### Native Anatomy foreign specimen

Render one pressure such as `branches` in a nonvisual domain, then test whether the invariant survives without collapsing into a shared word.

### Opportunity/Sensitivity specimen

Freeze one opportunity field. Run two deterministic sensitivity profiles. They may take or ignore lawful opportunities but may not manufacture new opportunities.

## Deferred lane — Corpus Weather

Corpus Weather is promising only after preservation is secure:

> **weather chooses where to look; weather does not decide what is there.**

Metadata-only pressure may guide inspection while retaining:

```
authority:none
interpretation:none
```

If density becomes importance, drift becomes era classification, or metadata becomes semantic judgment, stop.

## Crucible questions for future crossings

When a new specimen enters this frontier, ask:

1. What is positively witnessed before the crossing?
2. What may cross unchanged?
3. What may cross only as attributable lineage or residue?
4. What authority, identity, or consequence must be constituted locally?
5. What would counterfeit continuity look like?
6. What would counterfeit rebirth look like?
7. What horizon prevents a local stop from becoming a world claim?

## Stop condition

If this frontier collapses into generic `state`, `pending`, `identity`, `branch`, or `receipt` vocabulary without preserving falsifiable distinctions, keep the question and refuse the abstraction.

The question is allowed to remain larger than the current answers.
