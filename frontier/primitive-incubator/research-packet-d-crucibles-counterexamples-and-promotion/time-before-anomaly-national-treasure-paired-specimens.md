## Why these belong together without becoming one case

Two Deep Research runs on 2026-08-20 produced neighboring but importantly different specimens.

The first accidentally demonstrates a **research-method failure**: an underspecified request about time caused the research process to invent an unnamed anomalous artifact, then surround it with plausible provenance, incidents, chronology, and real neighboring science.

The second begins with no artifact and asks the substantive question: **what does serious physics, philosophy, and experiment actually establish or leave unresolved about time itself?**

The National Treasure slice preserves both while refusing to let either borrow evidentiary weight from the other.

## Specimen A — The Artifact That Wasn't There

**Class:** Failed / Unsupported as artifact history; useful as a method specimen.

The generated report eventually acknowledged that many of its dates and incidents were hypothetical placeholders. The important finding is therefore not a temporal anomaly. It is a provenance warning:

> **A plausible evidence-shaped narrative is not provenance.**

Useful controls survived the failure: establish the source object first, reconstruct custody before mechanism, use redundant synchronized clocks, preserve raw logs, distinguish witness testimony from instrument records, blind expectation-sensitive tests, and require repeatability before escalating toward exotic physics.

Repository target after landing: `cases/time-artifact-that-wasnt-there/`

## Specimen B — The Objectively Strange Nature of Time

**Class:** Open research frontier / v0.1.

The first pass surfaced a real convergence before any paranormal claim enters the room:

* relativity does not provide a single observer-independent global NOW;
* elapsed proper time is worldline/gravity dependent;
* the macroscopic arrow of time still requires explanation against more time-symmetric microscopic laws;
* quantum gravity contains a genuine problem of time and active relational/emergent-time programs;
* subjective temporal experience is experimentally deformable and therefore cannot simply be assumed identical to fundamental physical time.

That is enough to justify the inquiry, but **not** enough to close it.

The current research debt remains explicit: primary-source treatment of time-dilation experiments, Loschmidt/recurrence/low-entropy initial conditions, CPT and observed T violation, Wheeler–DeWitt, Page–Wootters, delayed choice and quantum eraser, serious retrocausal interpretations, Gödel/CTCs/wormholes/chronology protection, indefinite causal order and the quantum switch, Leggett–Garg/temporal Bell work, time crystals, and experiments that could actually discriminate interpretations.

Repository target after landing: `cases/strange-nature-of-time/`

## Frontier relation

This pair is useful to the Collective only under a strict boundary:

```
established temporal strangeness
        ≠
license to invent anomalies
        ≠
proof of one preferred ontology
        ≠
portable authority for eCODEsystem architecture
```

The portable research lesson is narrower:

> **Time is already strange in established inquiry; an invented anomaly is not required to make it strange.**

And the methodological complement is equally important:

> **The procedure used to find a mystery must not be allowed to manufacture its source object.**

## Promotion gate

Do not promote a broader “law of time” from this page.

Promotion would require at minimum:

1. the National Treasure source PR to land;
2. the substantive time case to receive the missing primary-source families;
3. claims to be separated into experimentally established, mathematically admitted, interpretive, frontier, and unsupported tiers;
4. any eCODEsystem application to re-prove its own local invariant rather than citing physics as metaphorical authority.
