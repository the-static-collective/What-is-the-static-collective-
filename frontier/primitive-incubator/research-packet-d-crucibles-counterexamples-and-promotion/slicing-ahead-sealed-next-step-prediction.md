## Why this is not recurrence counting

The relevant coherence is not agreement by repetition.

The field has spent increasing effort preserving distinctions such as:

```
similarity != genealogy
agreement != independent corroboration
recurrence != independent birth
history != authority
attention != support
re-entry != exact return
```

A useful self-pointer therefore cannot be inferred from raw phrase frequency or one dependency family echoing itself.

The stronger signal would be materially different native systems independently narrowing the same class of future transition while preserving their own anatomy.

## `NEXT-STEP-HOLDOUT-001`

The stronger discriminator is a historical holdout test.

For several earlier Collective cuts:

1. hide all work created afterward;
2. derive one to three unresolved directional pressures from the frozen past only;
3. require structural predictions rather than project names or keywords;
4. reveal the actual later work;
5. compare against chronology-shuffled, keyword-only, single-lineage, recurrence-only, and plausible-random nulls;
6. remove one supposedly load-bearing source family and rerun.

Candidate success condition:

> the true chronological corpus predicts the **class of later transformation** materially better than the nulls after dependency ancestry is accounted for.

Candidate failure condition:

> the same apparent predictive power can be reproduced by shared vocabulary, retrospective interpretation, one correlated lineage, or ordinary plausible guessing.

If the null wins, H0 should be refused rather than cosmetically narrowed again.

## Why Autodiscography is the strongest first world

The Autodiscography is a useful pressure-test because it is simultaneously ancestral, enormous, repetitive, heterogeneous, and still alive.

It contains enough recurrence to create false positives and enough particularity to punish flattening. A lawful re-entry system should not declare what the archive means. It should expose why an old artifact is near now through typed, source-visible relations and allow the human to choose, ignore, or refuse the crossing.

That makes the first plausible re-entry witness much smaller than a global memory system:

```
old artifact
  ↓
why is this near?
  ├── semantic resemblance
  ├── explicit lineage
  ├── shared unresolved tension
  ├── human-declared link
  └── rejected parallel
  ↓
human chooses or ignores
  ↓
local consequence, if any
  ↓
receipt
```
