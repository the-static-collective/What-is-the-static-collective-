# Weather, Residue & Causal Explanation — Jubilee Convergence Cluster

## Status

**Incubating.** This page records a cross-ecosystem convergence discovered after jublEchat's Nearby Growth v0 landed. It does not create a shared schema or portable package.

The interesting development is that several projects now independently express pieces of a law that Jubilee originally carried mostly as product philosophy:

```
provisional influence
  != witnessed state

current projection
  != append-only history

refused / abandoned history
  != nothing

causal explanation
  != authority
```

The useful next move is not centralization. It is to let the independent specimens remain themselves while giving jublEchat three bounded doors where the pattern can be felt by a human.

***

## Door A — Weather → Proposal → Witness

### Primitive

A low-authority field may shape attention and proposal space without becoming durable constitutional state merely because it occurred.

```
conversation weather
  -> optional proposal candidate
  -> explicit human cultivation
  -> witnessed event
  -> current projection
```

### Jubilee specimen

Current jublEchat keeps ordinary chat history in browser-local conversation state while durable ideas, versions, events, and lineage live behind separate hooks and authority paths. Model-generated proposal cards are visible in chat before a human chooses cultivation.

Project issue:

* https://github.com/the-static-collective/jublEchat/issues/6

### Independent analogues

* **Upper Room Dust / Attention Weather** — coarse shared attention weather carries `authority: none`, decays, and cannot self-amplify through its own reflexes.
* **Project0 L-Branch v0.1** — influence may change candidate eligibility while the authority set stays unchanged.
* **TranchNode Continuity Spine v0.1** — future attractors remain proposal-only until witnessed transfer.

### What must stay invariant

Weather is not a hidden write path. A model response cannot create a current `IdeaVersion`. Ordinary conversation may matter without being automatically preserved as constitutional history.

### Graduation evidence

A second materially different human-facing product would need to prove the same separation before considering a portable Weather → Witness contract.

***

## Door B — Residual Lineage

### Primitive

Rejected, refused, indeterminate, and deliberately abandoned history may remain attributable and available to later attention without acquiring the standing of the effect that failed or was declined.

> **What was refused may remain available to attention. It does not become authority, current state, or evidence that the refused effect occurred.**

### Jubilee specimen

Nearby Growth currently has a deliberately positive evidence law: active neighbors qualify only through exact direct relation, shared ancestry, or shared friction. Residual history therefore needs its own projection rather than being smuggled into the active-neighbor list.

Implementation issue:

* https://github.com/the-static-collective/jublEchat/issues/5

Design / plan PR:

* https://github.com/the-static-collective/jublEchat/pull/8

The selected v0 slice is read-only: exact rejected proposals and explicit path-abandonment dispositions become queryable as `authority: none`. No AI prompt injection, resurrection command, semantic ranking, or schema change belongs in the first proof.

### Independent analogues

* **Full Measure Collision Specimen 001** — refused and indeterminate history can produce deterministic read-only influence without changing constituted state or authority.
* **Refusal Topology** — attempted transitions that struck a constitutional boundary remain navigable boundary witness rather than successful state.
* **Project0 Snap-State v0.1** — active topology may recoil while append-only event history does not recoil.

### What must stay invariant

```
Held Proposal Seeds  -> not yet decided
Nearby Growth        -> active positively evidenced neighbors
Residual Lineage     -> rejected / abandoned history
```

A former version is not abandoned merely because it is old. Missing acceptance is not rejection. Presence is not support.

### Graduation evidence

After the read model exists, a separate specimen may test **human-selected residual influence**: a residue may seed a new proposal while remaining mechanically incapable of advancing current state directly.

## Door C — Why Current? / Still Alive

### Primitive

A human-facing read model can explain why the present projection is present while separately showing what the transition refused to flatten.

```
Why Current?
  -> smallest attributable causal path to present form

Still Alive
  -> explicit tensions / questions / dispositions that survived

Nearby Growth
  -> current lawful adjacency

Residual Lineage
  -> historical refused / abandoned paths
```

### Jubilee specimen

jublEchat already carries strict ancestry helpers and first-class tension/question/path fields. The next architectural step is to extract those explanations from the large UI component into pure, fail-closed read models.

Project issue:

* https://github.com/the-static-collective/jublEchat/issues/7

### Independent analogues

Several projects now need a neighboring human question without yet sharing a contract:

* Corpus OS: why is this ref in the constituted `WorldCut`?
* TranchNode Continuity Spine: why is this stage lawfully operative?
* Full Measure: why does the inhabited Garden currently look this way?
* Project0 Snap-State: why is this topology active now, and what happened before recoil?

### What must stay invariant

Explanation is a projection. It cannot manufacture causality from prose similarity, timestamps, or nearby records. Missing or contradictory ancestry must fail closed rather than produce a polished story.

Still Alive must use explicit witnessed friction/disposition fields. It must not regenerate the Beautiful Summary Trap by inferring tension from punctuation or model summaries.

### Graduation evidence

Do not create a universal `WhyCurrent` type yet. Wait for one materially different second implementation whose own domain law can map cleanly without lying.

***

## Cross-cutting observation — reversible projection / irreversible witnessed history

Jubilee's current pointer and Project0 Snap-State's active topology now form two materially different specimens of a smaller recurring distinction:

```
present projection may change or recoil
history does not recoil
```

This is worth watching, not freezing. A shared primitive would be premature until the projects demonstrate that the same law survives a third context without importing product-specific semantics.

***

## Architectural posture

The cluster should remain **federated**:

* jublEchat owns the first human cultivation/read-model specimens;
* Full Measure owns residue/re-entry behavior in its world layer;
* Upper Room owns attention weather;
* Project0 owns its bounded propagation/topology experiments;
* TranchNode owns continuity evaluation;
* GitBook owns this non-authoritative comparison and graduation memory.

The architecture is allowed to rhyme without becoming one schema.
