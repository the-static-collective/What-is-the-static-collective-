* five global verbs: `connects`, `descends-from`, `tests`, `bears-on`, `constitutes`;

> Provenance may travel. Authority stays local.

## First repository pressure result

The Bandcamp archaeology seed arrived with richer local vocabulary than the portable contract. That pressure did not justify widening the global grammar. Domain-specific source roles and interpretive claim labels remain in `extensions`, while non-test colloquial uses of `bears-on` normalize to `connects` with the source wording preserved.

The pressure did reveal one real substrate defect: a private `pointer-only` source could not preserve provenance without carrying an excerpt or resolvable pointer. The v0 contract now permits digest-only redacted testimony only when the source is `private` or `restricted` and export is `pointer-only` or `prohibited`. Public testimony still requires an excerpt or resolvable pointer.

The full private-source Bandcamp packet was not published. A reduced pressure specimen retains only the digest, lawful coordinate, graph relation, uncertainty, and explicit redaction.
