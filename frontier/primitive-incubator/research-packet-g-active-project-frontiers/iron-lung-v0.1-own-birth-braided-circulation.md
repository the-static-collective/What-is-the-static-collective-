# Iron Lung v0.1 — Own-Birth Braided Circulation

**Status:** design committed in Iron Lung / awaiting human review before implementation planning

**Canonical project authority:** `the-static-collective/iron-lung` issue #1 and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-19-iron-lung-v0.1-design.md`

## Felt possibility

Iron Lung treats transformation as boundary exchange while keeping literal software claims separate from the pneuma/breath interpretation that inspired the project.

The project asks:

> **What crossed the boundary that made this transformation possible, what remained continuous while it moved, and by what authority did the crossing occur?**

## Braided Transfer

Every transfer is always exactly three-stranded:

1. **Substance** — what crossed.
2. **Lineage** — what it remains continuous with.
3. **Authority** — what crossing or transformation was permitted and witnessed.

Missing or damaged knowledge remains explicit (`unknown`, `refused`, `strained`, `broken`) rather than removing a strand.

Working invariant:

> **Movement preserves three distinct questions: what crossed, what it remains continuous with, and by what authority it may move or change. Damage to one answer must not erase the other two.**

## Circulation and repair

A damaged braid may remain circulable without becoming admissible or assimilable.

```
transport != admission != assimilation
```

Capability-based circulation discovers admitted organs that can inspect or repair the exact damaged dimension. The vascular layer may route toward repair; it may not manufacture the repair.

Repair produces an immutable descendant braid. The damaged ancestor remains part of history.

## Repair manifold

The current braid state plus admitted capabilities yields a bounded set of lawful repair options. There may be zero, one, or many.

When multiple routes are simultaneously lawful, Iron Lung does not silently choose one. The bounded route set ascends to the **Continuity Spine** for prospective evaluation.

The Spine may rank or annotate lawful routes using origin, present state, invariants, and proposed attractor. It may not select or authorize a route.

> **Future constrains proposal; present authority selects.**

Ascending flow updates the self-model with evidence/results. Descending flow constrains becoming with proposals/preferences. The two remain distinct.

## Own-birth specimen

The first executable specimen will be Iron Lung's own birth: a three-strand birth braid that begins incomplete, circulates through admitted repair capability, exposes any lawful fork to the Spine-shaped proposal boundary, requires explicit present-authority selection, and emits a descendant braid that can become structurally assimilable.

The bootstrap itself must remain visible. The project may reference external witnesses; it may not self-certify the evidence or authority that made its birth possible.

## Two-layer boundary

The **literal layer** records only project-native or explicitly supplied facts: braid state, lineage refs, authority refs, admitted capabilities, routes, selections, repairs, witnesses, and resulting cuts.

The **pneuma layer** remains an explicit interpretive hypothesis space for breath, spirit, received possibility, and newness. It may generate questions or language. It may not impersonate physical energy, evidence, or authority.

## Non-claims

This design is not a thermodynamic simulator, theology-as-measurement, autonomous repair system, generic service mesh, cross-repository authority contract, or universal vascular schema.

Do not promote Braided Transfer or Repair Manifold into a shared Pattern from this single specimen.

## Proposed operational compression

```
inhale -> braid -> inspect -> route -> repair -> re-evaluate -> admit -> assimilate -> exhale
```

with the fork:

```
multiple lawful routes
  -> ascend Continuity Spine
  -> proposal/ranking
  -> present selection
  -> continue
```

Until the written design is reviewed and implementation lands, this page remains a **draft project-backed hypothesis**, not executable proof.
