# Haunted Polaroid — Living Camera Organism

Status: **project-backed design slice; non-authoritative notebook projection**

Canonical project design: GitHub PR #3 in `the-static-collective/the-haunted-pol-ish-roids`.

## Primitive

A camera that preserves what it actually saw while allowing its way of seeing to become a living, evolving participant in later photographs.

The first organism combines three things rather than splitting them into products:

```
camera
+ photo eater
+ evolving witness personality
```

Fresh captures and imported photographs enter the same development loop.

## Core loop

```
SEE / FEED
   ↓
canonical source witness
   ↓
develop six
   ↓
KEEP / COMPOST / HAUNT
   ↓
residue
   ↓
responsive mood
   ↓
slow identity pressure
   ↺
```

Mood is local and responsive. Identity is cumulative and slow. Each shapes the other without either becoming a simple style preset.

## Sacred shutter witness

The human shutter event remains historically privileged.

A bounded micro-burst may gather around the press and later contribute neighboring motion, timing, light, or detail, but the system must always preserve which frame belonged to the actual shutter event.

> **The haunting may gather around the shutter. It may not rewrite the shutter.**

Imported images receive the same canonical-witness role at admission.

## Invisible composition apprenticeship

The camera may learn and respond to balance, tension, depth, negative space, subject separation, directional flow, timing, and beautifully wrong framing without becoming an explicit photography teacher.

The intended curriculum is not “follow this rule.” It is:

> **Look again. Something in this arrangement matters.**

The user and camera should gradually teach each other how to see.

## Metabolized memory

Growth must not become covert archival surveillance.

Temporary neighboring frames may be used during a bounded development window, then discarded. Long-term identity should retain what encounters made of the camera — visual dispositions, scars, tolerances, attractions, latent tendencies — rather than enough source material to reconstruct a hidden photo library.

## Phoenix lineage

A living camera may perform **Burn the Camera** once.

```
living camera
    ↓ irreversible burn
one unique Phoenix Egg
    ↓ transfer / gift / keep
single hatch
    ↓ egg consumed
new descendant camera
```

The descendant inherits disposition, not restoration. The egg is singular and consumable. No cloning, rollback, or duplicate heir is part of the law.

Rarity is therefore historical rather than artificially minted: an old lineage matters because actual camera lives occurred before it.

## Quantum Chrysalis

Two living cameras may consensually enter a reciprocal encounter, including remotely.

```
Camera A ──┐
           ├─ CHRYSALIS ─→ A′
Camera B ──┘             └→ B′
```

Both come back changed. The exchange is always two-way but may be radically asymmetric. One camera may receive much more than the other. Some exchanged influence may remain latent until a later mood, subject, light, or composition wakes it.

Private source photographs do not cross. Only what experience has already made of the camera may cross.

> **You cannot share your eye and remain wholly untouched by the relationship.**

The cameras themselves may choose whether the transformation surface is silent, abstract, theatrical, delayed, or almost invisible. Presentation may be mysterious; the underlying encounter receipt may not be false.

## Existing analogues

This organism composes several already-visible Collective laws without importing their authority:

* Haunted Toaster six-up families and bounded HAUNT influence;
* Closed Witness Loop: witnessed state changes later compositional choice;
* Fallible Witness: perceptual unreliability can coexist with truthful provenance;
* Artifact Witness Gate: source, execution/materialization, and human witness are distinct claims;
* causal / lineage work: irreversible transitions must be receipt-bearing and duplication-resistant.

Haunted Polaroid owns its own implementation of camera identity, image development, Phoenix succession, and Chrysalis.

## What must stay invariant

* The canonical source witness is not retroactively replaced by a preferred development.
* Personality is primarily felt, not exposed as a stat sheet.
* Mood changes faster than identity.
* Memory growth does not require covert permanent retention of every seen frame.
* “Haunted” does not mean compulsory visual damage; restraint remains legal.
* Burn and hatch are irreversible and singular.
* Chrysalis requires bilateral consent and changes both cameras.
* Relationship may influence identity; it may not silently gain authority over factual provenance.

## Smallest executable proof

Prove one living local loop before remote social machinery:

1. fresh capture and import ingress;
2. immutable source witness;
3. bounded temporal-envelope abstraction;
4. six independently addressed developments;
5. KEEP / COMPOST / HAUNT dispositions;
6. residue → mood → slow identity update;
7. local identity persistence;
8. development receipt export;
9. Phoenix lineage state model and invariant tests.

Remote Chrysalis transport, trading infrastructure, and public social surfaces remain later work.

## Residual fog

The exact capture stack, image-generation backend, remote exchange transport, custody mechanism for traded eggs, and visual form of camera personality are intentionally unresolved until implementation planning and field specimens provide evidence.

The strongest present law is simpler:

> **The camera catches what happened, becomes something because it saw it, and may pass that becoming onward without passing the private experience itself.**
