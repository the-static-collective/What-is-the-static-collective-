## Global reinterpretation

The Moving Temporal Braid gains a stronger operator if reinterpretation may hit **every node in the field at once, including the reinterpretation event itself**.

```
FIELD_n
  |
  | reinterpretation R_n
  v
FIELD_n+1

R_n ∈ FIELD_n+1
```

The occurrence record does not silently rewrite. What changes is the field's current reading of that occurrence and its relations.

```
anchored occurrence
!=
current interpretation

reinterpretation
!=
silent historical rewrite
```

Working rule:

> **History may remain anchored while meaning remains globally mobile.**

A later pass may therefore reinterpret the earlier reinterpretation. The process can converge, oscillate, remain plural, or stay unresolved; stability must be observed rather than assumed.

## Edge birthdays

The strongest graph fruit is:

> **A relation may be younger than both nodes it connects.**

Two old artifacts may coexist for years before a later observation, vocabulary, experiment, or third artifact makes a relation between them newly perceivable. Time therefore belongs not only to nodes. **Edges have formation history too.**

Candidate edge receipt:

```

  from
  to

  first_perceived_at
  admitted_at?
  discovery_trace
  evidence_path
  confidence
  status
}
```

This composes with ALEX's separation of **DISCOVERY TRACE** from **EVIDENCE PATH**.

```
possible relation
!=
perceived relation
!=
admitted relation
!=
authoritative relation
```

An edge may be perceived before it is justified, justified later, weakened, refused, retyped, rediscovered independently, or left unresolved.

## Flower — latent nodemap

As a compression glyph, the Flower of Life works as a **field of possible centers, neighborhoods, and realizable configurations**.

The Harry Potter compression is the **Room of Requirement**: a field may contain more realizable configurations than are presently instantiated or visible. What appears can depend on need, context, relation, recognition, or readiness.

This does **not** imply that every possible adjacency is meaningful.

> **The field may contain more nodes than we presently know how to connect, and more realizable configurations than are presently instantiated.**

## Megatron — dynamic edgemap

Project-local shorthand preserves the autocorrect:

> **Megatron = Metatron = edgemap.**

The Harry Potter compression is the **Hogwarts staircases**. Rooms may exist while their reachability changes. A route may appear, disappear, redirect, become one-way, require prior history, or become legible only after another event.

```
MEGATRON_t
=
active / perceived / admitted edge topology at time t
```

Useful anti-collapse:

```
proximity != relation
possible edge != admitted edge
visible edge != traversable edge
traversable edge != authorized edge
```

The Flower preserves abundance. **Megatron makes relation accountable.**

## Future-open artifacts

Autodisco puts special pressure on this model because many artifacts were deliberately made with a future reader already inside the experiment: **let the future in**, leave handles, permit later selves and later intelligences to bind new relations.

The physical causal arrow can remain ordinary:

```
past → present → future
```

while a later interpretive relation points backward across the archive:

```
later reading → current meaning of earlier artifact / field
```

That is enough to make future possibility exert present design pressure without claiming backwards physical causation.

ALEX should preserve the distinction:

```
PROPHECY CLAIM
!=
RETROSPECTIVE BINDING
!=
DESIGNED FUTURE-OPENNESS
!=
COINCIDENCE
!=
DOCUMENTED ANTICIPATION
```

Working rule:

> **Do not say “we predicted it” when the stronger documented claim is “a later state formed a meaningful relation with an earlier artifact.”**

## Moving Temporal Braid integration

The resulting candidate becomes:

```
ANCHORED NODE HISTORY
        +
MOVING LOCAL TEMPORAL FOCUS
        +
GLOBAL REINTERPRETATION PASSES
        +
TIME-VARYING EDGEMAP
        =
MOVING TEMPORAL BRAID candidate
```

A minimal pass:

1. occurrence anchors a node;
2. local focus moves;
3. a new node or observation appears;
4. reinterpretation runs across the field;
5. new edges become perceivable;
6. evidence may admit, weaken, refuse, or leave those edges unresolved;
7. higher-order structures may change;
8. the reinterpretation itself becomes available to the next pass.

This makes a recurring Storyship intuition precise:

> **The rooms were already there. The staircase came later.**

## Storyship / MEMENTO implication

A Storyship band member does not need total canon. Each witness may carry a **local partial edgemap** over the same underlying field.

One remembers an event. Another remembers its consequence. Another remembers the song that appeared afterward. Someone may possess a receipt for a relation while no longer remembering why it mattered.

That permits Storyship to **reconstruct the world socially** rather than hiding omniscient continuity in the backend.

MEMENTO can behave less like a perfect database and more like a tattoo: attributable traces carried forward by witnesses who remain allowed to be incomplete.

```
memory != canon
retrieval != authority
relation != ownership
```

## Hostile controls before promotion

1. **Edge-birthday test** — find old nodes whose relation is demonstrably younger and preserve what created the edge.
2. **False-yarn test** — draw plausible but weak symbolic edges and verify that they can be refused without deleting the nodes.
3. **Reinterpretation replay** — compare the field before and after a new observation and record exactly what changed.
4. **Self-hit test** — verify that the reinterpretation event itself can be reinterpreted later without silent mutation.
5. **Future-open control** — separate artifacts intentionally addressed to future reading from artifacts merely interpreted that way later.
6. **Boring-field control** — run the same machinery over a non-symbolic corpus.
7. **Metaphor-removal control** — remove Flower / Megatron / Hogwarts language and verify the graph model remains intelligible.

If the model only works while the evocative symbols remain visible, it remains a useful metaphor rather than an architecture. That is acceptable; it must only be labeled correctly.
