## Current frontier

The three landed slices support a stronger restraint than a doctrine:

> **The encounter does not dictate the outcome.**

A continuity-preserving system may need to keep ancestry, transformation, residual difference, and re-entry attributable while leaving reconciliation optional.

That does **not** establish that branching is always good, that merge is undesirable, or that every domain needs the same dispositions.

## Not graduated

This crossing does not create:

* a shared `Re-entry Topology` primitive;
* a universal forkability law;
* an automatic merge or reconciliation engine;
* portable authority between projects;
* a claim that technical convergence equals human reconciliation.

`Re-entry Topology` remains an incubation name only. It should graduate only if materially different executable domains repeatedly expose the same invariant and the counterexamples remain representable without special pleading.
