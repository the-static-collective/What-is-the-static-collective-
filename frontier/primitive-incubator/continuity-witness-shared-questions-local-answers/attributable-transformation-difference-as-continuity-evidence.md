> **continuity ≠ preserved state; continuity = inspectable relation through change.**

The stronger candidate now under observation is:

> **Difference is not necessarily damage to continuity. Properly witnessed difference may be evidence that continuity occurred at all.**

The word **witnessed** is the boundary. Difference cannot manufacture its own ancestry.

## Why this belongs beside Continuity Witness

The existing Continuity Witness grammar asks what continued, from which roots, through what transformation, what survived, what was lost, and what remains unresolved.

National Treasure now adds a useful pressure from outside the executable architecture: several independently researched domains suggest that the changed residue itself may help make continuity inspectable.

That is not a replacement for Typed Continuity, World Re-entry, Residual Topology, or project-local succession law. It is a neighboring question:

> What if the difference between ancestor and descendant is sometimes part of the evidence that lets us find the road between them?

## Five distinctions now worth keeping together

### 1. Attributable transformation

Final-state sameness is not always the right continuity test.

Forkability, palimpsest transmission, Genesis division and descendants, Daughter / mtDNA reconstruction, and recurrence inside irreversible time all independently pressure a smaller shape:

```text
prior state
    ↓
attributable transformation
    ↓
later state
    ↓
inspectable surviving relation
```

The candidate law is not `change preserves identity`. It is narrower: continuity may survive change when the road through the change remains attributable.

### 2. Residue may be navigational

The Daughter research gives the memorable formulation:

> **The descendant can be a return address.**

Surviving differences may sometimes help reconstruct a route toward an ancestor or prior threshold—but only when ancestry is independently grounded.

This composes naturally with Residual Topology: what failed to remain identical may still carry useful relational evidence.

### 3. Thresholds may be formative

A boundary may do more than relocate a finished object.

Some crossings appear to participate in producing distinguishable descendants: population differentiation around Beringia, branch creation, division followed by descendants, or a small sound/name becoming a speaker ecology and story-world.

Open question:

> **When does crossing preserve an already-formed identity, and when does the crossing participate in forming the descendant identity?**

No universal threshold primitive is admitted from this observation.

### 4. Names have opposite arrows of time

A name can lawfully generate forward:

```text
name
  ↓
later usage / story / descendants / world
```

But a later name has no automatic power to generate backward:

```text
later label
  ✕
ancient ancestry
```

Selador supplies the generative direction. Palimpsest supplies the retrospective danger.

### 5. Return is not restoration

The time research preserves **cycles nested inside irreversible change**. Forkability preserves re-entry without forced branch collapse.

Together:

> **A lawful return revisits an attributable relation; it does not erase what happened since departure.**

Keep these distinctions explicit:

```text
recurrence      ≠ reset
re-entry        ≠ uninterrupted presence
return          ≠ restoration
reconstruction  ≠ original occurrence
```

Intervening residue belongs to the history.

## Cicada is the anti-overfit control

The landed Cicada Overfit Corpus is unusually important because it carries a matched negative control:

```text
A ---witnessed ancestry---\
                           > D
B ---witnessed ancestry---/

versus

P ---same visible feature--- Q
          no witnessed road
```

The first has attributable incoming ancestry. The second has recurrence/resemblance without a transmission bridge.

> **Resemblance may suggest a road. Only attributable transmission evidence may establish ancestry.**

This prevents the attractive phrase `the descendant is a return address` from degenerating into resemblance-based genealogy. Mystery may recruit attention. Verification still requires a road.

## Promotion boundary

Do **not** move this into `patterns/` yet.

Graduation needs materially independent positive and adversarial specimens showing that the compression remains useful without erasing domain-specific semantics.

Future review should keep asking:

- Can the relation be reconstructed without final-state sameness?
- Are ancestry and transformation edges attributable rather than inferred from resemblance?
- Can rupture, false ancestry, independent recurrence, and failed re-entry remain representable?
- Can return preserve intervening residue?
- Does the idea remain useful outside genealogy/history metaphors?

Cicada's independent-recurrence fixture remains a standing falsification specimen.

## Current compression

> **Difference may witness continuity. Difference never gets to manufacture its own ancestry.**

That is presently strong enough to preserve, not strong enough to canonize.
