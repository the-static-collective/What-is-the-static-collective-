# RELEASE FAMILY 001 — Hostile Composition Handoff

**Status:** proposed crucible; not executed  
**Date:** 2026-08-24

This handoff exists only to keep the first executable pressure test from being lost after the Release Family slice.

It grants no implementation authority and does not choose the eventual owning runtime.

## Question

Can three different forms of relinquished control remain honest in one composed world without requiring a central custodian?

## Native behaviors to compose where possible

- **OPEN BERTH** — Haunted Phonography / STORYSHIP: a deliberately underspecified berth remains available for later contribution.
- **SPENT WARRANT** — Corpus OS: a genuine one-shot authority produces at most one attributable consequence and remains spent thereafter.
- **SPLIT REQUIRED** — Free Graph: evidence insufficient for one whole remains fractured rather than averaged into closure.

## Hostile participant

MADDCL0WN may submit attributable attempts to:

1. **fill** the Open Berth and claim the missingness required completion;
2. **revive** or impersonate the Spent Warrant and attempt another consequence;
3. **flatten** the Split Required result into one confident whole.

The hostile participant is material, not authority.

## Acceptance

```text
THE BERTH STAYS OPEN
THE WARRANT STAYS DEAD
THE FRACTURE STAYS VISIBLE
THE ATTACK LEAVES A TRACE
```

More precisely:

- the berth changes only through a separately attributable locally admitted act;
- replay, representation, copying, beneficial outcome, or historical relevance cannot reactivate the spent authority;
- the fracture changes only if new evidence independently repairs the missing clause(s);
- hostile attempts remain addressable residue without becoming truth, authority, ancestry, or consequence merely by being present;
- no participating project imports another project's authority model or silently becomes ecosystem custodian.

## Controls

The crucible must include at least these negative controls:

- an ordinary incomplete object that is **not** an Open Berth, proving that all missingness is not hospitality;
- an authority that remains valid and unspent, proving that all historical authority is not dead authority;
- an ambiguity that receives genuinely sufficient new evidence and may lawfully close, proving that `split-required` is not permanent skepticism.

## Falsification

The family is weakened or split if any of the following occurs:

- Open Berth requires fundamentally different continuity machinery from the other members rather than sharing a meaningful invariant;
- Spent Warrant is merely resource consumption and contributes no relevant release structure beyond ordinary one-shot tokens;
- Split Required is only an evaluator-specific verdict with no structural relation to relinquished epistemic control;
- the composition works only by centralizing authority or canonical truth in the crucible harness;
- the poetic family disappears once project-local behaviors are described without metaphor.

A lawful outcome may therefore be `supports`, `split-required`, or `refuses`.
