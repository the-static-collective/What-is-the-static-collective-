## Question

> Can the existing continuity and relation grammars preserve plural ancestry when it is actually witnessed while refusing the same ancestry story when the visible pattern recurs without a transmission bridge?

The hostile shape is simple:

```
A ---witnessed ancestry---\
                           > D
B ---witnessed ancestry---/

versus

P ---same visible feature--- Q
          no witnessed road
```

The point is not that networks are truer than trees. The point is that a representation must be capable of refusing a false single-parent tree when the evidence is plural — and refusing ancestry when resemblance is all that exists.

```

repeated visible feature  -> preserve resemblance
```
