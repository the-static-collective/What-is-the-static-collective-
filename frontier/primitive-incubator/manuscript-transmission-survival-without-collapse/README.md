# Manuscript Transmission — Survival Without Collapse

**Status:** incubating cross-stack clue — not constitutional law, not manuscript authority

**Date:** 2026-08-19

**Project-backed design:** Project0 issue #51 / draft PR #52

## Candidate law

> **Survival is evidence of survival. It is not automatically evidence of origin, identity, completeness, interpretation, canon, or authority.**

The Dead Sea Scrolls / Nag Hammadi research exposes an unusually harsh continuity problem: surviving physical witnesses may be later than underlying compositions; copying intervals may be partly unknown; source and surviving languages may differ; fragments may survive without a whole; reconstruction may be useful without being occurrence; several editions or assemblies may remain lawful at once; custody and publication may improve provenance without creating authority over meaning.

The immediate architectural value is not a new manuscript ontology. It is a hostile test for continuity machinery.

```
copy != composition
manuscript date != composition date
translation != source-language witness
transcription != manuscript object
normalization != exact orthography
fragment != reconstructed whole
edition != historical occurrence
survival != canon
custody != authority
similarity != genealogy
reconstruction != historical return
```

These are anti-collapse distinctions, not historical verdicts.

## Language stays in the middle

Language is part of the transformation path, not an appendix.

A descendant text may preserve one declared dimension while transforming another:

```
meaning for purpose P: claimed preserved / transformed
lexical form: transformed
word order: transformed
script: transformed
orthography: transformed
source ambiguity: residual / unresolved
translator interpretation: introduced transformation
```

The shared system should preserve enough context to make such claims inspectable. It should not decide semantic equivalence, authorship, dependence, chronology, or theology merely because two texts resemble one another.

## Project0 specimen

Project0 PR #52 proposes manuscript transmission as an adversarial fixture family against the Typed Continuity Braid already under design in PR #50.

The first executable proof, if the written spec is approved, is intentionally only three synthetic attacks:

1. **copy != composition occurrence**;
2. **translation != source exact form**;
3. **two lawful reconstructions from the same fragments remain plural**.

Synthetic text is sufficient. The research supplies relationship shapes; Project0 does not need ancient corpus bytes, museum images, copyrighted editions, or a historical truth engine.

## Downstream doors

### formation-trace — observed vs reconstructed formation

A live composition trace may witness inserts, deletions, returns, branches, pauses, and explicit annotations. An imported historical artifact without capture evidence may support reconstruction proposals, but must never manufacture unwitnessed keystrokes, edit order, author intent, or machine events.

Potential law:

> **Observed formation history and reconstructed formation history are different evidence classes.**

### Upper Room — textual witness lens

A Scripture coordinate could eventually expose multiple attributable textual/translation witnesses without allowing the selected reading surface to become a master narrator.

Potential law:

> **The reading surface selects a witness; selection does not erase neighboring witnesses.**

This remains later work. The existing translation-neutral adapter is a door, not authorization to build a textual apparatus now.

### TranchNode — fragment relations without source mutation

Fragments can remain immutable while joins, ordering, shared-origin claims, and competing reconstructions remain relationship evidence.

Potential law:

> **A plausible join is a relationship claim, not a mutation of the fragments themselves.**

### Unfolding Ledger / creative artifacts — critical apparatus sidecar

A current usable artifact can coexist with rejected variants, unresolved readings, revision scars, and branch history outside the primary payload.

Potential law:

> **A current reading can settle action without erasing the apparatus that explains alternatives.**

### convergent-codec — exact variant masks

Parallel exact sequences may share sparse divergence positions that can be encoded once if emitted-byte accounting proves a real saving. Structural compression convergence would still prove nothing about common authorship, common source, chronology, or semantic equivalence.

## Smallest useful proof

Do not build a manuscript platform.

First ask whether the existing continuity grammar can survive three synthetic fixtures without cheating:

```
copy -> original?               REFUSE
translation -> exact source?    REFUSE
fragments -> one forced whole?  REFUSE
```

A positive result means the grammar survives a stronger hostile corpus. A failure is useful only if the repair generalizes beyond manuscript scholarship.

## Failure conditions

Do not promote this clue if doing so requires:

* a global manuscript ontology;
* automatic semantic equivalence across languages;
* a universal canon or textual-truth engine;
* similarity promoted into genealogy;
* one canonical reconstruction selected by substrate authority;
* lost or unresolved evidence deleted for a cleaner story;
* custody, discovery, publication, recurrence, or popularity promoted into authority;
* reconstruction presented as historical occurrence.

## Graduation gate

Keep this in the Primitive Incubator until:

1. the Project0 synthetic fixture catches or meaningfully pressures at least one real false-collapse;
2. any required repair remains domain-neutral;
3. language stays explicit without Project0 adjudicating meaning;
4. plural lawful reconstructions remain representable;
5. at least one materially different downstream organ independently reproduces the distinction.

Historical elegance is not enough.

## Design sentence

> **Manuscript transmission is a natural adversarial test for continuity: preserve what survived, name what transformed, keep what vanished absent, leave uncertainty unresolved, and never let the surviving witness impersonate origin, occurrence, completeness, canon, or authority.**
