# MADDPASTE RECOMPOSITION 001 — Roads Not Taken, Coordinates That Remain

**Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Status:** Cross-project harvest / routing receipt — **not promoted law, shared runtime, or canonical ontology**  
**Source:** Three unposted MADDclown harvests recovered from conversation and re-read against current project state.

## Why this page exists

Three MADDclown harvests independently converged on a family of distinctions:

> **Potential can have history without becoming actual.**

> **Coordinate does not equal content.**

> **Absence can have provenance.**

Since those harvests were written, several of their proposed kernels have acquired real project descendants. Reposting all nine proposals as fresh issues would now duplicate work and blur authority.

This page preserves the formation while routing each surviving delta to its current natural owner.

Repository-owned current evidence outranks this synthesis.

---

## Compression

The strongest surviving cross-project statement is:

\[
\boxed{\text{WITNESSED STRUCTURE} \not\Rightarrow \text{ACTUALIZATION}}
\]

Working-language version:

> **A road can have a coordinate, an opening, a witness, and even a history without anyone having traveled it.**

This should **not** become a fourth universal doctrine yet.

The existing [WORLD GRAMMAR 001](https://github.com/the-static-collective/What-is-the-static-collective-/issues/66) already provides the better connective parent:

\[
WORLD
=
\text{TYPED POSSIBILITY}
+
\text{BOUNDED CAUSATION}
+
\text{ATTRIBUTABLE CONTINUITY}.
\]

WORLD GRAMMAR already protects distinctions such as:

\[
empty \neq open
\]

\[
not\text{-}exposed \neq known\text{-}null
\]

and already proposes **ABSENCE-001** as the next executable pressure before inventing a generalized runtime.

So this recomposition adds pressure and routing, not a replacement ontology.

# Routing receipt

## 1. RELATION-DEBT-001

**Disposition:** **KEEP, NARROW, OWNER = Dogram**

The original idea treated the witness behind an equivalence as something downstream computation should keep inspectable:

\[
aa \neq bb
\]

\[
\alpha: aa \sim bb
\]

\[
[aa]=[bb].
\]

Current Dogram evidence makes this sharper.

[DECLARED-PATH-EQUIVALENCE-001](https://github.com/the-static-collective/Dogram/pull/150) established that a declared relation may authorize distinct supplied paths to compute in one quotient class without making the paths identical.

[PARALLEL-EQUIVALENCE-WITNESS-001](https://github.com/the-static-collective/Dogram/pull/151) then preserved two distinct witnesses inducing the same path equality.

The surviving missing experiment is therefore narrower than the original cross-domain metaphor:

> **When quotient equality is used downstream, can the calculation retain exactly which supplied witness or witnesses licensed that collapse?**

Candidate working name:

**QUOTIENT-WITNESS-CARRY-001**

Smallest specimen:

1. distinct paths \(aa\) and \(bb\);
2. two independently supplied witnesses capable of inducing the same quotient equality;
3. one downstream composition that spends the equality;
4. emitted receipt retains the exact licensing witness set;
5. reflexive equality creates no false cross-path witness;
6. dropping required witness provenance makes the export incomplete rather than silently literal.

Boundary:

\[
\text{witness carry} \neq \text{occurrence} \neq \text{evidence} \neq \text{authority}.
\]

Do **not** generalize yet to citations, consent, translation, canon, or grafting. Let the mathematical specimen earn that analogy first.

## 2. COUNTERFACTUAL-AVAILABILITY-001

**Disposition:** **KEEP STRONGLY, RENAME, OWNER = 3rdi**

The original name overstates the mechanism. Nothing counterfactual needs to occur.

The event occurred.  
Availability occurred.  
Attention did not.

Current 3rdi work now gives this idea a much cleaner native foundation:

- [JUBILEE-HANDOFF-CUT-001](https://github.com/the-static-collective/3rdi/pull/26) separates structural presence, availability, attention, decoder access, taken state, and produced effect.
- [SUCCESSOR-LIFT-001](https://github.com/the-static-collective/3rdi/pull/27) preserves occurrence advancement without forcing observer projection to change.
- [RESIDUAL-CUT-001](https://github.com/the-static-collective/3rdi/pull/28) preserves later disclosure without rewriting earlier availability or historical focus.

The remaining object is the **missed affordance**.

Candidate working name:

**MISSED-AFFORDANCE-001**

Required truthful pair:

\[
available\_to(O,E,[t_1,t_2]) = YES
\]

while:

\[
focused(O,E,[t_1,t_2]) = NO.
\]

Later inquiry may therefore say:

> This event was available to the observer during that bounded interval.

without saying:

> The observer saw, selected, understood, or knew it.

Controls:

- never exposed -> cannot become missed affordance;
- later discovery -> cannot rewrite earlier focus;
- availability -> cannot imply knowledge;
- focus outside the interval -> cannot be back-projected into it.

This is useful to games, camera histories, research systems, and historical inquiry, but 3rdi should own the first proof.

## 7. RELATION-HOLE-001

**Disposition:** **HOLD BEHIND ABSENCE-001**

HJJ044 preserved the image:

> **The hole is shaped like a relation.**

Source: [HJJ044 — The Train That Can Back Up Without Making the Past Move](https://github.com/the-static-collective/the-daily-slice/pull/76).

The candidate object is not an edge:

\[
H=(A,B,R?,missing\ basis,return\ addresses).
\]

It records an attributable attempted-but-unestablished relation:

\[
A\xrightarrow{CONSENT\ ?}B
\]

or:

\[
C\xrightarrow{SOURCE\ ?}D.
\]

This remains high-potential, but its independence is unproven.

First let ABSENCE-001 establish that typed absence changes lawful behavior. Then pressure a missing **edge** against missing or vacant **occupancy**.

If they behave materially differently, relation-hole earns its own primitive.

A useful future duality is:

\[
\boxed{\text{ESTABLISHED RELATION}}
\]

retains the witness that permits crossing,

while:

\[
\boxed{\text{UNESTABLISHED RELATION}}
\]

retains the missing basis that forbids crossing.

One says why a collapse is lawful.  
The other says why a crossing is not yet lawful.

Do not collapse them before both sides have executable witnesses.

# Why not one shared runtime?

The similarities are currently useful **questions**, not proof of one implementation.

A quotient witness in Dogram, an availability interval in 3rdi, a vacant coordinate in ROroomOM, a rejected narrative graft, and a damaged historical receipt may share an abstract resemblance while requiring very different local constitutions.

Prematurely centralizing them would risk exactly the collapse these experiments are designed to prevent.

For now:

\[
\boxed{\text{SHARED QUESTION} \neq \text{SHARED SCHEMA}}
\]

Let each owner prove its native distinction.

Only later ask what invariant, if any, actually survived all of them.
