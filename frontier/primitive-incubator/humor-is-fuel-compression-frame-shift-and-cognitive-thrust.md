
## What research actually supports

Peer-reviewed humor research gives substantial support to the cognitive middle of this model:

* joke comprehension is often modeled as detection of incongruity followed by revision, resolution, or **frame-shifting**;
* conceptual blending can selectively project structure from multiple mental spaces into a new blend with **emergent structure** rather than merely taking their union;
* experimental ERP work distinguishes stages of incongruity detection, resolution / mental-set breaking, and later affective appreciation;
* laughter is embodied and has been proposed as a useful object for studying global cognitive state and cognitive flexibility.

The same material can occupy different relational coordinates after the shift.

Working definition:

> **Frame travel:** a transition between representational spaces in which previously fixed relations are reinterpreted under a different coordinate system.

This is the sense in which humor may produce eCODE **thrust**.

## Humor as shortcut edge

A successful joke can compress a larger invariant into a small carrier that a person can later unpack.

For example:

```
Received ≠ admitted
Witnessed ≠ constituted
Evidence ≠ authority
Refusal remains attributable
```

can compress to:

> **lol absolutely not — but receipt it.**

If the receiver can later decompress the joke and recover the important distinctions, the joke has behaved like a **human-efficient shortcut edge** through the Free Graph.

It is not an authoritative edge. It is a traversal aid.

## Proposed house rule — invariant-preserving humor

> **If the joke decompresses back into the intended invariant, keep the joke.**

A joke may be useful documentation when it reduces traversal cost without corrupting the boundary it stands for.

But:

> **Funniness ≠ fidelity.**

A memorable compression can be wrong.

That distinction is essential. Humor may move interpretation. It does not silently move truth, admission, authority, or constitution.
