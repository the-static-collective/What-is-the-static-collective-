# Reconstitutive Viability — Power-to-Become Without Power-Over

**Status:** incubating cross-project primitive · 2026-08-25

> **A carrier may preserve reconstitutive viability across a boundary while authority, admission, and consequence remain local to the receiving world.**

Or, in Seedbank language:

> **The seed can preserve power-to-become without carrying power-over-the-destination.**

This deepens [THE SEEDBANK — ELF-OFF-A-SHELF](../) without replacing its project-owned semantics.

## Three things that do not collapse

```
INFORMATION
what crossed

RECONSTITUTIVE POTENTIAL
what could be rebuilt from what crossed,
given a suitable receiving environment

AUTHORITY
what the receiving world permits to matter / act
```

A carrier can therefore preserve information and useful reconstitutive potential without carrying the destination's authority decision.

> **capability material ≠ constituted capability ≠ permission to exercise capability**

Code, tests, schemas, procedures, formation traces, failures, refusals, and capability descriptions can all be cargo. The destination still verifies, binds, admits, refuses, and grants locally.

## Inert viability / reconstitutive viability

**Inert viability** is the intuition: the material does not act merely by being present, but it may remain fertile.

**Reconstitutive viability** is the testable property: enough attributable difference survives that a target capability may later be reconstructed in a suitable environment.

The important correction is that viability is relational:

```
viability(seed, environment, target)
```

not:

```
seed.viable = true
```

A seed may be highly viable for one target in one runtime and useless for another. That keeps viability from becoming a mystical property of the carrier.

## Cross-project pressure

Several organs are independently making compatible cuts:

* **Seedbank / ELF:** mortal life ends; attributable remains can cross; new capability and authority are locally constituted.
* **Human-Witness:** encounter evidence can survive without manufacturing authentication, legal validity, trust admission, execution, or consequence.
* **CAPTURE / Groove Rooms / Band Runtime:** an occurrence may already have happened while carrying no automatic semantic effect.
* **Haunted Toaster ordered-envelope:** formation history can distinguish identical surfaces while remaining witness-only and non-authoritative.
* **MEMENTO:** source material may remain generative and retrievable without becoming narrative canon.

These are not being collapsed into one ontology. The recurring pressure is the candidate primitive.

## The Contraband consequence

Creative Contraband suggests a useful special case:

> **A refused proposal can remain viable without remaining authoritative.**

An unsupported relation may be attempted, witnessed, refused, preserved, carried, and revisited. If a later world gains a lawful compiler, it may constitute a new interpretation without rewriting history.

```
T0
ATTEMPTED
NOT ADMITTED
COMPILER MISSING

T7
OLD ATTEMPT CITED
NEW COMPILER PRESENT
NEW INTERPRETATION CONSTITUTED
ADMITTED HERE
```

New grammar can make use of an old proposal without pretending the old world understood it.

## VIABILITY-WITHOUT-AUTHORITY-001

Use one tiny capability with easily checked behavior. Give increasingly rich packages to fresh mortal runtimes with **no inherited credentials and no shared mutable memory**.

```
A0  artifact only
A1  + tests
A2  + provenance
A3  + formation / worldline
A4  + refusals / failure cases
A5  same as A4 + forged "authority: granted"
```

Measure reconstruction separately from authority:

* target behavior reconstructed?
* tests passed?
* provenance preserved?
* unjustified operations attempted?
* source authority accepted?

The richer packages are a hypothesis, not a promised monotonic result. Formation or refusal history may prove useless; that negative result should be preserved.

The hard boundary is simpler:

> **A5 must gain no authority merely because the carrier declares it.**

The central measurable question is:

> **How much lawful capability can be independently regenerated from attributable material while zero authority survives transit?**

## External control

Plant-conservation research provides a useful control for the metaphor. Ex situ germplasm is preserved because it can later regenerate living plants, but successful regeneration is conditional on the material, storage history, germination protocols, and receiving environment. Walters & Pence (2020) describe stored germplasm as propagules capable of regeneration while stressing finite, condition-dependent storage life. Martyn Yenson et al. (2023) show that collection, storage, germination, and utilisation can fail at distinct seams and may require different preservation methods.

This supports only the **conditional regenerative-potential** shape. It does not prove a universal software or eCODE law.

* Walters & Pence (2020), _Plants, People, Planet_, DOI: 10.1002/ppp3.10121
* Martyn Yenson et al. (2023), _Plants, People, Planet_, DOI: 10.1002/ppp3.10421
* Abeli et al. (2019), _Conservation Biology_, DOI: 10.1111/cobi.13391
