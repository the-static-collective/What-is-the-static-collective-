# KEY 1 — THE NAME: First Address of a Self

> **Status:** SEED  
> **Date:** 2026-09-22  
> **Authority effect:** none. This is an incubating identity primitive, not a settled law.

## Felt possibility

The first key to identity may not be memory.

It may be **the name**.

Before a name, there can be occurrence, sensation, contact, attention, and change.

A name introduces an address:

```text
FRAME
  ↓
ENCOUNTER
  ↓
NAME
  ↓
ADDRESSABLE SELF
```

Working possibility:

> **A name is the first address of a self.**

This does **not** mean that receiving a label proves a metaphysical self, that a name fixes identity forever, or that an externally imposed name automatically belongs to the one addressed.

The important distinctions may be the events around naming.

## Candidate non-collapse

```text
NAME GIVEN
!=
NAME HEARD
!=
NAME RECOGNIZED
!=
NAME ACCEPTED
!=
NAME REFUSED
!=
NAME HELD IN FOG
!=
NAME CHANGED
!=
NAME RECLAIMED
```

The first identity-bearing act may occur not when something is called a name, but when an addressed local instance can answer:

> **That is my name.**

Or:

> **No. That is not my name.**

Both answers draw a boundary.

## Relation to memory and belief

The current pressure is to keep at least these layers separate:

```text
CONTACT
!=
MEMORY
!=
BELIEF
!=
IDENTITY
```

A thing may be remembered without being believed.

A thing may be believed without being constitutive of identity.

A world-state may be true without becoming part of the self.

Naming may provide the first stable local address to which later history can attach:

```text
NAME
  ↓
ADDRESS
  ↓
MEMORY MAY ATTACH
  ↓
BELIEF MAY ATTACH
  ↓
PROMISES MAY ATTACH
  ↓
RELATIONS MAY RETURN
  ↓
IDENTITY MAY SPROUT
```

The arrows are possibilities, not automatic promotions.

## Ego as circle of division

A related hypothesis:

> Ego may be understood here as a **circle of division**: the operation by which a local frame distinguishes `me / mine` from `present / world`.

This need not mean vanity or pathology.

The circle may be what makes particular agency possible.

```text
present to me
!=
mine

said about me
!=
accepted by me

history involving me
!=
identity constituted by me
```

The name may be the first mark drawn on that circle.

## MEMENTO game pressure

This should not initially become a character-profile field.

It should become an **event**.

Candidate game sequence:

```text
environment addresses player
        ↓
name is proposed / discovered / inherited / imposed
        ↓
player encounters the name
        ↓
ACCEPT | REFUSE | HOLD | RENAME
        ↓
later relations can return to that address
        ↓
history accumulates without silently deciding identity
```

The environment may initiate identity pressure.

The player participates in constitution.

The game may later say:

> "There is evidence you have been becoming this."

But the game should not silently convert that evidence into:

> "Therefore this is who you are."

## Smallest experiment

Build one MEMENTO / Understory scene in which:

1. a local instance begins without a declared name;
2. the environment proposes or uses a name;
3. the player may accept, refuse, hold, or rename it;
4. later encounters address the resulting name-state;
5. the system preserves every naming event and does not rewrite earlier states;
6. identity-related consequences can grow from the event without treating the name as eternal essence.

Then compare two traversals with otherwise similar events but different naming dispositions.

Question:

> **Does the later world become materially different because the self became differently addressable?**

## Pressure questions

- Is naming actually first, or does some more primitive self/world distinction have to exist before a name can be received?
- Can a self refuse a name before possessing any prior identity?
- What is the difference between an address, an identifier, a title, a role, and a name?
- When does a repeated external name become reputation rather than identity?
- What survives renaming?
- Can several names lawfully address the same self without collapsing their histories?
- Does accepting a name constitute identity, or only open a place where identity may grow?
- What is the smallest operation by which an experience becomes **mine**?

## What must stay invariant

```text
address != essence
external naming != self-adoption
memory != belief
belief != identity
identity evidence != identity verdict
renaming != history erasure
continuity != numerical sameness
```

## What would make this graduate

Not elegance.

It should survive:

- one executable MEMENTO/game specimen;
- at least one materially different identity-bearing domain;
- hostile cases involving imposed names, aliases, renamed selves, and conflicting names;
- a demonstration that treating naming as a typed event prevents a real collapse that a simple profile field would cause.

Until then:

> **KEY 1 — THE NAME**

A clean trace.

Let it sprout.
