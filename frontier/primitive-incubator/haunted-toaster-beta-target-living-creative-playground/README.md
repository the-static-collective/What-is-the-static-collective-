# Haunted Toaster Beta Target — Living Creative Playground

Status: **renderer hot path proved in packaged field testing; BETA ancestor still awaits explicit Re-listen witness before promotion**

Canonical project issue: [Haunted Toaster #148](https://github.com/the-static-collective/the-haunted-toaster/issues/148)

First child slice: [Haunted Toaster #147](https://github.com/the-static-collective/the-haunted-toaster/issues/147) — Toastmood-informed six-up + two-parent CROSS.

## Target

Haunted Toaster beta should stop feeling primarily like a configuration surface and become a **playable creative ecology**.

The governing product law is:

> **Small number of powerful, composable acts over a large lawful possibility space.**

The human should feel less like they are dialing renderer parameters and more like they are playing with visual creatures that can mutate, breed, carry history, and occasionally discover something neither side explicitly requested.

The beta target is not “more controls.” It is **more meaningful agency with less furniture**.

The 2026-08-18 packaged field run sharpened that law further:

> **Keep the Toaster's personality. Increase its expressive vocabulary.**

> **Do not make the human specify more. Give the Toaster more lawful degrees of freedom to decide differently.**

## Ordinary beta loop

```
BRING SOURCE
    ↓
six creatures appear
    ↓
recognize one, two, or none
    ↓
legal creative verbs become available
    ↓
new six-up / changed lineage / remembered consequence
    ↓
repeat until one world feels inevitable
    ↓
accept → canonical timeline → witnessed render
```

Creative play happens upstream. The accepted `VisualScore → ResolvedTimeline → preview/render → receipt` authority boundary remains intact.

## Track 0 — renderer trust gate

The repaired BETA integration line has now produced both restrained and fully responsive packaged field witnesses.

Most importantly, `Static Between Stations` completed through the formerly dangerous hot path with `split-horizon-v3`, `lattice + advect`, transient Elastic Topology Response, a bounded 48-knot contour compacted from 214 source knots, Echo Tunnel and Cathedral Fan apparitions, and a final accepted render.

Human verdict: **“goofy, very toaster.”**

That is evidence to build forward from, not a reason to normalize or simplify the renderer.

The current ancestor candidate remains PR #175. One original human gate is still pending: explicit Re-listen must prove that human anchors remain exact while machine-owned evidence may lawfully change. Do not silently promote, tag, or release the ancestor before that witness.

## Track 1 — candidate ecology

Owned by project issue #147.

The first beta move changes six-up from a menu into a population:

* no Toast Feel preselection required for the ordinary initial family;
* Toastmoods become latent pressure lanes informing diversity;
* no privileged default/reference candidate;
* exact two-parent `CROSS`;
* typed inheritance rather than whole-object averaging;
* deterministic descendants and genealogy;
* coverage before randomness;
* starting chromatic identity becomes meaningful family-distance evidence without becoming a quota or a new user preselection control.

Working criterion:

> **Six substantially different hypotheses about what this source wants to become.**

## Track 2 — Creative Verb Kernel v1

Beta should make creative transformations first-class **acts**.

Conceptually:

```
creative verb
  + subject / parents
  + locks
  + constraints
  + canonical source evidence
  + seed / policy version
        ↓
  derivation plan
        ↓
  six-up or explicit refusal
```

Every verb must remain deterministic, replayable, inspectable, lock-safe, and upstream of renderer execution.

Start with only four verbs:

### MUTATE

One-parent bounded exploration. Existing behavior becomes one member of the verb grammar rather than the whole interaction model.

### CROSS

Exactly two parents. Typed inheritance and bounded mutation. Project issue #147 owns the first implementation slice.

### MOLT

Strong transformation that preserves recognizable creature identity while shedding a bounded set of expressive layers.

The result should feel like **the same organism after changing skins**, not a random reroll.

### HAUNT

An unchosen or prior candidate may leave explicit non-authoritative residue that biases a later family through a bounded influence policy.

The ghost may influence. It does not become parent, score authority, timing authority, or renderer authority.

Do not add a larger verb catalog until these four prove the operation boundary.

## Track 3 — lawful joy / anti-boredom

### One joy slot

An ordinary family may reserve **at most one** role for a lawful-surprise candidate whose job is material semantic distance rather than median affinity or raw intensity.

This must reuse existing seeded outer-rail / coverage machinery rather than create another chaos engine.

> **One child may exist because it is delightfully elsewhere.**

A quiet mutant may be the furthest candidate. Difference is not amplitude.

### Refuse fake diversity

If a transformation cannot produce meaningfully distinct lawful descendants, do not dress six near-clones as choice.

Return an explicit refusal such as `NO_NEW_BLOOD` or equivalent. The next act may widen an authorized boundary or choose another verb.

## Track 4 — lineage and creative memory

Beta should preserve a compact, inspectable family tree:

```
source
  ↓
family 0
  ├─ A
  └─ B
       ↓ CROSS
     family 1
       ↓ MOLT
     candidate C
       ↖ HAUNT: discarded D
```

Genealogy should live in derivation/artifact evidence where possible, not inside canonical `VisualScore` merely to prove ancestry.

The lineage should answer:

* where did this candidate come from?
* what was inherited?
* what transformed?
* what merely influenced it?
* which locks/constraints shaped the branch?
* can the exact family be replayed?

### Elsewhere memory

When deliberately revisiting the same source, the Toaster may optionally consume prior family coverage as **search history** so it explores materially different lawful regions instead of pretending the prior encounter never happened.

History influences search. It does not become execution authority.

## Track 5 — grammar discovery remains frontier

A future system may notice repeated successful mutations that appear to imply a reusable creative primitive not yet named by the vocabulary.

It must never silently rewrite its own canonical grammar.

A future bounded proposal may surface something like:

```
NEW ORGANISM?
```

Human admission and an explicit vocabulary/version change would still be required before the proposed primitive becomes normal generation vocabulary.

This is intentionally beyond the first beta target.

## Explicitly deferred

Keep these out of first beta unless field evidence demands them:

* three-plus-parent breeding — likely a distinct future **COMPOST** operation;
* seasonal/environmental laws such as WINTER, DROUGHT, FEVER, or STATIC STORM;
* unlimited verb catalogs;
* self-modifying canonical grammar;
* AI taste ranking / automatic winner selection;
* engagement optimization;
* hidden renderer improvisation;
* ambient nondeterminism.

## Beta acceptance target

The umbrella becomes materially real when one packaged session proves:

1. source → diverse six-up without mood preselection;
2. two candidates → deterministic `CROSS` descendants;
3. one candidate → identity-preserving `MOLT`;
4. one discarded/prior candidate → bounded non-authoritative `HAUNT` influence;
5. one lawful joy slot → materially surprising but still constrained candidate;
6. fake diversity → explicit refusal rather than cosmetic duplication;
7. lineage → causal reconstruction of the accepted winner;
8. replay → exact regeneration of each derivation family;
9. locks / constraints → absolute throughout creative play;
10. accepted winner → unchanged canonical timeline / renderer authority boundary.

## Field criterion

The proof is not merely that more combinations exist.

> **Put a song in the room and see what species evolves around it.**

The session should be enjoyable enough that the human wants to continue playing even before deciding to render.

The latest field checkpoint adds a second criterion:

> **More ways to surprise without ceasing to be the Toaster.**
