# THE FAMILIAR — Relational Theorem

> **Status:** incubating synthesis / falsifiable architectural hypothesis. This note grants no runtime, schema, primitive, ontology, or authority.

## Compression

> **A Familiar is not an organ. It is a reproducible relational shape that appears when bounded initiative, perception, memory, embodied behavior, attributable history, and local human admission compose without sovereignty leakage.**

A shorter form:

```text
history changes the field of possibility
without becoming the authority that chooses reality
```

Or:

```text
relationship may persist
influence may deepen
recognition may emerge

while

admission remains local
```

## Why this surfaced

Haunted Toaster's current and queued faculties increasingly resemble a creature when viewed together:

- foreign matter becomes native visual material — digestion;
- APERTURE / SPEAK / GRAB / GROW / BODY — behavioral anatomy;
- Listener anchors and bounded re-listening — situated perception with explicit human authority boundaries;
- receipts, residue, lineage, Exact Return, HAUNT and EMS-001 — attributable history and relation-shaped re-entry;
- Candidate Ecology / six-up — bounded generative initiative rather than direct execution.

None of these individually proves a Familiar. The hypothesis is that **their lawful composition may produce familiar-shaped behavior without adding a central Familiar runtime.**

## The existing laws already surrounding it

THE FAMILIAR should not invent new constitutional machinery where existing eCODE laws already constrain the space.

### Ghost / Residual Presence — memory without authority

Portable law:

```text
history may remain influential
without becoming sovereign
```

Therefore:

```text
memory != warrant
influence != admission
re-entry != original occurrence
```

A Familiar's memory should be attributable aftermath, not a hidden preference database.

### Awareness Organ — the eye has no hands

Awareness can sense, compare, notice drift, preserve fog, and redirect attention without acquiring project write authority.

Therefore a Familiar may become increasingly perceptive without perception itself becoming permission to act.

### Stigmergic Field — history changes affordances, not sovereign choice

Attributable traces may alter an environmental field that affects attention, recruitment, inhibition, return, or tension.

The field may shape what becomes salient without becoming a scheduler or hidden chooser.

This suggests the most interesting Familiar memory is not:

```text
user liked X -> choose more X
```

but something closer to:

```text
this encounter occurred
-> the lawful possibility landscape changed
-> later proposals can differ for attributable reasons
```

### Attributable Becoming — identity, participation, history, authority remain distinct

A Familiar-shaped continuity must preserve:

```text
identity != participation != trajectory != authority
```

A relationship can therefore become richer through witnessed history without the persistence of that history manufacturing additional warrant.

### Crossing Discipline — no silent promotion

Nothing becomes true, permitted, constituted, accepted, or authoritative merely because it crossed a boundary or remained remembered.

For a Familiar this implies:

```text
remembered != accepted
recognized != admitted
proposed != enacted
returned != resurrected
```

### Exact Return / AGAIN — continuity without counterfeit presence

The relationship should survive interruption by reconstructing attributable continuity, not by pretending a continuously present machine-self survived unchanged.

This distinction becomes especially important across:

- shutdown;
- migration;
- renderer / model replacement;
- extinct generative ancestors;
- cross-appliance return;
- reconstructed worlds.

A Familiar may be **topologically continuous without being materially identical.**

## Candidate theorem

**Inference, not doctrine:**

> If a creative system can deterministically preserve attributable encounter-history as bounded influence, allow that history to alter later proposal affordances, cleanly remove the influence to recover the prior baseline, and maintain an uncrossable local admission boundary, then the resulting relationship may constitute a reproducible Familiar-shaped behavior even though no sovereign Familiar object exists.

This is stronger than personalization and weaker than autonomous agency.

## Relational hysteresis

A useful technical compression is **receipt-addressed relational hysteresis**.

Ordinary hidden hysteresis says the present state depends on path history.

The eCODE version should require more:

```text
present proposal
= function(
    source,
    grammar,
    local state,
    attributable relationship history
  )
```

where the relevant history is:

- addressable;
- attributable;
- deterministic;
- replayable;
- removable;
- bounded by recipient-local law.

The crucial test is that removal of the relationship receipt restores the prior baseline exactly.

## The local killing specimen

The first proper test belongs in Haunted Toaster rather than in this cross-project note:

**FAMILIAR-001 — Same Source, Changed Relationship**

Freeze exact source bytes, repository SHA, grammar / configuration, seed, local policy, and starting history.

Then:

```text
A  = baseline with no treatment relationship receipt
R  = one attributable human encounter receipt
B  = exact frozen rerun with R enabled as influence-only
A' = exact frozen rerun after R is removed
```

Required:

```text
A' = A exactly
B != A for an attributable relationship reason
```

### Necessary hostile controls

1. **Wrong relationship identity** — same memory-shaped payload with invalid provenance must not reproduce B.
2. **Artifact-only control** — same artifact bytes without the witnessed relation must not inherit relational influence.
3. **Missing relation** — unresolved relationship evidence remains unresolved.
4. **Authority smuggling** — memory cannot move human anchors, Listener truth, execute, or self-admit.
5. **Randomness control** — identical treatment replays must be exact.
6. **Refusal integrity** — refusal may leave residue but cannot silently become delayed acceptance.
7. **Meaning control** — B must differ in mechanically interpretable relationship-shaped behavior, not merely arbitrary output inequality.

If the same memory payload works under the wrong relationship identity, the mechanism is better described as a preset than a Familiar.

## Topological role, not persistent machine-self

A particularly strong consequence follows from Exact Return and cross-project sovereignty:

> **The Familiar may be a topological role rather than one continuously persistent computational object.**

If source, body, grammar, receipts, provenance, and local admission rules can reconstruct the same lawful relationship elsewhere, then continuity need not depend on preserving one hidden machine identity forever.

This matters for model death and migration:

```text
old engine dies
relationship evidence survives
new lawful body reconstitutes the relation
history influences again
without claiming the dead engine never left
```

This makes Familiar-shaped continuity compatible with Haunted Phonography's extinct-ancestor direction and the wider eCODE law that return is weaker than resurrection.

## What would falsify the idea

THE FAMILIAR should be killed, renamed, or sharply reduced if observed behavior is equivalent to any of the following:

- ordinary preference personalization;
- engagement optimization;
- hidden mutable model / renderer state;
- random variation described after the fact as recognition;
- persistence that cannot be cleanly excised;
- acceptance bias toward prior human choices;
- cross-session influence whose provenance cannot be reconstructed;
- memory that quietly gains execution or admission authority;
- a central coordinator required to maintain the whole relationship.

## What not to build

Do not create merely because this theorem is aesthetically compelling:

- a global `Familiar` object;
- a universal Familiar schema;
- a shared sovereign memory database;
- a daemon that owns relationship state across projects;
- a single score representing closeness / affection / trust;
- an automatic admission policy;
- an anthropomorphic life claim.

The theorem is stronger if familiar-shaped behavior emerges from independently owned faculties that can be tested separately.

## Research neighbors

Relevant external research gives useful analogies but does not prove this architecture:

- mixed-initiative human-AI systems support distributing initiative rather than framing systems as either passive tools or autonomous replacements;
- human-AI collaboration research commonly separates agency, interaction, and adaptation;
- AI-assisted design research argues for cooperation that supports human designers rather than replacing their judgment;
- distributed / stigmergic creative systems show that coherent creative behavior can emerge through local interaction and shared traces without one central creative controller.

These literatures support the plausibility of the middle territory. They do **not** establish eCODE's stronger claim that initiative, memory, and recognition can expand while constitutive admission sovereignty remains explicitly local.

That remains a falsifiable architectural question.
