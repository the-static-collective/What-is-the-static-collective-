# Death Is Local — Extinction Requires a World Claim

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Status: incubating structural primitive, not a metaphysical or biological claim about human death.** The purpose is to type cessation, continuity, residue, and extinction more carefully across eCODE worlds.
{% endhint %}

## Core distinction

Let

`DEAD_B(x, t, criterion, evidence)`

mean that `x` no longer satisfies a declared criterion for life, agency, participation, identity, reachability, or authority **inside boundary `B`** at time `t`, with named evidence.

That does **not** entail:

`for every B': DEAD_B'(x, t)`

Candidate compression:

> **DEATH IS LOCAL; EXTINCTION REQUIRES A WORLD CLAIM.**

The point is not to deny cessation. It is to forbid an untyped local observation from silently promoting itself into a universal extinction claim.

## Four candidate laws

1. **Death requires a boundary.** Name what ceased, where, by what criterion, and on what evidence.
2. **Local death cannot silently promote itself to global extinction.** `unreachable here` is not equivalent to `gone everywhere`.
3. **Continuity needs a carrier, not immortality.** A surviving population, replica, descendant, receipt, causal mark, topology, manuscript, genet, or other attributable continuation must bear the claim.
4. **Death may leave consequence without authority.** Residue can constrain future state without becoming a currently authorized actor.

A useful eCODE state is therefore possible:

`present(x) = 0`

`consequence(x) > 0`

`authority(x) = 0`

This is the seam already exposed by [Ghost / Residual Presence — History Without Authority](../../../patterns/ghost-residual-presence-history-without-authority.md), [The Room Can Vanish; the World Remains](../../../patterns/world-reentry-memory.md), [Continuity Witness — Shared Questions, Local Answers](../continuity-witness-shared-questions-local-answers/), and [Crossing Discipline — No Unaccounted Consequence](../crossing-discipline-no-unaccounted-consequence.md).

| CRDT causal deletion              | item is absent                                       | causal deletion metadata persists                         | residue blocks false resurrection                                                                       |

The quantum lane is unusually valuable because it adds a stricter requirement than the looser continuity analogy: **if something is claimed to survive local disappearance, ask where the carrier is.** See the child page Quantum No-Hiding — Forced Carrier, Local Twist, X.

## Candidate grammar

```
DEATH(
  subject,
  boundary,
  criterion,
  witness_time,
  evidence
) -> {
  cessation,
  receipt,
  residue,
  continuators[],
  extinction_scope,
  reentry_possible?,
  authority_after
}
```

Default discipline:

* `cessation` may be locally true.
* `global_extinction` is **not inferred** from local cessation.
* `continuators[]` must be attributable, not merely similar.
* `residue` may constrain future interpretation or state.
* `authority_after = none` unless separately constituted.

## Falsifiable specimens

### False Global Death / Re-entry

World A declares participant `X` dead or unreachable. World B retains an independently attributable continuation of `X`, or later produces lawful re-entry evidence.

The system must:

* preserve A's local death receipt;
* refuse to upgrade that receipt into global extinction;
* allow later continuity evidence to be evaluated;
* refuse to resurrect expired local authority merely because continuity exists elsewhere.

### Causal Tombstone

Replica A removes `X`. A stale replica later presents an old live state.

The system must preserve enough causal residue to prevent silent resurrection while keeping `present(X)=0`.

### Carrier Challenge

Whenever a continuity claim is made after disappearance, require:

`WHERE_IS_X_CARRIED?`

Answers may include a descendant, replica, causal record, transformed state, reconstructive address, or other explicitly typed carrier. `somehow in the relationship` is not automatically sufficient.
