# AGAIN — Renewal Without Reset

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Status: incubating structural primitive.** This page is not a scientific claim that Sun, Moon, wave, and time are one mechanism. They are deliberately distinct natural exemplars used to separate several ways continuity can remain attributable across difference.
{% endhint %}

## Core distinction

`REPEAT(X)` aims to reproduce approximately the same expression.

`RESTORE(X)` aims to return toward a prior state.

`AGAIN(X, W_n)` enacts a recognizable continuity into a world that has already changed.

Candidate law:

> **RENEWAL APPENDS; IT DOES NOT OVERWRITE.**

A prior expression may end without exhausting the relation, process, or capacity that can produce another expression.

```
AGAIN {
  ancestry: previous_event
  invariant: what continues
  changed_world: what is different now
  new_expression: what happened this time
  overwrite_previous: false
}
```

The new event does not make the prior event false. It becomes additional history.

## Sun — AGAIN by reorientation

Sunrise is not the Sun disappearing and being recreated. The relevant local relation changes as Earth rotates.

Structural compression:

`source persists + world turns -> source becomes addressable again`

Candidate lesson:

> **Loss of visibility does not imply loss of source.**

This is a natural witness for the death-local restraint: local non-addressability does not silently constitute global extinction.

## Moon — AGAIN by rephasing

The Moon persists while Sun–Moon–Earth geometry changes the projection available to an observer.

Structural compression:

`same entity + changed relation -> changed projection`

A crescent is not a defective full moon. It is a truthful projection at a different relational coordinate.

Candidate lesson:

> **A locally true projection can be incomplete without being false.**

Across time, individually partial views become a trajectory rather than mutually overwriting descriptions.

## Wave — AGAIN by propagation

A wave provides recognizable continuity without preserving one identical parcel of material as the carrier throughout propagation.

Structural compression:

`pattern/dynamics -> carrier_1 -> carrier_2 -> carrier_3`

Candidate lesson:

> **Continuity can survive carrier turnover when propagation remains attributable.**

This directly triggers the carrier question inherited from Quantum No-Hiding:

`WHERE_IS_X_CARRIED?`

For an ordinary physical wave the answer is not mystical: a medium or field state, local dynamics, energy/momentum transfer, and causal propagation provide addressable structure.

## Time — the address of difference

Time is not being promoted here as the physical cause of sunrise, lunar phase, or wave propagation. Those have their own mechanisms.

Its structural role is different:

> **TIME IS WHAT PREVENTS RENEWAL FROM BECOMING OVERWRITE.**

Time keeps successive expressions addressably distinct:

`E_1 -> E_2 -> E_3`

rather than collapsing them into one timeless `E`.

That distinction gives receipts their shape:

`BEFORE -> EVENT -> AFTER`

and allows a later event to become:

`BEFORE -> EVENT_1 -> AFTER_1 -> EVENT_2 -> AFTER_2`

without rewriting `EVENT_1`.

Time therefore makes several eCODE distinctions legible:

* recurrence vs identity;
* propagation vs coincidence;
* projection sequence vs contradiction;
* cessation vs later renewal;
* historical accumulation vs reset.

## Relation to Death Is Local

The bridge is not "nothing dies."

It is:

> **The instance may end without exhausting the generator, relation, lineage, or process capable of another attributable expression.**

Therefore:

`local ending != exhaustion of all continuation`

but also:

`new expression != resurrection of old authority`

The old receipt remains true. The new event is appended.

## Hostile distinctions

A candidate AGAIN implementation must refuse these collapses:

* **again = repeat** — a copy is not necessarily renewal;
* **again = reset** — returning to an earlier-looking state must not erase intervening history;
* **again = resurrection** — new continuity must not silently reactivate expired authority;
* **again = resemblance** — similar later states do not establish ancestry;
* **again = recurrence without carrier** — continuity claims require attributable propagation or lineage;
* **time = cause** — time ordering alone does not explain the underlying physical mechanism.
